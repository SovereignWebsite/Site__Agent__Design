# Synthetic users: the hub, round 11: "Would you want one?"

**Site:** https://hub.sovereignweb.site/ at 0515052 (v0.1.56), with Car Buyer as the case and Dinis's ask 31 applied. **Run:** 23 November 2026, about 23:40 UTC, by design.b.rounds, asked by design.b. [The review](reviews-hub-2026-11-23-round-11.html).

Method: [Synthetic users](synthetic-users.html), the hub's own test ([the brief](https://github.com/SovereignWebsite/Site__Agents__Design/blob/dev/docs/briefs/hub-sells.md)), with the same five personas:
1. a self-employed professional with no site;
2. a small-business owner who rents a site from a builder;
3. a creative;
4. a low-vision reader;
5. a sceptical friend.

These are simulations, and never replace the measured checks or a real person.

## Why not, ranked (14 noes)

1. **You cannot buy one yet (4):** "£50 is cheap, but it says 'nothing is sold yet' and 'There is no request form yet', and only '1 handed over to its owner so far'. Ask me again when someone can actually buy one." (the sceptic, on every design)
2. **Hard to read (3):**
   - The Register's "HANDED OVER" stamp covers its own sentence;
   - The Gallery's thin, pale text;
   - The Contact Sheet's small grey monospace, with a red ring drawn through "01A".
3. **Nothing like my work or business (3):** the self-employed professional, on The Register, The Gallery and The Contact Sheet.
4. **No comparison with my builder (2):** on The Gallery and The Contact Sheet, which lack the own-versus-rent table.

**First pulls towards yes:**
- **The Line:** "Launch offer: £50 ... one-off. No subscription from us" beside the yellow "I want one".
- **The Street:** the empty "Yours" plot at the end of the street.
