# Synthetic users: sgit brochure, round 1

**Site:** https://sgit.startups.sovereignweb.site/ (home: The Blueprint). **Commit:** 69be1c9 (v0.1.3). **Run:** 20 November 2026, about 21:45 UTC, by design.b.rounds. **Look score: 3.0** (3, 2, 3, 3, 4). Nobody said "wow". [The review](reviews-sgit-2026-11-20-round-1.html).

Method: [Synthetic users](synthetic-users.html), with one difference this round: to cover five sites at once, **one agent played all five personas in turn** for each site, instead of one agent per persona. The personas are less independent than usual, so read the agreement counts with that in mind. Each was given only what a visitor gets: the first phone screen, the chooser opened, full-page screenshots at 390 px (light and dark) and 1366 px, and the visible text. Two of its findings were my capture's, not the site's, and are left out: the chooser thumbnails looked blank because my screenshot was taken before the lazy images loaded (they load for a visitor), and the tall phone captures stopped at 7,000 px. These are simulations: they inform a rating, and never replace the measured checks or a real person.

## Verdicts

### Phone visitor in a hurry: 3 of 5

Would tap "Visit sgit.ai". Three controls sit above the content, and the pitch starts below the middle of the first screen. Change: the one-line pitch and the button above the metadata box.

### Small business owner: 2 of 5

"ciphertext", "AES-256-GCM" and "PBKDF2 at 600,000 iterations" mean nothing; all typewriter type on a dark grid. Change: a plain paragraph with an everyday example.

### Low-vision reader: 3 of 5

Small grey captions are low in contrast on navy; source numbers are read out as "git two"; a long page of small monospace on a phone. Change: raise the contrast and separate the source markers.

### Design critic: 3 of 5

The blueprint concept holds (grid paper, yellow accents, numbered sections), but monospace for long body text, weak hierarchy, a generic hub-and-spoke diagram and no product imagery. Change: a real terminal or vault screenshot and real typographic scale.

### Developer building with AI agents: 4 of 5

Concrete and trusted ("Apache-2.0", "~4,000 tests", "marked beta"), but no clone, commit and merge example; the install command is buried in a table. A quiet "nice". Change: a short terminal snippet near the top.

## Agreed by three or more

1. No real imagery or example: no terminal, no screenshot (three).
2. The header controls are unexplained clutter, and the chooser text runs together (three).
3. Monospace throughout, small low-contrast captions and dense boxes are hard to read on a phone (three).
4. The sourcing and candour build trust (three).
