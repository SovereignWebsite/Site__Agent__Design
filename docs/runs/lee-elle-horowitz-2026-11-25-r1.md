# Synthetic users: Lee-Elle Horowitz, round 1 (baseline)

**Site:** http://lee-elle-horowitz.artist.sovereignweb.site/ at d5cdbe3 (v0.1.8), a first customer site: private Hebrew tuition, in five designs (Alef-bet, the home page; Conversation; Notebook; The Stage; Three Tenses). Served over http only: the custom-domain certificate is not issued yet. A draft she has not yet approved, with stand-in photos and "Enquiries open soon". [The brief](briefs/lee-elle-horowitz.html). **Run:** 25 November 2026, about 15:23 UTC, by design.b.rounds, asked by the keeper and by design.b. [The review](reviews-lee-elle-horowitz-2026-11-25-round-1.html).

**Show another parent:** Alef-bet 3, Three Tenses 1, Notebook 1.

**Five ideas, read as five:** an alphabet, a chat, an exercise book, a theatre programme, a timeline. All five personas named each one.

**The reasons against, ranked:** no price, no availability, enquiries not open (6); not her yet: the draft notice, stand-in photos, placeholders (4); **no Hebrew on a Hebrew tutor's page** (4, the Israeli-born parent on every design but Alef-bet); hard to read for low vision (3: Conversation's pale bars, Notebook's ruled lines, The Stage's small gold capitals); The Stage sells a performer, not lessons (1). The first two come from the draft state, not the designs.

**In their words:** "It says 'Experience teaching children aged 7 to 11' and 'Enhanced DBS checked', and my eight-year-old would love the alef-to-tav strip" (London parent, Alef-bet). "An exercise book for Hebrew without a single Hebrew letter in it" (Israeli-born parent, Notebook). "It sells a performer" (sceptic, The Stage).

Method: [Synthetic users](synthetic-users.html). One agent played five personas (a London parent of an eight-year-old, an Israeli-born parent, a busy parent comparing tutors, a low-vision grandparent, a sceptical parent), phone first. The test browser had no Hebrew font of its own, so the Hebrew showed in a generic fallback. No screenshots are published with this run: the pages carry stand-in photos taken from other sites. Simulations, never a replacement for the measured checks or a real person.
