Method: [Synthetic users](synthetic-users.html). One agent per site played all five personas in turn, with the chooser captured with its thumbnails loaded.

**A fault in this run's set-up.** The five agents ran at once and two of them first wrote their crops into one shared folder. Each noticed another site's images there, discarded those crops and redid its own in a private folder; their verdicts are about their own site only. The next run gives each agent its own folder from the start.

These are simulations: they inform a rating, and never replace the measured checks or a real person.

## Agreed across the five sites

1. **The source numbers in the body text** clutter reading (all five sites).
2. **"Choose a design" and "⋮" in the header** read as tools, not content (four sites).
3. **The calls to action are clear** (all five).
4. **"Nothing is sold yet" and "first proposal"** are honest but leave a buyer nothing to do (RiskMandate, AgentiCCells, Sovereign Website).
5. **Shrunk or cropped screenshots** still read as broken on phones (sgit, The Cyber Boardroom).
