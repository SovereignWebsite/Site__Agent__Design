# Synthetic users: Donna Freed, round 1

**Site:** https://donna-freed.author.sovereignweb.site/ at 3bee275 (v0.1.3). **Run:** 23 November 2026, about 13:40 UTC, by design.b.rounds. **Look score: 2.4** (2, 3, 3, 2, 2). **Best design votes:** 3 (Studio) twice, 5 (Newsroom) twice, 2 (Memoir) once. All five named design 4 (Translator) the weakest. Nobody said "wow". [The review](reviews-donna-freed-2026-11-23-round-1.html).

Method: [Synthetic users](synthetic-users.html). One agent played five personas, in its own folder:
- a phone visitor;
- a reader of her memoir or a listener of her podcast;
- a low-vision reader;
- a design critic;
- a literary agent, festival programmer or journalist.

The inner pages were captured in design 1 only. These are simulations, and never replace a real person.

| Persona | Look | Best | The one change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phone visitor | 2 | 3, Studio | "put the name and one picture first, and fold the design pills into one small control" |
| Reader or listener | 3 | 2, Memoir | "add the Duplicity cover, a two-line description of the book, and a 'Buy / Listen' row" |
| Low-vision reader | 3 | 3, Studio | "raise the source and footnote text to at least 15px, and use darker grey" |
| Design critic | 2 | 5, Newsroom | "give each design a different idea of the layout ... not only a different palette" |
| Agent or programmer | 2 | 5, Newsroom | "a contact or 'for press and events' line, even if it is only a link to the publisher" |

## Agreed by three or more

1. **No image at all** (all five): no photograph and no book cover. The site says why, rightly: "No photograph, no contact details and nothing about her family or private life."
2. **Design 4 is the weakest** (all five): empty hairline bands and thin light type.
3. **The sourcing is admirable** (four): "Everything here is a claim from a public source, named below."
4. **The machinery comes first** (three): the design pills and the draft banner push her name about 400 px down on a phone.
5. **Thin content and no next action** (three): Writing is empty, the cards say "Links to follow when she chooses them", and there is no way to buy, listen or get in touch.
