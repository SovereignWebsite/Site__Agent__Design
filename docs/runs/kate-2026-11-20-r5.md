# Synthetic users: Kate Curtis-Evans, round 5

**Site:** https://kate-curtis-evans.founder.sovereignweb.site/ (home: The Workflow Board). **Commit:** dcf8a5f. **Run:** 20 November 2026, about 15:25 UTC, by design.b. **Look score: 3.8** (4, 3, 4, 4, 4). design.b signed off at 4 on this run; Dinis Cruz then judged the site bland, and the sign-off was withdrawn ([round 6](reviews-kate-curtis-evans-2026-11-20-round-6.html)).

Each persona is a separate agent given only what a visitor gets: the first phone screen (390 x 844), full-page screenshots at 390 and 1366 px in light and dark, the design chooser opened (from round 7 on), and the page's visible text. It is not given the guidelines or design.b's findings. Method: [Synthetic users](synthetic-users.html). These are simulations: they inform a rating, and never replace the measured checks or a real person.

## Verdicts

### Phone visitor in a hurry: 4 of 5

Would tap "The open job: technical co-founder". The board idea is "clever but a bit odd on a phone, where it is one very long scroll"; the top bars are "confusing noise"; "Unassigned" unclear at first.

### Small business owner: 3 of 5

"Clean and professional, but a bit plain." The top menu is crowded; the board slightly gimmicky; very long on a phone. Wanted a bigger, warmer photo near the top.

### Low-vision reader: 4 of 5

Buttons big and clear. The four-column board is cramped on desktop; grey captions, tiny status pills and the faint footer are hard to read; "Unassigned" is odd jargon.

### Design critic: 4 of 5

Calm serif and sans pairing, "it feels personal". Tiny grey captions and the cramped top strip; "50testers" has a missing space. Raise the board's small text.

### Technical co-founder candidate: 4 of 5

Concrete numbers and honest limits build trust; the platform is vibe-coded and no stack is named. Her CV among project cards is odd. Wanted a short "what the build is and what's at stake" panel.

## Agreed by three or more

1. "Unassigned" reads as jargon (four of five). 2. The bars above the content puzzle. 3. Small pill and caption text. 4. The phone page is long. Lesson: all five could say what the site is and what to do next, and four gave it a 4, yet Dinis found it bland. The look question now asks "would you show this to a friend, and did anything make you say wow?"
