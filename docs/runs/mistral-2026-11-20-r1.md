# Synthetic users: Mistral provider site, round 1

**Site:** https://mistral.provider.sovereignweb.site at 983035b (v0.1.1). The home page is design 5, the hackathon poster; designs 1 to 4 and /demos/ were also shown. **Run:** 20 November 2026, about 23:40 UTC, by design.b.rounds. **Look score: 3.0** (3, 4, 3, 3, 2). **Best design votes:** 5, 3, 1, 2, 4 (no favourite). [The review](reviews-mistral-2026-11-20-round-1.html).

Method: [Synthetic users](synthetic-users.html). One agent played all five personas in turn, working in its own folder. The personas were:
- a phone visitor from the hackathon chat;
- a hackathon developer;
- a low-vision reader;
- a design critic;
- a small business owner.
These are simulations, and never replace the measured checks or a real person.

| Persona | Look | Best | Weakest | The one change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Phone visitor | 3 | 5 | 4 | "put the venue, time and organiser link in the hero" |
| Hackathon developer | 4 | 3 | 1 | "add copy buttons and confirm the key signup" |
| Low-vision reader | 3 | 1 | 2 | "put the sources in footnotes" |
| Design critic | 3 | 2 | 3 | "give each design its own content structure, not just new colours" |
| Small business owner | 2 | 4 | 3 | "a plain-language 'why this matters to your business' line on the home page" |

## Agreed by three or more

1. The inline "(Source: ... as read on 20 November 2026.)" tags clutter every page.
2. What each persona needed most was missing or buried: the venue and time, a confirmed way to get a key, and what "sovereign" means.
3. Designs 1, 3 and 4 share most of their chrome and words; /demos/ looks like another site.
4. The candour builds trust: "Nothing has been agreed with Mistral", "An estimate, not measured".
