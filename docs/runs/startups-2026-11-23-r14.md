## What this round's items did

- **sgit** rose from 3.0 to 3.8, with four 4s for the first time. Four personas said the cut hero works: the headline, one quote and both buttons fit the first phone screen. The captioned, tinted frame makes the capture "read as genuine evidence". But it shows a page of pentest prose, not the product, and its small text does not survive at phone width (three personas).
- **The Cyber Boardroom** rose from 2.8 to 3.4. Four personas called the conversation hero the clearest way to grasp the product. Three said it ends on a question with no answer, and that the black "What else a director can ask" band is the weak joint: dim grey text, and a third colour world.
- **AgentiCCells:** four 4s again (3.8). "A website like this" on the first phone screen "helps clearly". The cards one to a row are readable, but add length and lose the sense of a team.
- **RiskMandate:** 3.6, three 4s. Hatching only the delta "makes the gap visible", and the one-to-a-row cards "read cleanly". The numbers band is too long, with a grey "as on riskmandate.ai, read ..." caption on every figure (three personas).
- **Sovereign Website** fell from 3.4 to 3.0, all five at 3. The column of captures puts proof on the first screen. But each capture is the top slice of a site, mostly its menu bar, so faces and names are cropped out (three personas). The owners' quote lines, Kate's "first owner to edit her own site herself" above all, went with the text-only repeat (three personas).

Nobody said "wow".
