# Synthetic users: RiskMandate brochure, round 1

**Site:** https://risk-mandate.startups.sovereignweb.site/ (home: The Brochure). **Commit:** 125112b (v0.1.3). **Run:** 20 November 2026, about 21:45 UTC, by design.b.rounds. **Look score: 3.0** (3, 3, 3, 3, 3). Nobody said "wow". [The review](reviews-risk-mandate-2026-11-20-round-1.html).

Method: [Synthetic users](synthetic-users.html), with one difference this round: to cover five sites at once, **one agent played all five personas in turn** for each site, instead of one agent per persona. The personas are less independent than usual, so read the agreement counts with that in mind. Each was given only what a visitor gets: the first phone screen, the chooser opened, full-page screenshots at 390 px (light and dark) and 1366 px, and the visible text. Two of its findings were my capture's, not the site's, and are left out: the chooser thumbnails looked blank because my screenshot was taken before the lazy images loaded (they load for a visitor), and the tall phone captures stopped at 7,000 px. These are simulations: they inform a rating, and never replace the measured checks or a real person.

## Verdicts

### Phone visitor in a hurry: 3 of 5

Four layers of controls before the headline; the hero screenshot is too small to read. Change: a one-line header.

### Small business owner: 3 of 5

The candour reassures ("No insurer has agreed to anything on this page"); "mandate", "grant", "delta", "barrier" and "SHarness" do not. Change: one plain example near the top.

### Low-vision reader: 3 of 5

Superscripts read as "side two three"; small grey captions; screenshot text unreadable at any zoom; a 190 px header that fills the screen at 200 per cent. Change: spacing in the chooser and readable source markers.

### Design critic: 3 of 5

Disciplined (one green accent, honest captions, a timeline to scale), but a pasted hero screenshot, a basic hub diagram repeated by cards, stock stat components, the same rhythm throughout. Change: one strong visual of mandate, grant, delta and barrier.

### Head of security: 3 of 5

Would open the free ABP library, but every button says "Visit riskmandate.ai". The calm tone clashes with the screenshot's "We can control your agents.". Change: a direct link to a sample ABP, shown on the page.

## Agreed by three or more

1. The phone header pushes the content down (four).
2. The hero screenshot is too small and adds little (four).
3. The only action is to leave, with no route to a sample (three).
4. The honest sourcing builds trust (three).
5. The terms are unexplained at first contact (three).
6. Tidy, but nobody said "wow" (five).
