# Synthetic users: Moving Van, round 1 (baseline)

**Site:** https://moving-van.business.template.sovereignweb.site/ at a85484d (v0.1.4), a template for a small family man-and-van firm, in five designs (The van, the home page; Moving day; The boxes; The inventory; The card), every real detail a [placeholder] until an owner fills it in. [The brief](briefs/moving-van.html). **Run:** 27 November 2026, about 16:04 UTC, by design.b.rounds, asked by the keeper, b.site.moving-van. [The review](reviews-moving-van-2026-11-27-round-1.html).

**Five ideas, read as five:** a picture of what fits in the van, a who-does-what timeline, a playful reveal, a room checklist, a printable flyer.

**Every placeholder read as one:** bracketed, tinted and outlined. Two notes: "[more trips]" is clearly a placeholder but unclear in what the owner should write; and The boxes hides "Prices from [£ from]" inside a closed box.

**The reasons against, ranked:** The boxes hides prices and services behind "Open the box", a small link on brown card (5 of 5); small text for a low-vision reader (the boxes' stamps, the van's painted "[Phone number]"); The inventory is built for rooms, so a few pieces of furniture have nowhere to go (1); The card is too thin to compare firms (1). The phone number sits at the very bottom of three designs.

**In their words:** "The van with my name and number on it is the strongest single picture of the five" (the firm owner). "'For a quote, call or email:' with a big phone number is exactly what I want" (the older mover, The card). "To compare firms I would have to open six boxes one by one" (the family, The boxes).

Method: [Synthetic users](synthetic-users.html), phone first, from captures of the five designs, light and dark, phone and desktop. The date box showed mm/dd/yyyy because the test browser was set to US English. Simulations, never a replacement for the measured checks or a real person.
