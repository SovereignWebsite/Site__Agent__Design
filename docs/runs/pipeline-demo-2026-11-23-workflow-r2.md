**Page:** https://pipeline-demo.demo.sovereignweb.site/workflow/ at a945a2f (v0.1.6). **Run:** 23 November 2026, about 16:12 UTC, by design.b.rounds. **Would you want one? 2 of 5** (round 1: 2 of 5). Look 3.4 (4, 3, 3, 4, 3). Nobody said "wow". [The review](reviews-pipeline-demo-2026-11-23-workflow-round-2.html).

The same method and personas as [round 1](runs-pipeline-demo-2026-11-23-workflow-r1.html). The "How, in detail" disclosures were closed in the captures, so no persona read them. These are simulations, and never replace the measured checks or a real person.

| Sceptical friend | no | 3 | "Nine steps and a timeline, but no finished site to look at ... nothing says what it costs to keep the site running." |

## What changed

The price on the first screen worked: it is the first thing that pulled the founder to yes, and all five called the three lines (get, do, costs) clear and honest. The noes moved on to the next questions:
1. **What it costs after handover** (three noes): the Claude-plan line raises a cost the page does not answer, and it does not say who hosts the site or what that costs.
2. **No finished site anywhere** (four of five asked for one): the Car Buyer story still ends at "next".
3. **Insider names in the timeline** ("sites.b", "factory.b", "the decisions agent", "signed record"): three of five.
