# Synthetic users: The Cyber Boardroom brochure, round 1

**Site:** https://the-cyber-boardroom.startups.sovereignweb.site/ (home: The Newsroom). **Commit:** fe6a639 (v0.1.3). **Run:** 20 November 2026, about 21:45 UTC, by design.b.rounds. **Look score: 3.0** (3, 3, 3, 3, 3). Nobody said "wow". [The review](reviews-the-cyber-boardroom-2026-11-20-round-1.html).

Method: [Synthetic users](synthetic-users.html), with one difference this round: to cover five sites at once, **one agent played all five personas in turn** for each site, instead of one agent per persona. The personas are less independent than usual, so read the agreement counts with that in mind. Each was given only what a visitor gets: the first phone screen, the chooser opened, full-page screenshots at 390 px (light and dark) and 1366 px, and the visible text. Two of its findings were my capture's, not the site's, and are left out: the chooser thumbnails looked blank because my screenshot was taken before the lazy images loaded (they load for a visitor), and the tall phone captures stopped at 7,000 px. These are simulations: they inform a rating, and never replace the measured checks or a real person.

## Verdicts

### Phone visitor in a hurry: 3 of 5

"The Cyber Boardroom", then "Startups Newsroom", then the pill and "Sovereign Website": it reads as three brands, and the headline starts about 290 px down. Change: one brand line.

### Small business owner: 3 of 5

Neat but plain; the jargon ("serverless and driven by GenAI bots and agents", "pip or Docker") and the superscripts "look like homework". Change: one plain sentence near the top on who should care.

### Low-vision reader: 3 of 5

Clustered superscripts; small, low-contrast captions; a desktop timeline of dots labelled 1 to 7 with only "2025" on the axis; the product screenshot is unreadable text in an image. Change: spaced source links and a text timeline.

### Design critic: 3 of 5

The serif headline, drop cap and red pull quote show intent, but the masthead, rules and teal bar stack up before content; the timeline has no labels; dark mode keeps a bright screenshot. Change: label the timeline.

### Non-executive director: 3 of 5

Would try Athena via thecyberboardroom.com; there is no price, contact or "book a demo". "community edition", "beta" and releases ending in January 2025 raise "is it maintained?". Change: state plainly what a board gets and how to start.

## Agreed by three or more

1. The header is crowded and it is unclear whose page this is (four).
2. Clean but plain, no wow (four).
3. The only next step is leaving (three).
4. The source clusters clutter the text (three).
5. The timeline is unlabelled (three).
