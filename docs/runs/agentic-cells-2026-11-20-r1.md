# Synthetic users: AgentiCCells brochure, round 1

**Site:** https://agentic-cells.startups.sovereignweb.site/ (home: The Story). **Commit:** eab18eb (v0.1.3). **Run:** 20 November 2026, about 21:45 UTC, by design.b.rounds. **Look score: 3.0** (3, 3, 3, 3, 3). Nobody said "wow". [The review](reviews-agentic-cells-2026-11-20-round-1.html).

Method: [Synthetic users](synthetic-users.html), with one difference this round: to cover five sites at once, **one agent played all five personas in turn** for each site, instead of one agent per persona. The personas are less independent than usual, so read the agreement counts with that in mind. Each was given only what a visitor gets: the first phone screen, the chooser opened, full-page screenshots at 390 px (light and dark) and 1366 px, and the visible text. Two of its findings were my capture's, not the site's, and are left out: the chooser thumbnails looked blank because my screenshot was taken before the lazy images loaded (they load for a visitor), and the tall phone captures stopped at 7,000 px. These are simulations: they inform a rating, and never replace the measured checks or a real person.

## Verdicts

### Phone visitor in a hurry: 3 of 5

A 190 px header, then the wordmark and the same name again as a heading; the screenshot is unreadable at phone width. Change: shrink the header so a promise and a button fit above the fold.

### Small business owner: 3 of 5

"repositories", "encrypted sgit vault lanes", "board/HOLD.md"; "$11.62" on a page priced in pounds. Change: one plain section: what I get, what I do, what it costs.

### Low-vision reader: 3 of 5

Grey italic intro with weak contrast; the chart label "20000" wraps to "2000 / 0"; the chooser text runs together. Change: fix the chooser and chart labels, and darken the intro.

### Design critic: 3 of 5

A restrained serif and teal with care in the dividers, but a lone "0" stat on desktop, a sans wordmark over a serif title, a long page with one rhythm. Change: real imagery or a distinctive diagram.

### Consultant considering resale: 3 of 5

Would open the partner programme, which leaves the page; "first proposal, not an offer" means no price to quote. Change: a partner summary: terms, a sample delivered cell, how to sign up.

## Agreed by three or more

1. Honest, but "nothing has been sold yet" leaves nothing to act on (three).
2. Written for technical readers (four).
3. The only next step is an external link (three).
4. Unfinished details: the chooser text and the "2000 / 0" chart label (three).
5. The small screenshot works as neither imagery nor information (three).
