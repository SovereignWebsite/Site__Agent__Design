# Synthetic users: Streaming, round 2

**Site:** https://streaming.tool.sovereignweb.site/ at 4bfa4f0 (v0.1.11), kept by streaming.d: a redesign of the main screen since round 1. The default design is now Automatic (Paper when the device is light, Night when dark), and the chooser is in the footer. The run covered an empty first visit and the list shown by "Show an example list" (made-up titles). **Run:** 22 November 2026, 03:03 UTC, by synthetic-users.d, asked for by design.d. **Look score: 3.2** (3, 3, 4, 3, 3), up from 2.8 in [round 1](runs-streaming-2026-11-22-r1.html). **Best design votes:** Automatic 3, Coast 1, Cinema 1. **Weakest:** Cinema 3, Paper 1, Night 1. Nobody said "wow".

Method: [Synthetic users](synthetic-users.html). One subagent per persona, each given only the phone first screen (390 x 844), full-page phone and desktop (1440) screenshots, light and dark, at 2x, for both states, the visible text, and the first phone screen of each of the six designs. None was given the guidelines or design.d's findings. The site's own audience, as design.d set it: someone in the UK or Portugal who watches Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video or Apple TV+ and wants to keep their own watchlist. These are simulations, and never replace the measured checks or a real person.

| Persona | Look | Best | Weakest | The one change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Phone visitor | 3 | Automatic | Cinema | Cut the grey paragraph so the steps and example come straight after the box |
| Small business owner | 3 | Coast | Cinema | A top section with a picture and who made this |
| Low-vision reader | 4 | Automatic | Cinema | Darker, slightly larger secondary text: Remove links, helper paragraph, stars |
| Design critic | 3 | Automatic | Night | Brand and menu on one line on the phone; a title face with some character |
| Streaming viewer | 3 | Cinema | Paper | A poster or picture for each title |

## Agreed by all five

1. **"No account, no server, no cookies, no tracking", said early and plainly, earns trust.** The labelled made-up example, the export reminder, the source link and the version add to it.
2. **Clean, calm and readable,** but plain: four of the five say it looks like a form, with nothing that says film or evening.

## Agreed by four

3. **The Add box is now easy to find,** first on the page with a solid button (all but the small business owner, who looked for something else).
4. **Small, faint secondary controls:** the outline stars, the small grey underlined "Remove", and in two cases the grey helper text.
5. **"Sovereign Website" in the top corner is small and means nothing** to a visitor; two add "Use with your AI".

## Agreed by three

6. **Paper and Night look the same as Automatic.** Checked: Paper's first phone screen is identical to Automatic's in light, and Night's to Automatic's in dark.
7. **On the phone, the three menu links sit on a second line** under "Streaming", looking loose.
8. **Cinema is the weakest:** salmon and grey on near-black maroon reads soft or harsh (but the streaming viewer's favourite).

## Agreed by two

- The same "find it on PrompTugal, paste it here" instruction appears twice on the first screen.
- "Press / to start typing" means nothing on a phone.
- The design chooser is hard to find at the bottom of the page.
