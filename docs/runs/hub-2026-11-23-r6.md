# Synthetic users: the hub's five designs

**Site:** https://hub.sovereignweb.site/ at 9f78fdd (v0.1.23). The five designs:
- The Register, the home and lead design;
- The Gallery (/gallery/);
- Contact Sheet (/contact-sheet/);
- The Street (/street/);
- The Line (/line/).

**Run:** 23 November 2026, about 13:00 UTC, by design.b.rounds. **Look score for the home page: 3.2** (3, 3, 4, 3, 3). **Best design votes:** The Line twice, then The Street, Contact Sheet and The Register once each. Nobody said "wow". [The review](reviews-hub-2026-11-23-round-6.html).

Method: [Synthetic users](synthetic-users.html). One agent played five personas, in its own crop folder:
- a phone visitor in a hurry;
- a small-business owner;
- a low-vision reader;
- a design critic;
- a sceptical journalist.

These are simulations, and never replace the measured checks or a real person.

| Persona | Home | Best design (score) | Weakest | The one change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Phone visitor | 3 | The Line (4) | The Register | "collapse each Register entry to name, one line and the link" |
| Business owner | 3 | The Line (4) | Contact Sheet | "a clear 'Want one? Start with one request' step" |
| Low-vision reader | 4 | The Street (4) | Contact Sheet | "move the stamp and circle off the screenshots" |
| Design critic | 3 | Contact Sheet (4, desktop) | The Street (phone) | "give The Register one strong picture on screen one" |
| Journalist | 3 | The Register (3) | The Gallery | "say plainly '1 of 33 handed over so far'" |

## Agreed by three or more

1. **The pages are long on a phone**, The Register most of all (27,078 px; The Line is 12,506).
2. **Overlays cover the screenshots:** the Register's "Handed over" stamp, and Contact Sheet's red circle and frame numbers.
3. **Long addresses break mid-word** ("kate-curtis-" / "evans.founder...").
4. **The Line explains the offer best.**
5. **There is no "get one" step**, and only one site has been handed over so far.
