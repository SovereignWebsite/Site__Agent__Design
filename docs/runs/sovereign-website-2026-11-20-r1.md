# Synthetic users: Sovereign Website brochure, round 1

**Site:** https://sovereign-website.startups.sovereignweb.site/ (home: The Brochure). **Commit:** 490c1c9 (v0.1.3). **Run:** 20 November 2026, about 21:45 UTC, by design.b.rounds. **Look score: 2.8** (3, 3, 3, 3, 2). Nobody said "wow". [The review](reviews-sovereign-website-2026-11-20-round-1.html).

Method: [Synthetic users](synthetic-users.html), with one difference this round: to cover five sites at once, **one agent played all five personas in turn** for each site, instead of one agent per persona. The personas are less independent than usual, so read the agreement counts with that in mind. Each was given only what a visitor gets: the first phone screen, the chooser opened, full-page screenshots at 390 px (light and dark) and 1366 px, and the visible text. Two of its findings were my capture's, not the site's, and are left out: the chooser thumbnails looked blank because my screenshot was taken before the lazy images loaded (they load for a visitor), and the tall phone captures stopped at 7,000 px. These are simulations: they inform a rating, and never replace the measured checks or a real person.

## Verdicts

### Phone visitor in a hurry: 3 of 5

Two menu rows, the pill and a stray "Sovereign Website" above the headline; two brand names within 250 px. Change: a one-line header.

### Small business owner: 3 of 5

Wanted a price or a sign-up, but "Nothing is sold yet"; "In figures" counts seconds and commits. Change: show a real finished small-business site from the Hub.

### Low-vision reader: 3 of 5

The kicker is small white text on a gradient; small grey captions; unreadable screenshot text. Change: larger, higher-contrast chooser labels and small text.

### Design critic: 3 of 5

Consistent (one rust accent, tidy cards, good dark mode), but an empty desktop timeline with only 1, 2 and 3 on a line, an orphan card, eight repeats of rule, heading, cards. Change: one strong original visual.

### Freelance photographer: 2 of 5

A page for visual people with no visual work. Change: a real image-led site that was built and handed over this way.

## Agreed by three or more

1. Nothing to do but leave (three).
2. Jargon and source numbers (three).
3. No real example of a finished site, no strong imagery (three).
4. Clean but generic, no wow (five).
5. The header and chooser are cluttered (three).
