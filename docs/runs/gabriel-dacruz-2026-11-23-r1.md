# Synthetic users: Gabriel DaCruz, round 1

**Site:** https://gabriel-dacruz.agenticcells.site/ at ddf6875 (v0.1.26). **Run:** 23 November 2026, about 15:30 UTC, by design.b.rounds. **Look score: 3.2** (3, 3, 2, 4, 4). **Best design votes:** The Sleeve twice, then The Cue Sheet, The Score and The Signal Chain once each. Nobody said "wow"; the critic called The Sleeve's hero "almost a wow". [The review](reviews-gabriel-dacruz-2026-11-23-round-1.html).

Method: [Synthetic users](synthetic-users.html). One agent played five personas, in its own folder:
- a phone visitor;
- a film or game director looking for a composer;
- a low-vision reader;
- a design critic;
- a music listener or fellow musician.

**A fault in this run's capture:** the chooser page /d/ was saved over the home page's files, so the personas judged Premiere (the home design) only from its thumbnail. I recaptured the home page afterwards (below). These are simulations, and never replace a real person.

## Agreed by three or more

1. **The hero quote is the best line on the site** (all five): "He can take a song, write it out for a full orchestra, and then stand in front of that orchestra."
2. **The Sleeve's hero has the strongest look.**
3. **"TO CONFIRM" everywhere weakens trust** (four personas), above all "his exact role on each is TO CONFIRM".
4. **The music is too far down,** and the black or blank tiles hurt.
5. **There is no way to contact him about work.** The only route is "MESSAGE ON INSTAGRAM", and that is for lessons.
6. **The phone layouts cramp the content:**
   - the design switcher wraps to three lines;
   - the poster strip is cut at "WAR FI";
   - the /works/ table is cut at "SPOT".
