# Synthetic users: Grace Farrell, round 2

**Site:** 1611e49 (v0.1.4); the home page is design 2, Fringe flyer. **Run:** 21 November 2026, about 01:10 UTC, by design.b.rounds. **Look score: 2.8** (3, 3, 2, 3, 3), up from 2.4. **Best design votes:** 5, 5, 2, 5, 4, so Editorial leads. Nobody said "wow". [The review](reviews-grace-farrell-2026-11-21-round-2.html).

Method: [Synthetic users](synthetic-users.html). One agent played all five personas, in its own folder. It was given the visible text, so two of its findings were checked against the accessibility tree and did not hold for screen readers: the map numbers are hidden, and no heading is read twice. The doubled kicker and heading is still seen twice. These are simulations, and never replace the measured checks or a real person.
