## What each content item did

- **The Cyber Boardroom, the conversation band:** helped most. All five personas named it; 3.0 to 3.6.
- **RiskMandate, the prices in order:** "clearer and more honest" to the sceptic and the buyer. RiskMandate reached four 4s again.
- **sgit, the timeline rail:** helped, but a persona measured the dots and found them evenly spaced, though the caption says "Spaced by the time between dates". Checked: each date carries its gap (`--gap:42`, `--gap:90`, `--gap:2` and so on), and every dot sits 61 px below the last. The CSS does not use the variable.
- **AgentiCCells, the Stop bar first:** the promise is now on the first phone screen. It did not shorten the long column of cards.
- **Sovereign Website, the keys and the self-check:** the self-check as the one next step helped. The key ring "reads as a tree": its numerals overlap the rings, and its legend circles are faint.

Nobody said "wow".
