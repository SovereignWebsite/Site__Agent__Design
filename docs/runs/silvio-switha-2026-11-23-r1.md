## Agreed by three or more

1. **The film is the strongest hook:** "Watch the film, 3:38", large and above the fold on every design (all five).
2. **The pages are too long and repeat themselves.** The same figures (2,300, 43, 16, 21, 1,236) and the same quotes come two or three times on one page (four personas).
3. **The Studio is the most distinctive design** (four).
4. **"Contact details to follow."** Every "Hire Silvio" and "Book a session" ends there (three).
5. **The small monospace labels are hard to read** (three).
