**Sites:** sgit (f2878a0), The Cyber Boardroom (9529348), RiskMandate (f77d6eb), AgentiCCells (b491fa1), Sovereign Website (c110a7e), all v0.1.5, home designs as rebuilt after round 1. **Run:** 20 November 2026, about 22:25 UTC, by design.b.rounds. Method: [Synthetic users](synthetic-users.html). As in round 1, one agent per site played all five personas in turn, which makes them less independent than one agent each. The personas were the phone visitor, a small business owner, a low-vision reader, a design critic, and the site's own audience (a developer; a non-executive director; a head of security; a partner consultant; a photographer). This time the chooser was captured with its thumbnails loaded. These are simulations: they inform a rating, and never replace the measured checks or a real person.

## Agreed across the five sites

1. **The source number after the headline looks like a typo** (sgit, The Cyber Boardroom, Sovereign Website).
2. **The opening does not say plainly what it is or who it is for** (sgit: "abstract jargon"; Sovereign Website: "'Show me one.' is too cryptic").
3. **The shrunk screenshots cannot be read on a phone** (sgit, The Cyber Boardroom, Sovereign Website).
4. **After the first screen it becomes card after card,** with no change of rhythm and no imagery (every site).
5. **"Choose a design" and "Sovereign Website" in the header puzzle visitors** (every site).
6. **The source markers are small and faint,** and a screen reader may join them to words. The markers now carry an aria-label "source N"; the run-together digits came from the visible text the personas were given, which is not what a screen reader reads, so this is weighed lightly.
7. **Jargon shuts out the non-technical persona** on every site except Sovereign Website's showroom. That is expected for these audiences, and is weighed against the site's own audience.

Points raised by single personas that the review takes up:
- The Cyber Boardroom: the real site's "Important Note" says chats are publicly available, which worried the director; quote it plainly.
- RiskMandate: show one real policy excerpt.
- AgentiCCells: "$11.62" on a page in pounds.
