## What this round's items did

- **Sovereign Website** rose from 3.0 to 3.4. The owners' sites now land on the first phone screen, and four personas called them the strongest element. Three said the three owners are then repeated lower down as text-only blocks, and that the 2 + 1 grid leaves the third thumbnail alone and pushes the button to the edge.
- **AgentiCCells:** the example site now starts on the first phone screen, and three personas said so. But it shows one site under "A website like these", so "these" over-promises. The diagram cards are cramped on phones. Three 4s, not four.
- **RiskMandate:** four 4s. The delta "partly helps": the hatching covers the whole grant, so the delta is not distinct, and labels on stripes hurt the low-vision reader. The 2 by 2 cards fit one screen but run at about three words a line.
- **The Cyber Boardroom** fell from 3.6 to 2.8. The new hero half lands: on a phone it is cropped to the grey prompt list, with text too small to read (three personas). The live chat bubbles just below are the best part (four personas). The investor noted that the latest releases cited are from December 2024 and January 2025.
- **sgit** fell from 3.4 to 3.0, all five at 3. The new headline helps (three personas grasped the product from it at once). The hero is crowded: two quotes, then a screenshot whose "Penetration Test Report" heading reads as the page's own next section.

Nobody said "wow".
