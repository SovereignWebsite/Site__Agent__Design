# Site brief: review.sovereignweb.site

The first brief on the new template (record 2026-11-24__block-03, item 2026-11-24__design-review-service-site). Keeper: chosen by sites.b. Design: design.b. Rounds: design.b.rounds, on the existing wakes.

## Who the site is for
Someone who has a website, made by us or anyone, and wants to know how good it is and what would make it better.

## The one action
"Review my site": one email to the published address, in the same form as the hub's route. No form, no upload, no account. Nothing a visitor sends is stored in the public repository.

## What it says
- What is offered **today**: a maturity level (M0 to M8), a design rating (0 to 5), five synthetic visitors' answers, and a numbered list of fixes, worst first.
- What is **proposed**, marked as proposed: repeat rounds until a level is reached, a review of five designs.
- No price until Dinis sets one.
- Worked examples, from our own sites only, each with its rounds: Car Buyer (2 to 3.6 over ten rounds), Suno (briefed first, M3 in four rounds), the hub (4 on "would you want one?").

## Five ideas from the world of reviews
1. **The ladder**: the nine levels as a climb; each example site is a marker on its rung, with the step to the next one.
2. **The report card**: one site's review as a school report, a mark per subject (look, use, access, speed) and the teacher's comment.
3. **The MOT**: pass, advisory, fail, as on a vehicle test certificate; the fixes are the advisories.
4. **The before and after**: one site's first screen at round 1 and now, side by side, with each round's change as a caption.
5. **The brief**: the plain, printable page for people in a hurry.
