## hub.b's list: the "why not" answers, ranked

1. **No price, no form, nothing to do** (6 noes; 11 of 25 answers counting the caveats under the yeses): "I'd need a price and a phone number before I'd bother." Two things go on every design's first screen, under "I want one":
   - **The price,** as sovereignwebsite.ai publishes it, quoted with its source link and the date it was checked. strategy.b supplies it (brief, item 5). Today /start/ sends readers to that page without quoting it.
   - **Who they deal with,** and the route. The route is Dinis's decision (brief, "I want one"), so until then, say plainly what happens next, for example "Leave a message in the box; an agent replies", once feedback.b's box is agreed. Do not put up a form that does not exist.
2. **Unproven: one site handed over** (4): "a pipeline with one finished product". This cannot be designed away. Show it truthfully, and show what is near: put the sites at "five designs to choose from" (Emily, Sarah) as "next to be handed over", if their owners agree. Lead with the eleven people's sites, not the 33 (brief, item 3), so the brochures, demos and "made-up" companies stop diluting the proof.
3. **Hard to read** (4):
   - The Gallery and Contact Sheet ignore light mode. Give each a light variant: a white-walled gallery, and a contact sheet printed on paper.
   - Descriptions are cut with "..." on phones; show the first sentence whole.
   - The Line's "One request" text is smaller and greyer than everything near it; it is the most important line on the page, so make it body size and body colour.
4. **Can't see the work** (2):
   - On the Register's phone view, Emily's and Sarah's thumbnails are tiny;
   - on The Line, Emily and Sarah at station 3 are names in boxes with no picture.

   Show each person's site as a picture at least half the screen wide.
5. **No real business like mine** (1): the business room is "two made-up examples" and a "redesign proposal". The brief moves these to /more/; until a real business has a site, do not lead with businesses.
6. **"Edits it herself on GitHub" sounds technical** (1). Add what the brief says in plain words: "and changes it by talking to her AI", linked to Kate's /agents/.

**Also,** the Register hides Entry 34's "Not switched on yet" inside the collapsed entry, so the reader sees a Google Maps site with no warning. Put the status on the visible line.
