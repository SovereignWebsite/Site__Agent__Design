## To fix, in order

1. **Each design's first screen opens the same way:** the kicker, "Websites their owners own.", the same paragraph and the three counts. The idea arrives after it. Let each design's idea *be* its first screen:
   - **The Register:** the first entry, Kate's, with its "Handed over" seal, as the hero. Put the headline over it as the register's title: "The register of title: websites their owners own". The critic asked for "one strong picture on screen one".
   - **The Gallery:** the first framed site under its spotlight, with its wall label, filling the phone screen. The headline becomes the room's title.
   - **The Street:** the first shopfront, sign and door, must be whole on the first phone screen. Today its sign is cut off at the bottom edge. Put the counts on the road after it.
   - **Contact Sheet** and **The Line** already show their idea on the first screen; keep them.
2. **Overlays on the screenshots:**
   - The Register's "HANDED OVER" stamp covers Kate's own "Choose a design" button;
   - Contact Sheet's red circle cuts through Kate's name and button, and its frame numbers sit on words ("05A entrepreneur", "12A rsion").

   Move the stamp and the circle to the frame's edge, outside the picture: `.stamp{right:-12px;top:-12px}` on a frame with `overflow:visible`. Put the frame numbers on the film rebate under each frame, not on the picture.
3. **Long addresses break mid-word** ("kate-curtis-" / "evans.founder.sovereignweb.site"), and the Street's awning wraps "sovereignweb." / "site". Break only at the dots: insert `<wbr>` after each "." in the address, and set `overflow-wrap:normal;word-break:normal`. On The Street's signs, use a smaller size for long hosts: `font-size:clamp(11px,3.2vw,15px)`.
4. **Phone length.** The Register runs to 27,078 px on a phone, and The Line to 12,506. On phones, show each Register entry as its name, one line and the link, with the deed rows (Address, Kind, Held, Status, Files, Designs) in a `<details>` titled "The entry". Do the same for the Gallery's wall labels after the first room.
5. **The numbering:** the home page says "Design 2 of 5, The Register, the lead design", with The Gallery as Design 1. Make the lead design 1 and keep the order of the chooser. The footer's "the lead design is The Register" then agrees with the number.
6. **A next step for a visitor who wants one.** No design says how to get a site. Give station 1 of The Line ("One request") a link, and add one line under each hero: "Want one? It starts with one request", linking to the request page the hub already uses, if there is one. If there is none yet, say so plainly instead of linking.
7. **Honest counts in the hero.** "1 handed over" is the strongest fact and the easiest to miss. Make the counts read as a sentence: "33 sites working, 11 of them people's own; 1 handed over to its owner so far". Contact Sheet's 34th frame (Google Maps, "Not switched on yet") should be counted apart in the same way: "and 1 waiting to be switched on".
