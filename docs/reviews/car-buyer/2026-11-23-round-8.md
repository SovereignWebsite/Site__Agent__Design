## What to change, ranked

1. **Make the drawings bolder and exact:**
   - lines at least 3 px in the design's strongest colour, so they hold in dark mode;
   - caption each with what it shows ("Illustration: a windscreen with a price card"), not "Illustration" alone;
   - **Road atlas:** J1 to J7, as in the list, drawn as a road (two edges and a centre line), not a zigzag line; mark J6 with the ▲ London symbol;
   - **Logbook:** nine numbered fields, as in the list, with a "Registration" field at the top so it reads as a logbook;
   - **MOT certificate:** replace "PASS" with "EXAMPLE", or an unticked "Result" box, so it cannot be read as a verdict on the saved car;
   - **Windscreen card:** draw the car's bonnet and the wiper, so the half-circle reads as a windscreen.
2. **MOT certificate on a phone:** the notice box fills the first screen, so the drawing is never seen. Put the drawing above the notice, smaller, or start the notice with one line and the rest behind "Read the notice".
3. **The design picker:** the critic now finds it hidden at the foot. Keep it there, but add one small "Designs" link in the header row, at the right, as the startup brochures do in their footers.
4. **The draft banner:** as before, it is for Dinis to lift.
