## The synthetic users: 5 of 5; five ideas on the home page

[The run](runs-car-buyer-2026-11-23-r5.html). Look 3.4, up from 2.8. Four of five see five ideas on the home page:
- Logbook's numbered fields;
- MOT certificate's notice box and test items;

The low-vision reader could "tell them apart by shape, even blurred". The design critic counts four ideas and a default, because Forecourt is the plain card grid. /designs/ calls it "the plain reference".

## The rating: 3, and M3

M3 asks for five real ideas from the owner's world, each rebuilding the page, a rating of 3 or more, and synthetic users who can tell the five apart. The home page, where the designs are chosen and seen first, now meets that, by 4 of 5. So **Car Buyer is rated 3 and reaches M3.** The inner pages are where it reaches 4.

## What to change, ranked

1. **The checklist, in each design's own structure** (5 of 5 call it the same list in different paint). It is the page the personas use most:
   - **Logbook:** the three stages as numbered boxed sections, with the ticks as fields;
   - **MOT certificate:** each item as a test row, with its tick as the result ("Done", "Not yet");
   - **Windscreen card:** the three stages as three cards, each with a big count ("4 of 7 done");
   - **Road atlas:** the stages as junctions on the rail, with the symbols from the key.
2. **Windscreen card's empty state:** three personas named it weakest, because "Type a plate" and "Work it out" in giant boxes "looked broken". Before a car is saved, show what each box will hold ("Your registration", "When the plate was issued", "Your first-year total") in the same type, and make the first a link to Check a car. "Add a car" is in monospace and the other two in bold sans; use one face.
3. **Give Forecourt an idea of its own**, or keep it as the plain reference and say so on /designs/. It is the low-vision reader's best, so keep it calm. One idea: the dealer's forecourt board, with the five tools as priced-up cards ("Free", "Paid", "In your browser").
4. **The Londoner:**
   - the home page says ULEZ and the Congestion Charge are on top, but not what they cost; add the two daily rates, already cited on /costs/;
   - in Road atlas, ULEZ sits inside J6, the last and longest junction; give London its own junction or a key symbol.
5. **Small:** in Logbook, the "Check a car" pill makes row 1's text wrap awkwardly on a phone.
