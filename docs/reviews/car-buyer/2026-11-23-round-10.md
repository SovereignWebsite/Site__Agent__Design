## What to change, ranked

1. **The map on a phone:** a portrait version below 600 px wide.
   - The road runs down the screen, J1 at the top and J7 at the bottom.
   - The labels are at least 14 px.
   - The London district and the paid stop are kept.
   - The same SVG can switch with a `<picture>`-style pair of drawings, or with CSS that shows one of two `<svg>`s.
2. **Tie the checklist to the junctions:**
   - name its sections by the junctions they belong to ("J1 to J2: before you commit"), instead of A to C;
   - the map's grid letters (A to J) clash with J1 to J7, so use numbers for the grid, or drop it on phones.
3. **The paid diamond** marks "I understand why the seller may sell it, and any finance on it". Finance is found by the paid report, but the reason for selling is not. Split the item, or move the diamond to the finance half only.
4. **MOT certificate on a phone:** show "Not yet" as well as "Done", as on desktop.
5. **Small:** align the checkboxes on the diamond and triangle rows (2 px off).
