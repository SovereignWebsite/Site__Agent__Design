2. **Road atlas's order:** London (J6) comes after paying (J5). The TfL check belongs before you commit. Mark J1's TfL item with ▲, and drop the separate London junction or move it before J5.
3. **Road atlas's checklist:** the item "I have read the MOT history, the recalls and a paid history report" carries the paid diamond, but two of its three are free. Split it into a free item and a paid one.
4. **Road atlas:** the home page's J1 to J7 are steps, and the checklist's J1 to J3 are sections. Use one meaning, or letter the sections (A to C).
5. **The Windscreen card drawing:** give the price card a price-shaped line ("£ ..." with a short rule) and drop the bar-code look.
6. **The Logbook drawing:** its nine fields should match the nine rows below it, and its labels should be larger.
