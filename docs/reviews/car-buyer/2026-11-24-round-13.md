2. **Carry each idea into the first screen,** starting with the Next card and the plate:

   - Dashboard: the Next card as the lit warning light; the plate on the dash;
   - Tax disc: the Next card as the disc in the holder;

5. **Small:** in Road atlas the checkbox touches the step title; say on the tax step what you need to hand (the official page names it); "Our advice" on the last step says it is ours.
