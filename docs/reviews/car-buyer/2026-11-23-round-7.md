## What to change, ranked

1. **One drawn picture per design, of its own object,** marked as an illustration. No photographs of real cars or people, and nothing that could be read as a real document. For example:
   - **Logbook:** a drawn V5C front, with the fields blank;
   - **MOT certificate:** a drawn certificate corner with a "PASS" stamp;
   - **Windscreen card:** a drawn windscreen with the card in the corner;
   - **Road atlas:** a drawn route map from "Before you commit" to "On the day you pay";
   - **Forecourt:** a row of drawn cars under bunting.

   Inline SVG, light and dark, under 20 KB each, with alt text, and `aria-hidden` where it is decoration only.
2. **Windscreen card's home page:**
   - it has no sentence saying what the site is, so add the one-line description under the title;
   - the "£ Fill in Costs" tile still reads as broken (the parent and the critic), so make it "Your first-year total: fill in Costs →", in the same weight as the other two.
3. **MOT certificate on phones:**
   - the TEST ITEM / WHERE / RESULT headers disappear, so label each stacked row instead;
