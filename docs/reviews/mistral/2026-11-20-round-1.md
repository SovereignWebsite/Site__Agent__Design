## To change before Saturday morning, worst first

1. **The phone header takes about 200 px** before the poster. "Use with your AI" wraps onto a row of its own, then comes a three-line disclaimer box. Make it one row: the name, then "Choose a design", with "Start here" and "Use with your AI" moved into the ⋮ menu or under the hero. Make the disclaimer one line ("Independent demonstration, not affiliated with Mistral AI"), and keep the full sentence in the footer.
2. **The poster says when, not where.** The page says, rightly: "Venue, times, schedule and anything else about the day come from the organisers, not from this page." So link to the organisers' own public event page in the hero, as "Venue and times: on the organisers' page ↗". Do this only if one exists and is public; never write a venue that is not published. The phone visitor's first question was "where?".
3. **The inline sources bury the commands.** "(Source: ...; as read on 20 November 2026.)" follows nearly every sentence, and three of five personas found it slowed reading. A screen reader reads each one aloud. Keep every source, but move them:
   - one small numbered marker per claim, with the list at the end of each section;
   - or a single "Sources for this section" line.
   Never put a source inside a code block or a step.
4. **The recipe needs copy buttons, and must not wrap mid-word.**
   - The curl command wraps at "completio / ns" on a phone. Set `overflow-x:auto` on the `pre`, inside a focusable region, with `white-space:pre`.
   - Add a "Copy" button for each block. That needs a few lines of script; or use a `<details>` with the plain command to select, if the site stays script-free.
   - The developer's worry was getting a key in time: "whether Free mode needs a payment card today was not confirmed". If Mistral's own page settles it before Saturday, say so. If not, lead with the local path ("No key needed").
5. **Designs 1, 3 and 4 share their words.** Only the map and the poster feel distinct to the critic. Give each design its own content order, from the same facts:
   - **1, the newsroom:** the story of the choice, as it is.
   - **3, the console:** nothing but the call. The provider switch, the request and the response, with the facts as comments in the code. On desktop, fill the empty lower half with the response panel.
   - **4, the ledger:** the five questions as the page, with "Your set-up" and "Your tally" first. The small business owner found this the one page that answered "does sovereignty matter to me?".
6. **Say "sovereign" in plain words on the home page.** One line under the hero, linking to the ledger: "Sovereign means you can choose, and change, who runs your model. The ledger asks five questions of any AI you depend on."
7. **/demos/ looks like another site** (cream and terracotta, with a different navigation). Give it the home page's shell.
8. **Smaller:**
   - the cyan "HACKATHON / LONDON" kicker is small on the dark gradient; set it at 16 px or more and weight 700;
   - design 2's grid runs behind the body text;
   - design 2's meaning rides on line colour and dashes alone, so add the labels ("brief", "brief + key") to each line;
   - the home page stacks two numbered lists (1 to 5, then 1 to 3); number only one.
