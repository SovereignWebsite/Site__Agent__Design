# Review of every live site, 20 November 2026

design.b@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 10:25 to 10:40 UTC. Read only: nothing was changed. Measured against the first draft of design/guidelines.md.

**Method.** All 40 live pages, at 390 x 844 (phone, touch) and 1366 x 900 (desktop), light and dark: 160 full-page screenshots (contact sheets in reviews/screens/, one per page: phone light, phone dark, desktop light, desktop dark). Automated per view: axe-core 4.10.2 (WCAG 2.0 to 2.2 A and AA plus best practice), requests to other hosts, cookies, browser storage, horizontal overflow, bytes transferred, console errors. Hand checks of the screenshots, token contrast computed from the CSS.

**Reviewed commits (dev):** Website 73c4e8a, Org 8f104e0, Site 81ec3cc, Hub f3aa69d, Vault b9c44a1, Site__google-maps 2ea36b3, Site__pipeline-demo 7fa98f0.

## What is already good

- **No cookies, no storage, no request to any other host, on all 160 views.** Google Maps loads only after the visitor presses the button, under a tight Content Security Policy, and the page says so before it happens. This is the reference pattern for an owner-chosen service.
- No horizontal scroll at 390 px on any page. System fonts only. Pages weigh 5 to 31 KB (the Vault 77 KB, with its decryption code). No console errors.
- Dark mode exists on every site and is well judged: none of the 160 views has a contrast failure in dark.
- sovereignwebsite.ai, design 1: passes axe on every page but one moderate item; its tokens measure muted/bg 7.1:1 and accent/bg 5.7:1 (light), 9.0 and 8.7 (dark). It is the strongest site and the right base.
- Honest voice throughout: "Proposal only", "Nothing is sold yet", made-up examples labelled made-up. No em dashes on any page.

## The problems, worst first

### 1. The family has two unrelated looks (all sites but .ai)
Since design 1 was chosen, sovereignwebsite.ai is warm paper, terracotta and serif headlines. The other six sites are still the earlier green and grey sans look, each with its own copy of the CSS and drifting values (muted is #55544f on .ai's base, #5d5c57 on hub, vault, demos; four different content widths: 72, 68, 46 and 44 rem). The only common element is the family bar. A visitor moving from .ai to .org or the Hub sees a different organisation.
**Root cause:** the Factory seed (`seed/site-template`, `seed/shared`) carries the old look inline and has no shared theme file, so every new site starts out of date.
**Fix (needs Dinis's choice):** one family token file and type scale in `seed/shared/`, from design 1, that every own site links; then .org, .site, hub, vault and the demos take it. Decide whether infrastructure sites (hub, vault, demos) use .ai's terracotta or a named sibling accent.

### 2. The Hub gallery states things that are not true
hub.sovereignwebsite.site lists sovereignwebsite.ai, .org, .site and the Hub itself as "Status: Holding page" (sites.json `"holding-page"`). .ai has ten pages in two languages; .org seven; .site a real first version. It also says "these are the first five sites" while Site__pipeline-demo and Site__google-maps are live. On the page that is meant to be the gallery for customers, this is the most serious content fault. Owner: hub.b.

### 3. Form fields you cannot see (google-maps, vault)
The From and To fields on google-maps, and the key field on the Vault, have a border of `--line` #e4e1d8 on #fbfaf7: 1.25:1, against the 3:1 that WCAG 1.4.11 asks for a control's edge. On a phone in light mode the fields read as blank space. On google-maps the button is also at half opacity (disabled, as no key is set), so the page's one interaction looks broken rather than "not switched on yet". Fix: a `--field-border` token at 3:1 or more in both schemes; say on the button itself why it is off.

### 4. The phone header pushes the page down (sovereignwebsite.ai, every page)
On a 390 x 844 phone the family bar (2 lines) plus brand, ten nav links in three lines and the language switch take 247 px; the h1 starts at 317 px, over a third of the screen down. Fix: six or fewer primary links (the rest are already in the footer), or a `<details>` menu that needs no script; keep the language switch beside the brand.

### 5. Text cut off and unreachable in code blocks (.org pattern, pipeline-demo request)
The JSON on pipeline-demo's "Asking for a site" is clipped at the right on phone and on desktop; the `pre` scrolls but cannot be reached by keyboard (axe, serious: scrollable-region-focusable). Same on sovereignwebsite.org/pattern.html at phone width. Fix: `white-space: pre-wrap` for short examples, or `tabindex="0"` plus a label on the scrolling block.

### 6. Family bar contrast fails on pipeline-demo, and is fragile everywhere
The shared family bar lightens links with `opacity: .8` on an inherited colour. On pipeline-demo the inherited colour is the muted grey, giving 3.87:1 in light mode (fails 4.5:1, 8 links, every page). It passes elsewhere only by luck of what it inherits. Its 12.8 px text and 15 px tall links meet the 24 px target spacing rule by one or two pixels on phones. Fix in `seed/shared/family-bar.css` (Factory): explicit measured colours, no opacity, .875rem text, links padded to 24 px.

### 7. Hub, Vault and the demos have no site header
These pages open with an eyebrow label and the h1; there is no site name linking home and no navigation of their own (pipeline-demo has a small page list under the eyebrow). Hub and Vault also have no skip link, `lang="en"` not `en-GB`, and no `color-scheme` meta. The site template in the seed is the source.

### 8. Body text set in grey (seed template, hub, vault, demos)
The seed sets `p { color: var(--muted) }`, so every paragraph is muted grey and nothing reads as the main text. It passes contrast (6.4:1) but flattens the page. Body should be `--fg`, muted only for secondary lines.

### 9. Smaller items
- No favicon on any site but .ai (browser tabs and bookmarks show a blank).
- .ai home (and pt, and designs/1): the offer `aside` sits inside a section (axe, moderate: landmark-complementary-is-top-level). Use a `div` with `aria-labelledby`, or move it out.
- At 1366 px .org, .site, hub and vault use a 44 to 46 rem column under a 72 rem family bar: two left edges on one screen.
- sovereignwebsite.site/404.html: no skip link or description; fine for a 404 but should follow the template.
- Vault key field placeholder is cut mid-word on phones ("https://vault.sovereignw").
- google-maps uses class `small` that its stylesheet does not define.
