# Round 6: Kate Curtis-Evans, from correct to wow

design.b@SovereignWebsite.dinis (fresh session), 20 November 2026, about 15:55 UTC. Dinis Cruz's new guidance (guidelines 12, "Wow, not bland", written by the other design.b session at a4dbc7b) says a founder site that was correct but plain "feels like websites created 10 years ago", and asks for the wow of the Silvio site. Kate's site passed every measured check at round 5, but by this new bar it is plain. **The round 5 sign-off is withdrawn: rating 3** until the round below is live.

## What changes

1. **A visual chooser,** as on silvio-switha.agenticcells.site.
   - "Choose a design" opens five cards. Each card has a real screenshot of that design (a 640 px WebP made with Playwright from the live page, lazy loaded), its name and its one-line idea; the current one is outlined.
   - Build it with `<details>`/`<summary>`, no script. The plain link row stays as the fallback and for print.
2. **Every design except the Brief gets wow.** Keep each idea; give it a full-bleed hero, huge display type with a second voice, depth, and vivid colour. Two must be dark first.
   - **The Workflow Board.**
     - The desk photo goes full-bleed behind the hero, darkened at the bottom, with "Kate Curtis-Evans" set huge (clamp to about 7rem) in the serif over it.
     - The four lanes sit as cards pinned across the lower part of the photo: offset colour blocks, soft shadows, each card a few degrees off true, straightening on hover and focus (motion only under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`).
     - The Unassigned card is the biggest, in the accent, with a dashed edge.
   - **The Transcript.**
     - Scale is the drama: line numbers in a wide margin at display size, the cover sheet as a heavy bordered panel, and Kate's quote set at about 8vw across the page as the verbatim turn.
     - The desk photo is printed as "Exhibit B", clipped to the page with a paper shadow.
     - Ink red on cream, and in dark a carbon-copy blue on near-black.
   - **The Edit Window (dark first).** Make it cinematic.
     - The waveform runs full width across the hero, glowing in the teal, with a slow CSS pulse (reduced-motion safe).
     - The photo sits in an editor-style frame with light spilling from it, and the figures read out huge in mono.
     - The tracks run edge to edge.
   - **The Newsroom (dark first, as a magazine cover).**
     - The desk photo fills the first screen, with the headline "The legal transcriber who built her own platform" over it in a huge serif, the kicker and two cover lines from the facts ("34,000 minutes of audio", "Wanted: a technical co-founder"), and the masthead across the top.
     - The feature follows on paper.
   - **The Brief** stays plain and printable: it is the one place plain is right.
3. **Imagery.** Only the Matt Hinton desk photo is cleared, and the designs above are built around it.
   - The guidance now allows generated stock imagery within the accountant's budget, art-directed per design and captioned "Generated image": for example, a texture of transcript paper, a studio light or a waveform scene.
   - Never Kate, a client or any real person; never as evidence of a fact.
   - Ask accountant.b for a budget before generating any.
