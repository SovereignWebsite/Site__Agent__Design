## Measured

- **axe:** clean at 390 and 1366 px, light and dark. One h1, no sideways scroll. 2,184 px on a phone, 13 KB.
- **The seed's pages and the share card (1200 x 630):** pass. **Rule 2a:** fails, one design. /designs/ and /yours/ answer 404.
- **Only the chosen feed's host is contacted.** No cookies.
- **"Not affiliated", for each organisation:** passes. Choosing NASA, TED Blog, Variety or Rolling Stone shows "Built from [its] public WordPress data; not affiliated with [it]". The two WordPress feeds say "From WordPress's own site", under the page's own line "not affiliated with them".
- **A feed that fails:** a blocked feed says "That site did not answer just now. Try another." A slow one says "Loading the latest posts...".
- **A race puts one organisation's posts under another's name.** I reproduced it:
  1. wordpress.org's reply is delayed 8 seconds;
  2. NASA is chosen 0.8 seconds after load;
  3. at 4 seconds, NASA's posts show under "not affiliated with NASA";
  4. at 11 seconds, the late WordPress reply replaces them, under the same NASA line.

  Sent to sites.b ahead of this review. The fix is to drop any reply that is not for the site now chosen.

## What to change, ranked

1. **The race** (above).
2. **Show a section, not a list.** Each post as a card with its featured image and a short excerpt, where the feed gives them. Credit the image to the source.
3. **Put the select and the first posts on the phone's first screen.** Show the loading and error lines next to the select, where the eye is after a change.
4. **For the developer:**
   - one short "How it behaves" note: it fetches on every visit, nothing is cached or stored, and search engines see the static page without the posts;
   - what happens when a feed is slow or blocked: the two lines above.
5. **Five designs, from design.b's brief:** broadsheet, magazine, pinboard, river and brief. Each one is a style an agency could offer a client, which is the designer's "at least two styles to switch between".
