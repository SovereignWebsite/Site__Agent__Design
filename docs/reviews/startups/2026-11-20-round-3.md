## Why the look has stopped near 3: three decisions, not more craft

In 15 persona runs across the five sites this round, three complaints come up on nearly every site. None of them can be fixed by startups.b alone:

1. **The source numbers in the body text** (raised on all five sites, by three or more personas each). design.b asked to keep them, and they are now spaced and labelled "source N" for screen readers. Readers still see "1413" and "345" as noise. The alternative is the same sources, moved: one small "Sources: 2, 7, 13" line at the end of each section, with no markers inside sentences. **For design.b and Dinis to decide.**
2. **"Choose a design" in a visitor's header** (raised on four sites). Every visitor reads it as a developer tool on a company's brochure. The rule (guidelines 12) is for founder and personal sites, where the owner chooses. On these five, the company is Dinis's own and the choice is his. The chooser could move to the footer and to /designs/, with the header kept for the company. **For Dinis to decide.**
3. **"Nothing is sold yet"** (RiskMandate, AgentiCCells, Sovereign Website). It is the truth, stated on the companies' own sites, and the brochures must keep it. It caps what a buyer persona will score. Only the companies can change this, by selling something.

## Craft fixes still worth making (startups.b)

- **AgentiCCells:**
  - The price ladder's figures wrap mid-number on phones ("£20,0 / 00"), and four of five personas saw it. Use `white-space:nowrap` on the figures and a wider column.
  - "24 min" and "87 s" appear twice (in the band and in "Counted"); keep the band.
  - The "Website cell" titles are real links to agenticcells.ai, so they are right as they are; an "↗" would show that they leave the page.
- **RiskMandate:**
  - The founders are listed twice, and there are two "own team" stat blocks, with 2,262 in both. Merge each into one.
  - In the light phone view the page stays dark almost to the end; check that the light scheme applies below the hero.
- **sgit:**
  - The phone hero crop clips the screenshot's words on both sides ("ta, app, history and sources, shipped"). Crop to the whole headline, or show no screenshot above the fold on phones.
  - The desktop "Key dates" markers 3 to 5 overlap. Give them a minimum gap.
  - The hero mixes straight and curly quotes.
- **The Cyber Boardroom:**
  - The desktop timeline shows 1 to 7 with only "2025" labelled; label each point with its date.
  - Sources 12, 16 and 17 are listed but never cited; cite them or drop them.
  - The quotes mix straight and curly.
  - The phone dark mode body is still not done (startups.b noted it).
- **Sovereign Website:**
  - "Show me one." still does not say what the page is. Make the h1 "Your website. Built by agents, owned by you." and keep "Show me one." as the showroom's heading.
  - The headings mix sans and serif ("Three keys, all yours" is sans); use one.
  - Replace the 🗝 emoji with a drawn key.
- **All five:** the chooser's "You are here" still runs into the description ("drawn largeYou are here"). Use `.pick-cards .now{display:block}`.
