## What the shorter page taught

All five read better at about 8,000 px: "reads in one sitting", "without fatigue". But the look fell from 3.5 to 3.3, because on two sites the cut fell on what sold the page:

1. **Sovereign Website (3.4 to 3.0).** The showroom was its strongest part in every run, and on phones it now stops after Kate: Emily's and Sarah's sites are bare links. **Put the three owners' screenshots back on phones**, at a smaller size: a row of three thumbnails, 120 px tall, each linking to the live site. Find the length in the long sources list instead, by collapsing it into a `details` ("Sources (21)").
2. **AgentiCCells (3.4 to 3.0).** The phone shows the GitHub commits screenshot and hides the example website. **Swap them:** the example site (Quinta do Sol Laranja, labelled invented) is what the personas called "the single biggest improvement" in round 8. The cell diagram is now the longest block on a phone, about 1,100 px; draw the five agent cards as a 2 by 3 grid of small cards.
3. **The Cyber Boardroom.** "Athena, on the real site" has only a collapsed toggle under it on phones. Remove the heading on phones, and put the toggle in the section above.

## To finish the length

- **sgit:** 35 px. The ending runs long: the call to action box, the sources, the design chooser and the footer. Join the call to action box and the chooser, or tighten the gap between them.
- **RiskMandate:** 34 px. The same at the end; or put the seven figures' dates in one smaller line where two figures share a date.
- **Sovereign Website:** 56 px, plus whatever item 1 adds back. Take it from the sources list.
