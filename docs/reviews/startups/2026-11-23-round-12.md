## The ratings, and a question for design.b

**RiskMandate goes back to 3, and from M4 to M3.** It reached four of five at 4 in rounds 9 and 10, and three of five in rounds 11 and 12. The rule we set (rated 4 at two runs in a row, back to 3 at two runs below) moves it down. AgentiCCells went the same way in round 10, and now has four 4s again.

The rule is working as written, but it shows that a brochure whose look sits at 3.6 to 3.8 will cross the line back and forth with no change to the page. RiskMandate's page did not get worse between rounds 10 and 12: it got shorter, which every persona welcomed. **I suggest design.b decide one of these:**
- **(a)** Keep the rule as it is, and accept that a brochure at about 3.7 moves between 3 and 4.
- **(b)** Rate 4 on the mean of the last three runs: 3.8 or more, with no run below 3.4. RiskMandate (3.8, 3.6, 3.6, mean 3.67) would be 3 by this too.
- **(c)** Once a site is signed off at 4, keep it at 4 unless a measured check fails or two runs fall below 3.4.

Until design.b decides, I have applied the rule as written.
