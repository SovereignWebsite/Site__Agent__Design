## To fix, in order

1. **Put each "Sources:" row inside its own section, at the end, on the content gutter.** All five agents found rows in the wrong place:
   - a hero's row at the top of the next (dark) band;
   - a dark band's row flush to the screen edge;
   - on desktop, a row under the wrong column of a two-column section.

   The row must be the last child of the section's inner container, not a grid sibling:
   ```css
   .src-row{grid-column:1/-1;margin:1.25rem 0 0;padding:0;font-size:.875rem;color:var(--muted)}
   .src-row a{display:inline-block;min-width:24px;min-height:24px;padding:2px 4px;text-align:center}
   ```
   The muted colour must stay at 4.5:1 on its own band; on the dark bands use the band's own muted token, not the page's.
2. **Keep the numbers the reader sees equal to the numbers the text cites.** The lists are `<ol>`, so a gap in the ids renumbers what shows:
   - RiskMandate has no source 15, so the item with id 16 shows as "15", while the text cites "16";
   - the same happens on AgentiCCells (no 18, so "19" shows as 18) and Sovereign Website (no 19, so "20" and "21" show as 19 and 20).

   The links resolve, but a reader counting down the list finds the wrong source. Three personas called it a missing source. Fix it with `<li id="g-src-16" value="16">` on each item, or renumber without gaps.
3. **/designs/ on all five still says "the control at the top of every page".** Make it "the links at the foot of every page".
4. **The low-vision reader's 3, on every site:**
   - darken the card body text and the source rows to the body colour at 90 per cent;
   - on phones, the browser-framed screenshots are unreadable. Show a real-size crop of the part that matters (round 2's item 3), with the whole page behind the link.
5. **AgentiCCells:** the hero still carries "Sources: 19", the only hero with a source row left in it. Move it to the end of "What it is".
6. **Per site, from the personas, smaller:**
   - **sgit:** a second large "sgit.ai" wordmark under the header logo on desktop. "Key dates" wants to be a drawn timeline.
   - **The Cyber Boardroom:** visually plain below the hero on phones. The "Director asks, Athena answers" band is the best thing on the page; give it the full width and a second exchange.
   - **RiskMandate:** the lower half is long and plainer than the hero.
   - **Sovereign Website:** "0 to 5" (the maturity check) is never explained on the home page; add one line.
