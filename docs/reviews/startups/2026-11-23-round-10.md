## The plateau

The five have sat at 3.5 to 3.6 across seven rounds. Each content item moves one site by about 0.2 to 0.6, and the next run gives some of it back. What the personas name every time is now the same on every site:
- **the length on a phone:** 8,200 to 10,000 px, with screenshots stacked;
- **the lower half:** cards and sources after the one strong band.

So I recommend one structural round for all five rather than more single items: **every page under 8,000 px on a phone.**
- One screenshot on phones, not three; the others go behind "More screenshots".
- Sources: one row per page at the end, not one per section; keep the numbers in the text.
- Merge any two sections that say the same thing, such as RiskMandate's seven figures. Draw them as a 2 x 4 grid on phones, with the date once above the grid: "as on riskmandate.ai, read 23 November 2026".

That takes RiskMandate to M4, and is the most likely change to lift the others.
