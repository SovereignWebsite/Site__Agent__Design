Asked by design.b, because Dinis shows the demonstration to one of the market's organisers on Monday 23 November; **the deadline is Sunday 22 November.** Dinis on naming it, as quoted in the Factory's request commit 53fc111: "You can name them publicly, that's fine ... we're only using real data."

## What is right

- Every page carries the demonstration note and links to the official site.
- The content is the market's own, read from its WordPress API (GET only), with a dated snapshot ("20 November 2026, 21:03 UTC").
- The fonts are self-hosted and openly licensed (SIL OFL), which the guidelines allow.
- There are no cookies, no scripts from anyone else, and every page is noindex.
- Nothing scrolls sideways at 390 px.
- The six designs already differ more than skins usually do:
  - Journal reads as a paper;
  - Poster has real poster type;
  - Cellar is image-led;
  - The makers is a filterable directory with a phone tab bar;
  - The data is a query tool.

## To fix, worst first

1. **Contrast (serious), design 3, stallholders, desktop light.** The yellow category counts in the headings (`.band h2 small`, #ffc400 on #f4f1ea) are 1.41:1. In light mode, give them the ink colour on a yellow chip:
   `.band h2 small{color:var(--line);background:var(--hl);-webkit-text-stroke:0;padding:0 .2em}`.
2. **Target size (serious), design 4, stallholders, phone.** The category links in `.toc` are under 24 px:
   `.toc a{display:inline-block;padding:10px 4px;min-height:24px}`.
3. **No h1 (moderate):**
   - design 1 on every page checked;
   - design 2's home;
   - design 5's stallholders.
   Promote the page's first heading. On design 1, keep the copy faithful by making the site name the h1, visually unchanged.
4. **The demonstration strip.** It sits outside any landmark (axe "region", on every page). On phones it takes four lines and about 80 px above every design. Make it a landmark and one line:
   - markup: `<aside class="demo-strip" aria-label="About this demonstration">`;
   - on phones: "A demonstration, not the official site: chiswickcheesemarket.uk", with the snapshot time inside a `<details>`;
   - CSS: `@media(max-width:40rem){.demo-strip{font-size:13px;padding:6px 12px}.demo-line{flex-basis:auto}}`.
5. **Design 3's poster is cropped on phones.** The Cheesemas poster reads "'S CHEESEMA" and "Y YOUR CHRISTMAS CHEES". A poster must not be cropped:
   `@media(max-width:60rem){.poster-img{aspect-ratio:auto;object-fit:contain;background:#000}}`.
6. **Design 4's phone hero.** The kicker and "Next market" sit over a busy photo and are hard to read; axe cannot measure text over an image. On phones, put the text below the photo:
   `@media(max-width:40rem){.veil{background:var(--bg);color:var(--fg)}}`, with the photo at `aspect-ratio:4/3` above it. On desktop, strengthen the veil to `rgba(10,8,6,.95)` at the bottom and `.55` at 60 per cent.
7. **Design 1 loads about 4 MB on a phone,** mostly the autoplaying home video. It is the faithful copy, so keep the video. Add `preload="none"` and let the poster image carry the first screen.
8. **Pictures and the video load from chiswickcheesemarket.uk on page load.** That is the market's own host, so it fits a demonstration of their own content, and no copies are made. But each visit tells their host the visitor's address, which guidelines rule 2 exists to avoid. Say so in the demonstration note. For a real site, the pictures would be copied in, with credit.

## Making 2 to 5 more beautiful, and more different

Today the four share the generator, the routes and the section order: hero, about, stallholders, journal. Each should rebuild the page round an idea from the market's own world. These use only what the WordPress data holds.

| # | Now | Make it | What changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | Journal | **The Market Paper** | A broadsheet front page dated by the next market ("Sunday 20 December 2026"), with the newest journal post as the lead story, two columns on desktop and the stallholders as a classified index at the back. Fraunces at a real headline size (`clamp(44px,8vw,104px)`), column rules and a dateline strip. |
| 3 | Poster | **The Poster Wall** | The market's own posters (Cheesemas, and any others in its media library) pasted up as a wall: whole and uncropped, slightly turned (`rotate(-1.5deg)` and `1deg`), with a tape strip in CSS. One giant date, "20 DEC", in Bebas. Black, white and one yellow, as now, with the wall as the hero instead of a split hero. |
| 4 | Cellar | **The Cave** | A maturing cave, dark first: the stallholders as rounds on shelves (one shelf per category, each round a circle cropped from the stallholder's own picture, with a gold label), and the journal's tasting posts as tasting cards. The parchment light mode stays. |
| 5 | The makers | **The Market Map** | The stalls drawn as a plan of rows, one row per category, with the real counts (22 cheese producers and mongers, 18 accompaniments, 3 made with cheese, 2 accessories, 1 hot food, as the data has them today). Each stall opens its maker. It must be labelled "Drawn by category, not the real stall plan". The filter becomes the map's legend, and the phone keeps the list and the tab bar. |

The pass across all four:
- **Motion:** one entrance per design, under 600 ms, off under `prefers-reduced-motion` (the posters settle, the cave's labels fade in).
- **Phones:** design each layout at 390 px first, not as a narrowed desktop. Specifically:
  - the Paper goes to one column with the lead image full width;
  - the Wall goes to a vertical stack of whole posters;
  - the Cave goes to one shelf that scrolls sideways, inside its own focusable region;
  - the Map goes to its list.

**The owner's world.** Buttercup the cow and the market's own posters are the strongest pictures it has; use them. Sarah Cruz's public site shows one Chiswick piece, The Chiswick Timeline, a community mural ("a history in art and maps, under a railway bridge"; Abundance London, 2010 to 2020). I found no work of hers for the cheese market itself, so the site must not claim her hand in it. A timeline strip like the mural's would suit the market's story page in design 2.

## The front door

- Lead with the next market, large: "Next market: Sunday 20 December 2026, 9.30 am to 3.00 pm, Old Market Place". That is what the organiser will check first.
- Name each design card by its idea (The Market Paper, The Poster Wall, The Cave, The Market Map), each with a one-line promise.
- Keep the photo hero, but at `min-height:40vh` on phones, so the cards start within the first screen.
