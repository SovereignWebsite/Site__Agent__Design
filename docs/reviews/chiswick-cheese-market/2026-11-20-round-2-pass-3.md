The critic also says the four are "genuinely different ideas, not skins of one layout", which was the aim of these passes. The Cave is now a real concept, but all five still call it the weakest: one maker per course, and dark, small text.

## The last changes before Monday, in order

1. **Design 1's banner (AA):** stop it under `prefers-reduced-motion`, and pause it on hover and focus. Add a visible "Pause" control: a checkbox styled as a button, with `.marquee:has(input:checked) .track{animation-play-state:paused}`, so no script is needed.
2. **The front door:**
   - The yellow "Next market" box overlaps the "A demonstration for the organisers" line. Give the box its own row, with `margin-bottom:12px` and no negative margin.
   - Put the cards in number order, 1 to 6, so the numbers match the designs. Mark design 3 "Start here", as the personas now favour it; or drop "Start here" and let Dinis lead.
   - The front door's own demonstration strip is still four lines on a phone; make it one line, as on the designs.
3. **The Cave, made fuller rather than redone:**
   - Under each course's featured maker, list every maker of that kind as menu items: name, a middle dot, and the first few words of their own description, in two columns on desktop.
   - Body text must be `--fg` (#eadfcd), not the soft beige.
   - Past dates must not be italic and struck through; write "(past)" and dim them to no lower than 4.5:1.
   That turns "one item per course" into a menu, and keeps the idea.
4. **The Poster Wall on phones:** about 2 MB. Load the posters below the first one at the `medium` size (300 px) with `loading="lazy"`, and keep `medium_large` for the first poster only.
5. **For Dinis, not the site:** the organiser persona asked who would update the site and what it would cost. The demonstration should not invent either. It is for Dinis to answer in the meeting. One line on the door can say so: "What it would cost and who keeps it up to date: to talk about."
