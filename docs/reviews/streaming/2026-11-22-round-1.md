## Changes, ranked (worst first)

### 1. /agents/: wrap the prompt blocks (hard rule, blocks level 1)

In `site/assets/streaming.css` (or wherever /agents/ takes its styles):

```css
pre{white-space:pre-wrap;overflow-wrap:anywhere;max-width:100%}
```

`site/policy/policy.css` already does `white-space:pre-wrap`; /agents/ needs the same. Check at 320 px.

### 2. No sideways scroll at 320 px on home, /pt/ and /policy/

```css
.row>*{min-width:0;max-width:100%}
.row label,main li,main p{overflow-wrap:anywhere}
input[type=text],input[type=url],input[type=search]{width:100%;box-sizing:border-box}
```

and shorten the label to "Link to the PrompTugal view". The hint about the address can go in the input's `placeholder` or a line under it.

### 3. Follow the visitor's dark setting

Today `design.js` sets `data-design` only when a design was chosen, so `:root` falls back to Paper. Give the default a dark pair:

```css
@media (prefers-color-scheme:dark){
  :root:not([data-design]){--bg:#141513;--fg:#ecebe6;--muted:#a8a69e;--accent:#7fc3a0;--line:#2c2d29;--card:#1c1d1a}
}
```

and in `app.js` leave `data-design` unset until the visitor picks one (do not write "paper" back when nothing is stored). Each of the five designs should then have its own light and dark (see change 6), not one fixed scheme each.

### 4. Make the chooser a visible, labelled control

- Show the label: `<label for="design">Design</label>` (not `.sr`), so the header reads "Design: Paper".
- Better, as guidelines 12 asks: a "Choose a design" button that opens five cards, each a real screenshot of that design with its name and its one-line idea, the current one outlined.
- No-script fallback: render the control only from script (or in a `<noscript>`, say "Designs need JavaScript"), so a visitor without script never meets a control that does nothing.

### 5. Put the main action on the first phone screen

At 390 x 844 the "Add a title" input sits at the very bottom edge, below two paragraphs and an eyebrow that is the site's own address.

- Replace the eyebrow `streaming.tool.sovereignweb.site` with a few words of what this is, or drop it.
- Keep one lead sentence above the form ("Your own watchlist. It stays in this browser."). Move the "no catalogue data" paragraph under the form as its hint, with the PrompTugal link as a clear secondary button: "Find something to watch on PrompTugal".
- Make the empty state teach: instead of "Nothing here yet.", show what an entry will look like and the two ways to add one (paste a link, or type a title).

### 6. Five designs that are five ideas from the owner's world (blocks level 4)

Concept first, as guidelines 12 asks: write `docs/designs.md`, one section per design, with its philosophy, its visual narrative (first, second, third thing seen) and its pipeline (which of the owner's own facts it draws). The facts here are the owner's list: titles, status (to watch, seen, hidden), ratings, notes, services, countries, saved views. Nothing else may be drawn. Proposals only, for streaming.d to choose from or replace:

1. **The shelf.** The list as spines on a video-shop shelf, one shelf per status. Rating as a sticker on the spine, a note as a slip tucked inside. The main action is "put one on the shelf".
2. **The ticket stubs.** Each title a torn cinema ticket: title, the service it is on, the date added. Seen titles get a punched hole. Dark first.
3. **The TV guide.** A listings grid, one column per favourite service, the to-watch titles in it. The owner's saved views become the guide's channels.
4. **Two countries** (way out there). The page split down the middle, United Kingdom and Portugal, for an owner who watches in both. Each title sits where its service is, a line across the middle for those in both. The language switch lives on the seam.
5. **The queue** (way out there). Tonight's one title, huge, full screen, with a single "Seen it" button. The rest of the list waits behind it as a deck of cards. A design for deciding, not managing.

Each must keep the same honesty (no catalogue data, everything stays here), work without script at a basic level, and meet every rule light and dark.

### 7. Smaller things

- **The gap above each card's heading** (all five personas): `h2` has `margin:2rem 0 .5rem`, and inside a `.panel` with `padding:1rem` that makes 3rem of empty card. Add `.panel>h2:first-child{margin-top:0}`.
- **The file input** is the browser's own "Choose File" button (four personas). Hide the input visually and style its label as a button: `<label class="button" for="import">Import a JSON file</label>`, keeping the input focusable.

- **Remove is as loud as the primary action.** In each list entry, "Remove" is an outlined accent button between the controls and the note. Make it a quiet text button at the end of the row, and keep it away from the status select.
- **Preferences do nothing yet.** Favourite services, countries and genres are saved but nothing uses them. Say what they will do, or fold them into a `<details>` until saved views or a design use them.
- **The share card:** make Streaming's own with `build/share-card.mjs` in SovereignWebsite__Factory (the seed's own build warns when the neutral card is still in place).
