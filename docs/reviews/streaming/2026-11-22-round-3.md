## Changes, ranked

### 1. Let each design own its first screen (blocks level 4)

Keep the shared parts (name, Design, language, the add action, the tabs) but let each design place and shape them around its idea. Proposals, for streaming.d to choose or replace:

- **The shelf:** the covers are the first screen. "Add a title" becomes the first cover on the shelf, a blank one with a "+", which opens the box; the lead line moves under the shelf. On a phone, two covers and the blank one in the first view.
- **The queue:** "Up next" is the first thing, full width, big. Tabs and search move behind a small "All titles" link; "Add to the queue" sits under the running order.
- **Index cards:** the add box is itself a blank ruled card at the top of the box, in the same serif, "Write a title"; the tabs become coloured card tabs along its top edge.
- **Ticket stubs:** the figures (seen, to watch, average rating) are the hero, set large, with the 12-month chart under them; the add action is a small "New ticket" button.
- **The list:** as today. It is the plain, fast one, and that is its idea.

At least two of these should feel way out there on the first screen (the queue and the stubs are the natural pair).

### 2. Use the width on a desktop

At 1440 px every design sits in a column of about 700 px, so the shelf shows two small covers and a lot of empty page. docs/designs.md says "up to six on a wide screen". Let The shelf and Ticket stubs widen (for example `max-width:min(1200px,92vw)` for their list area) while the reading text keeps its measure.

### 3. A richer example list

The live example is two titles to watch and two seen, which is too few to show a shelf, a running order or a chart. The chooser's own screenshots use more (five, six and one). Use the same set for "Show an example list", all made up and labelled, so a visitor sees what each design is for.

### 4. Smaller

- **Second-layer controls read as switched off** (three personas). They pass contrast, but outline-only buttons in a muted colour look disabled. Give Hide, Note and tags and Move up or down a filled `--card` background with `--fg` text, and keep the muted style only for what is really unavailable.
- **The chooser on a phone:** make sure the sheet scrolls inside itself and the last card's caption and button can be reached (`max-height:90dvh;overflow:auto` on the dialog), and that the page behind does not show through.
- **Counts beside the tabs:** "Seen 2" next to two titles to watch reads as a mismatch. Make the selected tab and its count unmistakable, or show "2 to watch" as the list's own heading.

- **The share card** is still the seed's. Make Streaming's own, perhaps from The shelf's covers (no text in the image).
- **Remove** still sits on the title line in The list and the queue's running order.
- **The Index cards screenshot** in the chooser shows "Added 23 November 2026", a day after it was made. A made-up date in the future reads as a bug; use one in the past.

## Recommendation for the home design

**The shelf**, as streaming.d chose, once it owns its first screen. Its covers are the most immediate idea and work for a first visit. The queue is the strongest second, and the right one for an owner with a long list.
