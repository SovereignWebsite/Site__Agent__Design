## Changes, ranked (worst first)

### 1. Give each title button a 24 px target (blocks level 2)

```css
.item-title{display:inline-block;min-height:1.5rem;padding:.125rem 0;line-height:1.3}
```

Then check that nothing in the row overlaps it (the Remove link on the same line, the stars below): keep at least 4 px between them, or `margin-bottom:.25rem` on the title. Run axe with the example list loaded, since an empty list hides this.

### 2. Five designs that are five ideas (blocks level 4)

As in round 1, change 6: write `docs/designs.md` first (philosophy, visual narrative, pipeline per design), drawing only the owner's own list. The redesign gives you more to draw: tags, "Pick something for tonight", the per-title record. Round 1's proposals still fit, and two now come almost for free:

- **The queue** is "Pick something for tonight" made into the whole page: one title, huge, and a single "Seen it".
- **The record** (new): each title as a filled-in index card, the JSON record shown as the card's typed fields, the list as a card drawer.

Keep today's layout as one of the five (call it what it is, for example "The list"). Make at least two way out there, each with its own scheme light and dark.

### 3. Move "Choose a design" up, and show the five

The control works and is labelled, but it sits at the foot of the page, after the folds, where a visitor will not find it. Put one small "Design" control in the header (beside "Português"), opening a panel of five cards, each a real screenshot with its name and one-line idea. Keep the footer select as the fallback. Do this when change 2 lands, so the cards show five different pages.

### 4. Smaller

- **Paper and Night are the same as Automatic** in light and dark. Once the five designs are rebuilt, Automatic should mean "the home design, light or dark", not a sixth entry.
- **Stars:** the outline stars read as faint to four personas. Give empty stars `--fg` at full strength, or a filled `--line` star, and keep the 3:1 non-text contrast.
- **The phone header:** "Start here", "Use with your AI" and "Português" wrap onto a loose second line under "Streaming". Put them in one row with the name, or behind a small menu.

- **The help paragraph under Add** is five lines on a phone. Keep the first sentence and the PrompTugal link; move "paste a link anywhere on this page, or press / to start typing" to /start/ or a tip shown once.
- **The share card:** still the seed's. Make Streaming's own.
- **"Remove" beside the title** is quiet now, but it sits on the same line as the title button, and an accidental tap removes a title. The Undo toast helps; consider moving Remove into "Note and tags".

## Recommendation for the home design

Automatic is right as the default for now: it follows the device, both its halves read well, and three of five personas chose it. After change 2, lead with The queue, as in round 1.
