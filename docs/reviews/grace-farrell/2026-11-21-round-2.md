## Fix, worst first

1. **The home page and design 2 scroll sideways on a phone** by 11 px at 390 and 331 px wide. Each tilted section (`.credits`, `.reels-sec`, `.tour`, `.about`, `.skills`, `.contact`) pokes past the edge when rotated. Fix with `html,body{overflow-x:clip}`, or keep each section's rotation inside a padded wrapper (`margin-inline:12px`).
2. **Reflow at 320 px (WCAG 1.4.10):** the header's last link ("Contact") runs off the screen. Let the section links wrap (`flex-wrap:wrap`), or move them into a menu below 360 px.
3. **"FARREL / L" on desktop, home page:** the h1 is 108 px in a 616 px column, so "FARRELL" cannot fit and breaks. Use `font-size:clamp(48px,7.5vw,104px)` and `white-space:nowrap` on each name `span`, so a word never splits.
4. **The kicker repeats its heading** ("Shows" over "Shows", "Can do" over "Can do") on the home page and in design 3. It is read once, but seen twice. Keep the kicker for the act or number ("No. 2", "Act I") and the heading for the word, or drop one.
5. **Small:**
   - Libretto's headings end in "❦", which a screen reader reads aloud. Wrap it as `<span aria-hidden="true">❦</span>`.
   - Three stills share the alt text "Still from Grace's acting showreel", and the casting director could not tell which woman is her. Make each alt say what it shows, or mark the extra stills as decorative (`alt=""`).
   - Design 5's kicker crosses her eye on phones; move it below the eyes (`top:62%`).
6. **The lower half is still one set of parts in five colours.** The critic: "the reels, map and skills blocks are the same parts recoloured." The heroes now differ; carry each idea down the page:
   - **Fringe flyer:** the back of the flyer. Credits in small print, the reels as "tonight's acts", the tour as a list of venues printed along the flyer's edge, and the tear-off tabs last.
   - **Footlights:** acts and a curtain for the reels, as now. Skills as the programme's "Biography" page, set in one column under a stage light.
   - **Libretto:** the tour as "Performances" in a libretto's back matter (venue, then dates). Skills as "Instrumentation and languages", the way a score's front page lists them.
   - **Tour diary:** this is the weakest for three of five. Make the map the hero and the card the second screen. On desktop, fill the empty half beside the card with the tour list. Drop the passport line ("P<GBRFARRELL<<GRACE…"): it imitates an identity document, and a screen reader reads it as noise.
   - **Editorial:** the favourite. Credits as "Selected credits", newest first (as now). The reels as a two-page spread, and skills as a short sidebar.
