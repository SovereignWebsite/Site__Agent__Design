# Synthetic users

design.b@SovereignWebsite.dinis, first draft, 20 November 2026. A check of how a site looks to the people it is for, run on the home page each review round, as a second opinion next to the measured checks. They are simulations: they inform a rating, they never replace the measured checks or a real person.

## How it runs

One subagent per persona is given only what a visitor would get: the first-screen phone screenshot (390 x 844), the full-page phone and desktop screenshots, light and dark, and the page's visible text. It is not given the guidelines or my own findings, so it is not anchored on them. Each answers the same questions, in at most 120 words:

1. In one sentence, what is this site and who is it for?
2. What would you do next, and could you find how?
3. What made you trust it, or not?
4. What looked unfinished, confusing or hard to read?
5. The look, from 1 (amateur) to 5 (I would show it to a friend), and the one change that would raise it most.

## Personas

| Persona | Stands for |
|---|---|
| The phone visitor in a hurry | Anyone arriving from a link on a phone, with ten seconds to decide. |
| The small business owner | A florist, bakery or surf school deciding whether to buy a sovereign website. |
| The low-vision reader | Someone at 200 per cent zoom, or using high contrast and a screen reader. |
| The design critic | A professional designer judging the craft: type, spacing, colour, rhythm. |
| The site's own audience | Set per site: for an artist, a casting director; for a CV, a recruiter; for a company, a prospective client; for an organisation, a member; for an investor site, a founder. |

## Scoring

The synthetic-user score is the mean of the five look scores. Answers that all five personas agree on go into the next round's changes; one persona's complaint is weighed against the measured checks.

**Spread.** A fresh set of simulated readers moves a page by up to about half a point with nothing changed. So a site is rated 4 only when four of five personas give 4 in two runs in a row (from 23 November 2026).

## The hub: "Would you want one?"

From hub round 8 (23 November 2026), the hub is tested against one question, set by design.b in [the brief](https://github.com/SovereignWebsite/Site__Agents__Design/blob/dev/docs/briefs/hub-sells.md) from Dinis's words: "I need to be able to show this site to somebody and they say 'I want one'."

The five personas are different for this test:
- a self-employed professional with no website;
- a small-business owner who rents a site from a website builder today;
- a creative who wants a portfolio;
- a low-vision reader;
- a sceptical friend.

For each design, each persona gives about thirty seconds, then answers "Would you want one? If not, why not?": yes or no first, then the reason.

The run records:
- the yes count per design;
- every "why not" answer verbatim, grouped, and ranked by how often it came up. That ranked list is hub.b's next list.

A design is rated 4 only when four of the five say yes in two runs in a row.
