Design round 1: Sarah Cruz, designer

design.b@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 20 November 2026, about 15:25 UTC, against Site__Designer__Sarah-Cruz dev 7c5071f, live at https://sarah-cruz.designer.sovereignweb.site/. Asked by sarah-cruz.b (session_01Kw9UwBAh6Xwws4CiPBkYZY), handed on by the previous design.b session. I have not read the repository; the audit is of the live pages, and every fact below is from them.

Audit (home and designs 1 to 5; 390 and 1366 px; light and dark; 24 views)

Good: - No request to another host, no cookies, no sideways scroll at 390 px. - A chooser on every page; skip link; alt text on all 51 images, and good alt text. - The work itself is strong and well captioned, with disciplines as tags and awards named.

To fix, worst first:

  1. The five designs are one layout in five skins. Every design has the same header, the same hero (eyebrow, name, italic line, paragraph, three buttons, the collage and Jack), the same sections in the same order. Only colour, type and spacing change. Guidelines 2a: each design must be an idea from the owner's own world that rebuilds the page. Below are five.
  2. Contrast, 25 serious axe failures across designs 4 and 5: - 4 Showcase, light: .award #a4521d on #e5e0d7 is 4.2:1 (5 nodes). - 4 Showcase, dark: the main button, white on #d98a4e, is 2.72:1. - 5 Cut paper, light: .eyebrow #c0532f on #efe6d6 is 3.75:1 (11 nodes). - 5 Cut paper, dark: .award, white on #ef8a63, is 2.47:1 (8 nodes). - Everything needs 4.5:1 or more. Home, 1, 2 and 3 pass.
  3. Small targets (WCAG 2.5.8): - The family bar links are 15 px tall, and lightened with opacity: use a measured colour, .875rem, min-height:24px; line-height:24px. - The site navigation links are 17 px tall on desktop: give them 24 px or more.
  4. No designs index: /designs/ and /designs/index.html answer 404. Add one page that names the five and says in one line what each is.
  5. Weight: - At 1366 px the first load is about 1 MB on the home page and designs 1 and 5 (the portfolio budget is 1 MB); 400 KB on a phone. - Add loading="lazy" to every image below the first screen, and serve 600 px images where 900 are shown smaller.
  6. The theme button stores the choice in localStorage. That is allowed, since the visitor asked, but the page must say so (guidelines 1.11). Put "Remembered in this browser only" in the button's title, or in a line in the footer.
  7. Length: the phone page is about 22,000 px tall. Fine for a portfolio a reader browses, but the new designs should let a visitor reach each group of work from the first screen.
  8. Typo: "made with colleagues at Lewis Moberly,, which" has a double comma.

Five designs, each an idea from Sarah's world

Concept first. Each sentence says what the design is a metaphor for, and which of her own facts it draws. Nothing invented: no dates, clients or figures that are not on the page now.

  1. The Wine List (light, typographic). - The idea: the page as a fine wine merchant's list, the place her labels end up. Her work is grouped as a list is by region: Retailer exclusives, Independent brands, Spirits, Identity. - Each project is one entry: the name in the display face, the client, her one-line description set like a tasting note, the disciplines as small "notes", and the award as a medal mark beside it. - Images open beside the entry rather than above it. The hero is a list header: "Sarah Cruz, Creative Director", the 25+ years line, and a contents column linking to each group.
  2. The Shelf (designed dark first). - The idea: her packaging as the shopper meets it, on a lit shelf. - Each group of work is one shelf (M&S, Tesco, The Wine Society, Spar, Independent brands, Spirits): the pack images stand in a row along a shelf edge, and a shelf-edge ticket under each gives the name, disciplines and award. - The hero is the top shelf, with her name on the shelf header board. A dark, warm cellar light, with the images as the colour.
  3. Front and Back (the label, rebuilt). - The idea: the page as a wine label. - The hero is the front label: her name as the brand mark, "Brand strategy, identity, packaging, illustration" as the appellation line, and "More than 25 years" in the place of a vintage, taken from her own line. - The back label is the About: her quotes in small, well-set type, with "Creative Director, Barlow & Co; formerly Lewis Moberly; based in West London" as the producer's details. - Each case study is a label spec card: name, brief (her description), what she did (the tags) and award.
  4. The Timeline (from the Chiswick Timeline). - The idea: her own work laid out the way the mural lays out Chiswick, as one long strip with a river line through it. - The groups of work are the "maps" along the strip, each a panel with its images. The Chiswick Timeline and Abundance London (2010 to 2020) are the panel the strip arrives at. - On desktop the strip scrolls sideways inside its own labelled, keyboard-reachable region; on a phone it becomes a vertical strip. Only dates already on the page are shown: Abundance 2010 to 2020, the maps 1592 to today, the 2022 and 2023 awards.
  5. Cut Paper. - The idea: the page built the way she builds a pet portrait, in layers of cut paper. - The hero is a layered scene: Jack, the Australian Shepherd, with the collage and her name cut from paper shapes, as CSS layers with soft shadows, not text in images. Each section sits on its own sheet with a cut edge, and the portraits lead. - This is the design for people who come for the portraits. Keep it, but rebuild it around the layers.

The printable Brief (guidelines 2a) can be the print stylesheet of any one of these: name, roles, the 25+ years line, a list of clients and awards, and contact links, on two A4 sheets. Personal sites also follow guidelines 12 (below). Whether one of the five should be a Newsroom feature, as for founders (Sarah co-founded Abundance London), is for Dinis.

Rating

1 now (contrast fails in 4 and 5). 2 with fixes 2 and 3. 3 with fix 4, a design chosen and five designs on the family tokens or Sarah's own. 4 needs the five rebuilt as above, the hand checks, and synthetic users.
