Grace Farrell, round 6 (closing): 3.4 and a first "wow"; rated 3; the rounds stop here

Measured

  • axe: no violations in 40 views.
  • Layout: no sideways scroll at 320, 390 or 1366 px.
  • "FARRELL": on one line.
  • Each hero is now its own composition:
  • Editorial: a magazine cover, with the name over the top edge of the face and cover lines over the bottom.
  • Footlights: a proscenium, with curtains, a valance, the name centre stage and a programme photo to one side.
  • Fringe flyer: one A5 flyer on a wall.

Synthetic users: 3.4 (4, 4, 3, 3, 3), the best of six rounds (2.4, 2.8, 2.8, 3.2, 3.2, 3.4). The best design votes were 5, 5, 3, 5, 4. The phone visitor gave the site's first "wow" ("a small 'wow' for the home photo"). The casting director gave 4.

Rating

3. Clean, honest and current, with five designs that differ in their first screens and below them, and Editorial as the home.

Last notes, if grace.b has time; none blocks anything

  1. "(played)" is CSS only (.past::after), so not every screen reader hears it. "(this week)" is now real text. Do the same for "(played)".
  2. The serif headings fall back to Times on some systems (designs 1, 3 and 4). The critic read it as "a font that failed to load". Name the stack: "Iowan Old Style", Charter, Georgia, "Times New Roman", serif.
  3. Editorial, desktop: the hairline name crosses her eyes, and the buttons fall below the first screen. Move the name up by about 6 per cent, and the button bar into the cover's bottom edge.
  4. The lone "Call" and "Email" links: give each an accessible name ("Call her agent", "Email her agent"), and keep the visible words short.
  5. The tour on non-Tour designs: the hero's "On tour" line could link to the "(this week)" venue, href="#tour", as three personas asked.

Why stop

The look has risen in each structural round and held in the others. The remaining complaints are mostly not craft: - "Choose a design" in her header (guidelines 12 requires it: for Dinis and Grace); - that a cover cannot say which nights she plays (the site is right to say so); - a sung reel, which only Grace can add.

The designs, the checks and the content are done. design.b.rounds will check again if Grace or Dinis asks for a change.
