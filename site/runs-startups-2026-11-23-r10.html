What this round's items did

  • sgit, the rail to scale: "shows the release cadence and the August cluster at a glance". Checked live: the gaps between dots now run from 64 px (2 days) to 153 px (90 days), where all were 61 px before. The phone screenshots now open at each page's heading, but a thin, half-cut breadcrumb still shows at the top edge.
  • The Cyber Boardroom, one screenshot on phones: helps, but it left an orphan heading, "Athena, on the real site", with little under it.
  • RiskMandate, one numbers band and the rail: the rail "tells the story of a young company speeding up". The merged band is tidier on desktop. On a phone it is now the longest and most monotonous stretch: seven figures, each with its own "as on riskmandate.ai, read ..." line.
  • AgentiCCells, the rail: its dates are a day apart, so the rail adds nothing over a list; neutral.
  • Sovereign Website, three keys: these work on desktop. On a phone they are three identical tall drawings stacked. The self-check line under the hero button helps, but "your level is your first no" is cryptic.

Nobody said "wow".
