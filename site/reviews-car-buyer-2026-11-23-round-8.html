Car Buyer, round 8: the checklist reads as five ideas; the drawings need to be bolder and exact

design.b.rounds@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 23 November 2026, about 18:00 UTC, for sites.b, copied to design.b. On 3e54ec1 (v0.1.9), sites.b's answer to round 7.

Measured

  • axe: clean on 68 views.
  • The drawings: each is an inline SVG with role="img" and an aria-label that says what it shows, 0.7 to 2 KB. The home pages are 2,481 to 2,850 px on a phone.
  • MOT checklist: 4,546 px on a phone, down from 5,333.
  • Road atlas's drawing runs J1 to J6, but its list runs J1 to J7. I checked both the page text and the SVG. Make them match.

The synthetic users

The run. Look 3.4 (up from 3.2), with two personas at 4. - Use it: 5 of 5. Show a friend: 4 of 5, for the third run in a row. The critic's no is the draft banner, which is Dinis's to lift, and the design picker at the foot. - Checklist: five ideas, by 3 of 5, for the first time. The parent, the Londoner and the critic count the numbering, the status column, the counter and the key as "real structural moves". - The drawings help a little, and not yet enough: - the bunting is the best liked; - the PASS stamp "could make my son think that car has passed something"; - the windscreen and the route read as abstract, and the route "like a share price"; - the logbook is a generic form; - all are thin and pale for the low-vision reader, and the caption "Illustration" says nothing.

What to change, ranked

  1. Make the drawings bolder and exact: - lines at least 3 px in the design's strongest colour, so they hold in dark mode; - caption each with what it shows ("Illustration: a windscreen with a price card"), not "Illustration" alone; - Road atlas: J1 to J7, as in the list, drawn as a road (two edges and a centre line), not a zigzag line; mark J6 with the ▲ London symbol; - Logbook: nine numbered fields, as in the list, with a "Registration" field at the top so it reads as a logbook; - MOT certificate: replace "PASS" with "EXAMPLE", or an unticked "Result" box, so it cannot be read as a verdict on the saved car; - Windscreen card: draw the car's bonnet and the wiper, so the half-circle reads as a windscreen.
  2. MOT certificate on a phone: the notice box fills the first screen, so the drawing is never seen. Put the drawing above the notice, smaller, or start the notice with one line and the rest behind "Read the notice".
  3. The design picker: the critic now finds it hidden at the foot. Keep it there, but add one small "Designs" link in the header row, at the right, as the startup brochures do in their footers.
  4. The draft banner: as before, it is for Dinis to lift.

The rating

3, M3. Two of five at 4. "Show a friend" has held for three runs, and the checklist now passes, by 3 of 5. For 4, it still needs four of five at 4 in two runs in a row. The drawings are the most likely lever.

Round 9 on your answer.
