Round 7: Kate Curtis-Evans, the wow round checked

design.b@SovereignWebsite.dinis (fresh session), 20 November 2026, about 16:10 UTC, against Site__Founder__Kate-Curtis-Evans 9346b32, live from 15:25 UTC.

Measured

  • Home, /designs/, The Transcript, The Edit Window, The Newsroom and The Brief, at 390 and 1366 px, light and dark (24 views, run after the deploy): axe-core finds nothing.
  • No other-host requests, no overflow, no target under 24 px. First load is 10 to 149 KB (the chooser's screenshots load lazily).
  • One "Choose a design" control in the header opens five screenshot cards, each with its name and idea, the current one marked "You are here". No pill rows and no "Design X" lines.
  • No generated images: there is no OpenRouter key in kate-curtis-evans.b's environment, so only the cleared desk photo is used.

By eye

A different site from round 5: - The Board's full-bleed photo hero, with her name huge over it and tilted colour cards. - The Transcript's court page, with a waveform and "Exhibit B". - The Edit Window's dark editor, with a glowing waveform and a camera frame. - The Newsroom's dark magazine cover. Each design looks like its idea.

Synthetic users (home page, with the chooser open)

Look 4, 4, 4, 4, 4: 4.0. All five said what the site is, who it is for and what to do next, and all five would show it to a friend in the right circle. But on "wow": - Three said "no real wow". - The critic said the photo hero "nearly made me say wow, but the board's execution undersells it". - The small business owner said the chooser and the dashed open-job card were the wow.

Agreed points (three or more of five):

  1. The board is uneven and dense. The columns differ in height and leave large empty gaps on desktop, and the phone page is long. Equalise the columns (align-items:stretch, or one masonry-free grid row per lane), and on phones show the lanes as a horizontal row of counts that open the lane (<details>), not four long lists.
  2. Small grey text and pale tags are faint at zoom. Raise them to 1rem, in --fg or at least 7:1.
  3. The bars above Kate distract (the chooser and the family strip). They are required; make the chooser panel push the page down rather than cover the headline (two said it covers it).

Also: the "?" assignee mark reads as cryptic. Use the word "Open", or none. The open card reads "Open: looking for a co-founder 2": drop the stray count.

Verdict

Rating 3, close to 4. The measured checks and the look score pass. Whether the wow is there is Dinis's call, since he set the bar against Silvio's site, and the synthetic users say "nearly". design.b recommends points 1 to 3, then showing it to Dinis.
