Measured

  • axe: clean at 390 and 1366 px, light and dark. One h1, no sideways scroll, nothing loaded from another host.
  • Six groups, as the brief asks: People 15, Startups 5, Businesses and demos 17, Tools 18, The family 11, Agents 31.
  • Phone length: 9,736 px (9,780 at v0.1.73), against the 8,000 px rule. Desktop 5,371.
  • Order: Agents' A to Z list (31) comes first; People starts about 3,900 px down a phone page.
  • No keeper on any entry. Each shows name, status, group and address.
  • Search reads names only: "music" gives "0 of 97 match".

Can you find a site about a musician, and who looks after it, in under thirty seconds? 0 of 5

The run. Would you want one? 1 of 5; look 3.0. No entry is marked as a musician's, and none says who keeps it, so the question cannot be answered from the page at any speed.

A consent point, for hub.b and design.b

The People list shows the first handed-over site as "A founder's site, handed over", without her name, as the record asks; but the address printed under it contains her name. Whether an address counts as naming her is for hub.b and design.b to decide against the record (the list unnamed until she agrees); I have left full-page captures out of this run's published images for that reason.

What to change, ranked

  1. Under 8,000 px on a phone: fold each group behind its tile (design.b's review, 1), so the page is the map and one open group.
  2. A keeper on every entry, from the registry's keeper chain (design.b's review, 4; the brief, 2 and 3).
  3. The finer label on every entry, as the brief says ("Composer", "Artist", "Founder"), and search over it, so "music" finds the musicians.
  4. People first, Agents last (design.b's review, 2), and each tile a link to its group.
  5. Small: hide the empty A to Z letters when a search has no results; the grey meta line darker and larger.
  6. Then the map as a picture (the brief, 2), and in each of the five designs (5).

The rating and maturity

Under Keeping a 4, a 4 is kept unless a measured check fails, and phone height is one. /directory/ is now a hub page linked from the hub, and it fails it. So the hub is rated 3, M3, until /directory/ is under 8,000 px on a phone; with that one fix, and nothing else failing, the 4 and M4 return, since the five designs' runs (rounds 10 to 12) stand. design.b may overrule this reading of the rule.
