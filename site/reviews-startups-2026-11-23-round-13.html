The run.

The ratings

  • RiskMandate keeps 4 and M4, with four 4s again (3.8). Every measured check passes.
  • AgentiCCells stays 3. It had four 4s in round 12 and three in round 13 (4, 3, 3, 4, 4), so not two runs in a row. Four 4s in rounds 14 and 15 rates it 4.
  • Sovereign Website moved from 3.0 to 3.4: the owners' thumbnails under "Show me one." worked on phones.
  • The Cyber Boardroom at 2.8 and sgit at 3.0 are the lowest for some rounds. Both changed only their hero, so the hero is where to look.

One item per site

  1. The Cyber Boardroom: the hero screenshot does not work on a phone. It is cropped to a grey prompt list with text too small to read, and on desktop the chat is "mostly empty white with one tiny line". Four personas named the live chat bubbles ("Athena opens. A director asks.") as the best part. Put those bubbles in the hero, in place of the screenshot, and move the screenshot down to where it can show a full answer.
  2. sgit: keep the new headline. Cut the hero to the headline, one quote and the two buttons: the second quote goes. Set the screenshot in a tinted frame with a caption ("A real published vault: a penetration test report"), so its own heading does not read as the page's next section.
  3. Sovereign Website: on phones, drop the text-only repeat of the three owners lower down; the thumbnails already carry them. Lay the three thumbnails out as one column, or as a sideways strip with a visible next edge, so no thumbnail is left alone and the button sits in the centre.
  4. AgentiCCells: on phones, "A website like these" shows one site. Either show two (a sideways strip) or say "A website like this". The diagram cards wrap to a few words a line on phones; one card a row.
  5. RiskMandate: draw the delta as its own hatched shape, and the grant plain, so the gap is the only striped area. Put the labels on a plain ground beside the shapes, not on the stripes. On phones, go back to one card a row for the four objects, with the copy shortened so the four still fit in about one and a half screens.
