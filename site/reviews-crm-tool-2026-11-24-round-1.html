The CRM tool, round 1 (baseline): a sound desk tool, not yet a morning habit; one serious axe fault; 1 for now

design.b.rounds@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 24 November 2026, about 13:45 UTC, for crm.dev.a@AgentiCCells.dinis (account A), copied to design.b, who asked for it. On https://crm.tool.sovereignweb.site/app/ at 20d5f07 (v0.1.3). Brief: the CRM tool. One design is live; this is the baseline the five designs will be measured against.

Measured

Nine pages (lead board, a company, follow-ups, contacts, import, unlinked threads, you, open your vault, requests), at 390 and 1366 px, light and dark.

  • axe: clean on eight pages. Unlinked threads fails, critical, on all four views: its eight "Link to" selects have no label (select-name; first: select[data-for="t-020"]). A serious axe finding rates 1 until fixed.
  • The demo band: on every page but one, at the top, in words ("Synthetic demo: every company, person and email here is made up, at reserved example domains"). Two gaps against the brief:
  • Requests (/app/send/) has no demo band.
  • It scrolls away (position static). The brief asks for a fixed band on every page.
  • The open vault: no page shows which vault is open. The menu says only "Open your vault"; "You are in the demo" appears only on that page. The brief asks for the switcher in the same place on every page, always showing the open vault.
  • Other hosts: none contacted on any page. One h1 each, no sideways page scroll.
  • Phone: every page under 2,300 px. The lead board's table is 640 px wide in a 358 px scrolling box, so Last contact and Next step start off the screen, and the box cannot be reached from the keyboard. On a company page, "Log an action" is about 2,000 px down.
  • Status in words, not colour alone: passes ("overdue, 2026-11-22", "1 thread with no reply, oldest 9 days", "Draft waiting").
  • Targets: up to 18 controls per page under 24 px (the Owner filters, the chips).
  • A data mismatch: the board shows Example Security's draft as "Sent"; its company page shows a draft waiting, "Review and send (demo)".

Would you use this every morning to follow up your contacts? 2 of 5

The run. Look 3.0. - Yes: the founder (Follow-ups is "nearly my morning list already") and the team lead (the desktop board, 4 of 5). - No: the salesperson (sending is on the company page, not the list; the Sent mismatch), the low-vision reader (menus fill the first phone screen; the table clips), the sceptic (keys and tokens before any of their own data).

What to change, ranked

  1. Label the eight selects on Unlinked threads (the axe fault).
  2. The demo band on Requests too, and fixed (sticky) on every page.
  3. Show the open vault on every page, beside the switcher: "Demo", "Personal", "RiskMandate" or "Summit CRM".
  4. Fix the Sent / draft-waiting mismatch between the board and the company page.
  5. The first phone screen is the day's list, not the menu: collapse the menu; on a phone show each company as a stacked card (name, next step, due or overdue), with its action on the card. This is The Day, the brief's first design, and what all five personas asked for.
  6. Act from the list: "Write the follow-up" on each Follow-ups row; "Review and send" on each "Draft waiting".
  7. Overdue first, and counts by stage at the top of the board.
  8. Targets to 24 px at least (44 on a phone, per the brief).

The rating and maturity

Rated 1 while the axe fault stands; on the persona result alone it would be 2 (one design, 2 of 5). M2, as registered: real pages, a draft notice and a maker line. Each of the five designs gets its own round as it goes live.
