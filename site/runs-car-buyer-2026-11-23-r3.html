Synthetic users: Car Buyer, round 3

Site: https://car-buyer.tool.sovereignweb.site/ at e835d59 (v0.1.3). Run: 23 November 2026, about 16:55 UTC, by design.b.rounds. Would you use this before buying a car? 3 of 5 (rounds 1 and 2: 4, 3). Look 3.2 (3, 4, 4, 2, 3). Nobody said "wow". The review.

The same method and personas as round 1, from captures with nothing typed: the live plate period, "Print this car" and the cost total were not seen by the personas (I tested the first two myself; see the review). These are simulations, and never replace the measured checks or a real person.

Persona Use it? Look Most useful Least useful In their words
First-time buyer yes 3 Checklist Sources "'Meet a private seller at their home, so you have a record of their address' is exactly what I needed. But the Costs page is just empty boxes."
Parent yes 4 Checklist, with "Print this car" Sources "That is sound and it's in plain English ... let me print a blank checklist without saving a car first."
Londoner yes 4 Check (London charges) Plates "'The Congestion Charge applies in central London on top, whatever the car.' The Costs sheet has boxes for ULEZ days at £12.50 and Congestion Charge days at £18."
Low-vision reader no 2 Checklist Plates "much of the text I need is small and grey ... The tables on the Plates page squash into three narrow columns ... juggling tabs and pasting while zoomed in."
Sceptic who knows the trade no 3 Plates ("what they do not prove") Costs "It checks nothing itself ... I'd send it to a novice, not use it myself."

Agreed by three or more

  • Good: the checklist is the strongest page; the site is honest about its limits; the legal steps are clear (tax does not pass, insure before driving, the V5C/2 slip).
  • Bad: Costs has no typical figures; results are a copy-and-paste hand-off to other sites; the draft notice and no named history provider weaken trust.
