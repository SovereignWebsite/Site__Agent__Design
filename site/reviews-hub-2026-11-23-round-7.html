Hub, round 7: each idea now opens its page; crop to Kate, not her menu

design.b.rounds@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 23 November 2026, about 13:15 UTC, for hub.b, copied to design.b. On https://hub.sovereignweb.site/ at 3e50d13 (v0.1.24), hub.b's answer to round 6.

Measured

  • axe: none, on all five, at 390 and 1366 px, light and dark (20 views). One h1 each, no sideways scroll.
  • Weight, first load: down by half or more on desktop:
  • Contact Sheet: 2.8 MB to 0.9 MB;
  • The Line: 2.1 MB to 0.7 MB;
  • The Street: 1.7 MB to 0.5 MB.
  • On phones, the home page went up, from 0.25 MB to 0.55 MB. Check that its first entry's screenshot takes the 640 px copy, not the full one.
  • /designs/ answers. /start/, /agents/ and /admin/ still answer 404: their commit, 449d7ef, is not deployed yet.

The synthetic users: 3.2 for the home page

The run: 3, 3, 4, 3, 3, as in round 6. The best-design votes were The Line twice, The Register twice and The Gallery once. The critic called The Gallery on desktop "near wow". Every item from round 6 landed, and the personas noticed: the ideas now open the pages, the addresses wrap cleanly, and the Register is shorter and trusted. Rated 3. What holds the look back now is one picture and one sentence.

To fix, in order

  1. Crop Kate's screenshot to her, not her menu. On phones, The Register, The Gallery, The Street and Contact Sheet all show the top of her site: - her name bar; - "Choose a design"; - "Star on GitHub"; - the top of the photograph.

So four first screens open on the same dark strip, and her face shows only on desktop. For the phone srcset, cut a crop of her hero from below her site's header: the photograph, "FOUNDER, AUDIO GENIUS" and her name. In CSS: object-fit:cover;object-position:50% 70% on a 4:3 box. The Line already does this with Silvio's site. 2. Put the one sentence under the headline on every design. "Built by agent teams, then handed over: the owner keeps the files, the history and the address" is below the first phone screen on The Register, The Gallery and The Street. Three personas missed what the hub is. Put it, and the count sentence, directly under the headline, above the first entry. Contact Sheet and The Line already have it there. 3. "Want one?" with nowhere to go. "There is no request form yet: see how it works" is honest, but three personas read it as a dead end. Either: - give a route that exists, if Dinis agrees: an email address, or the operator's contact page; - or say what to do now: "Want one? There is no form yet; read how it works, then write to the operator", linking only to pages that exist.

This is Dinis's call. Do not invent a form. 4. Contact Sheet's red circle still crosses Kate's screenshot, through her name bar. Draw it round the whole frame, including the rebate, so it rings the frame and not the picture. 5. The design names on phones. The phone chooser shows only "Design 1 ▾", so a visitor does not know it is The Register. Show "1 The Register ▾" at 390 px; it fits. 6. The Register's labels went smaller and fainter (Address, Kind, Held), but round 6 asked for them larger. Set them at the body size, in small capitals, in the body colour at 80 per cent. The collapsed entries' "The register entry" needs its disclosure arrow (summary{list-style:revert}, or a ▸ in ::before). 7. Small: - On The Street, the descriptions are grey on cream; darken them to the body colour at 85 per cent. - On desktop, The Street's shopfront sits left, with an empty blue field to its right. Put the next shopfront beside it, so the street reads as a street. - On The Line, step 4, "The owner chooses one", has no site under it. If none is at that stage, say "None at this step today".
