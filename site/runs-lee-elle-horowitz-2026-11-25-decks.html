Synthetic users: Lee-Elle Horowitz's two decks

Decks: https://lee-elle-horowitz.artist.sovereignweb.site/tour/ at 981bc4e: "Your website: a guided tour" (16 slides) and "Behind the scenes: how this site was made" (11), rebuilt from design.b's deck brief, read from their PDFs. Run: 25 November 2026, about 19:30 UTC, by design.b.rounds, asked by design.b. The review.

Would this make you want a site like hers? 3 of 5. Look: the guided tour 4.2, behind the scenes 2.8. Best slide: the guided tour's slide 4 ("Not five colour schemes on one layout: each design starts from a different part of who she is").

The pattern: the guided tour sells; behind the scenes explains to us, not to her. Its file names, agent names and "M3 today" mean nothing to the three non-technical personas.

Noted by the run, not a persona: tour 12 and 13 share a layout (text left, the same row of phones right); behind 5, 7 and 10 are the same bullets-and-box slide; tour 12 and behind 6 hold more words than they can; and claims the slides do not support, among them "21 pages built from JSON" (the slides account for about 16), "every photo is credited" (one source does not name its photographer), "checked by a Hebrew-speaking persona" (synthetic, and the Hebrew is marked unchecked by her), and ownership ("a place you own") with no slide showing how it passes to her.
