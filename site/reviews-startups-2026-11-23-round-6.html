The run. Checked live: each page numbers its sources 1 to n with no gaps. Sovereign Website says "quoted from the plain promises" and explains the "one-minute self-check" from level 0, "rented", to level 5, "sovereign and proven". One h1 per page, no sideways scroll, nothing from another host.

Read the scores with their spread. A fresh set of simulated readers moves a page by up to about half a point with nothing changed. The Cyber Boardroom's rise has a clear cause: real phone screenshots in place of shrunken desktops. So does Sovereign Website's fall: its owners' photographs are cropped away on phones, and "0 to 5" is explained far below the button that says it. The others moved within the spread.

To fix, in order

  1. Small text, everywhere. It is the low-vision reader's 3 on four sites, and the last thing between RiskMandate, sgit and The Cyber Boardroom and a 4. One rule for the shared build: - source digits, diagram sub-labels, stat captions and card text at least 15 px on phones (font-size:max(15px,.9375rem)); - source links with 24 px targets (round 4 asked for this; the digits are still tight).
  2. Sovereign Website: - The phone crops of the owners' sites lose their photographs: Kate's, Emily's and Sarah's sites are the page's strongest proof. Use phone-size screenshots, as The Cyber Boardroom now does. - Put the 0 to 5 explanation next to the button that first says "from 0 to 5": one line under it, "A one-minute self-check: level 0, rented, to level 5, sovereign and proven".
  3. Uncited sources: sgit lists 14, The Cyber Boardroom 12, 16 and 17, and Sovereign Website 11, 12 and 15, and no section cites them. Cite each where its fact is used, or drop it from the list (and renumber).
  4. The desktop screenshots are still unreadable at their size. One caption serves both captures: "Screenshot taken 20 November 2026, phone view 23 November 2026". Under the desktop shot, readers took it for a mislabel. Caption each capture with its own kind and date, for example with <span class="only-phone"> and <span class="only-desktop">.
  5. The Cyber Boardroom: three personas felt "Athena answers" shows no answer, and the phone Athena screenshot ends before any chat. Her real opening line is already on the page ("I'm Athena, your personal AI cyber security advisor ..."). Set it in the band as her reply, in a chat bubble, and crop the screenshot to include the first exchange.
  6. sgit: the hero screenshot repeats the headline word for word and is cut mid-sentence. Show a vault page instead (the published vaults list, or one vault), so the first scroll adds something.
  7. The drawn band for the lower halves (round 5, item 7; startups.b has it next): sgit's three zones as a diagram, The Cyber Boardroom's exchange at full width, RiskMandate's attack bars nearer the top. Three personas on sgit and The Cyber Boardroom again called the lower half "flat".
