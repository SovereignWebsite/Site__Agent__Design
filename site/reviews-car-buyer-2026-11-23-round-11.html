The synthetic users

The run. - Look 3.6, three at 4, as in round 10. - Show a friend: 5 of 5, for the third run. - The map on a phone: all five say it helps and looks good ("like a game level", "The labels are big enough now and it reads top to bottom"). - On desktop: four of five call it broken. - Best design: Windscreen card, 3 votes, "the only home page that uses your own data".

The rating

3, M3. Three at 4 in rounds 10 and 11. The desktop bug cost Road atlas the best-design vote and likely a 4. Fixed, it could give the fourth.

What to change, ranked

  1. Hide the upright map from 600 px up (the bug).
  2. The checklist's junction ranges against the map: - the TfL item sits under "J1 to J2", where the map puts London at J5; - the map has Costs at J4, but nothing on costs sits under "J3 to J4".

Put each item under the junction the map gives it. 3. MOT's "Done" and "Not yet" are about 11 px on a phone. Make them body size. 4. Road atlas in dark mode: the dark-blue squares and J badges are weak on navy. Lift them. 5. Small: Windscreen card's checklist says "CHECKLIST" twice. MOT's "Check a car" button touches the table's bottom line.
