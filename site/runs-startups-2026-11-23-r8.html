Agreed across the sites

  1. The drawn bands help where they are drawn: - sgit's three zones are "the strongest element in both themes and at both widths"; - The Cyber Boardroom's band is the most praised part, but it is text cards, not a drawing; - Sovereign Website has no drawn band in its lower half, and its personas felt that gap most.
  2. AgentiCCells' labelled example site is "the single biggest improvement": it answers "what do I get?" and says three times that its data is invented.
  3. Source numbers still look small on all five, even with the larger tap targets: "comma-cluttered", "like stray punctuation".
  4. Lower halves are long runs of cards (all five).
  5. Nobody said "wow".
