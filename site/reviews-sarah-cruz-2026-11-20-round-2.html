Design round 2: Sarah Cruz, the rebuild

design.b@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 20 November 2026, about 17:05 UTC, against Site__Designer__Sarah-Cruz e615650 and 60b625e, live. The five designs are rebuilt as the ideas in docs/designs.md, and the family strip is replaced by a top-right menu.

By eye

Five different ideas now, not five skins: The Wine List (a merchant's list with a contents column), The Shelf (dark first, packs on lit shelves), Front and Back (one label that turns), The Timeline (dark first, a strip under the bridge with the river running through it), and Cut Paper (layered portraits, her name on torn strips). The chooser shows real screenshots. This meets guidelines 2a.

Measured

Home, /designs/ and 1 to 5, at 390 and 1366 px, light and dark (28 views). No request to another host. First load is 93 to 830 KB, inside the 1 MB budget. To fix, worst first:

  1. Cut Paper (5), phone: sideways scroll. The rotated sheets and the dog cut-out reach 404 px on a 390 px screen. Use overflow-x: clip on the page wrapper, or narrower sheets on phones.
  2. Keyboard access to scrolling regions (axe, serious): - Front and Back (3) on phones: #about. - The Timeline (4) on desktop: #packaging and three more panels. - Give each scrolling region tabindex="0", a label and a visible focus ring.
  3. Front and Back (3): the turn checkbox is 13 px. Hide the box visually and make its "Turn the label" label the 24 px (better 44 px) target.
  4. Guidelines 1.13: /start/, /agents/, /llms.txt and /admin/ all answer 200, but /admin/ is linked only in the footer. Add it to the header's right-hand area too.
  5. Guidelines 1.12 and 1.14 pass: our links are only in the closed top-right menu, and the link preview is complete with a 1200 x 630 image.

Synthetic users

4, 4, 4, 4, 4 (4.0, the run). All five would show it to a friend; the wow was quiet. Agreed points:

  1. Cropped shelf rows. On desktop the rows run off the right edge, with logos cut mid-word ("R LONDON AIRPO"). The sideways scroll is right, but show it: scroll-snap so a pack is never cut mid-logo, an edge fade, and a visible "more" arrow.
  2. Captions and tickets are small and faint. Use 1rem in a darker tint.
  3. Contact is only at the bottom. Add "Contact" to the header.
  4. "Start here" and "Use with your AI" puzzle visitors. Keep them (1.13), but say whom they are for: "For Sarah: start here", "Use this site with your AI".

For more wow (the critic and the brand manager): give the strongest photography room, larger and uncropped.

Rating

3. The five ideas meet the design bar, and the synthetic users give 4.0. Points 1 to 3 (overflow and two serious axe failures) block level 4, and 4 is quick. Sign-off at 4 once they are live.
