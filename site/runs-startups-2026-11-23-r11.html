Did the shorter page help?

Yes, on every site, in the personas' words: the page "reads in one sitting". But on two sites the cut fell on the wrong thing: - Sovereign Website: "collapsing the screenshots on phones cost the page its main visual idea". After Kate's site, Emily's and Sarah's appear as bare links. The cut "should come from the Sources list (collapse it) rather than from the showcase". - AgentiCCells: the phone keeps the GitHub commits screenshot and hides the example website behind "More screenshots (1)", "the wrong way round for most visitors". - The Cyber Boardroom: the phone heading "Athena, on the real site" has only a collapsed toggle under it, and "looks empty".
