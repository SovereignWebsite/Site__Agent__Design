  • The drawings' labels now say what each shows. The route's label reads "A road from J1 ... to J7 ... with J6 marked as London". No drawing contains "PASS".
  • A small "Designs" link ends the header menu.

The synthetic users

The run. - Use it: 5 of 5. Show a friend: 5 of 5, the first time; 4 of 5 in rounds 6 to 8. - Look 3.4, with two at 4, as in round 8. - Home page: five ideas, 5 of 5. - Checklist: five skins, 5 of 5. It was five ideas by 3 of 5 in round 8, with no change to the checklist since, so this verdict is at the noise line.

Where it stands, and the pace

The content, the tools, the five home structures and "show a friend" are done. The look has sat at 3.2 to 3.4 for four rounds (6 to 9) while each round fixed what the last one found. That is the same plateau the startup brochures reached, so design.b's pace applies here too: the next round runs only after a change that could move the look, not after fixes.

Fix these (no round needed)

  1. "PASS" on the MOT checklist labels the user's own tick, which "gives false comfort". Use "Done" and "Not yet" (round 5, item 1). The drawing's caption says "empty result boxes for you to fill in", but they are part of a picture; say "an example certificate with empty result boxes".
  2. Road atlas's order: London (J6) comes after paying (J5). The TfL check belongs before you commit. Mark J1's TfL item with ▲, and drop the separate London junction or move it before J5.
  3. Road atlas's checklist: the item "I have read the MOT history, the recalls and a paid history report" carries the paid diamond, but two of its three are free. Split it into a free item and a paid one.
  4. Road atlas: the home page's J1 to J7 are steps, and the checklist's J1 to J3 are sections. Use one meaning, or letter the sections (A to C).
  5. The Windscreen card drawing: give the price card a price-shaped line ("£ ..." with a short rule) and drop the bar-code look.
  6. The Logbook drawing: its nine fields should match the nine rows below it, and its labels should be larger.

What could move the look: for Dinis and design.b to choose

The personas now say they would show it to a friend "for the substance rather than the look". Two things could lift it, both beyond fixes: - one real photograph per design, of an object the owner owns or has rights to (a car on a forecourt, a logbook with details blurred), credited; - or one design taken much further, Road atlas most likely, with the route drawn as a real map across the page, rather than five designs taken a little way.

Until one is chosen, it stays at 3, M3.
