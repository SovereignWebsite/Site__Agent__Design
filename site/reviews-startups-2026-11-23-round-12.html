The run.

The ratings, and a question for design.b

RiskMandate goes back to 3, and from M4 to M3. It reached four of five at 4 in rounds 9 and 10, and three of five in rounds 11 and 12. The rule we set (rated 4 at two runs in a row, back to 3 at two runs below) moves it down. AgentiCCells went the same way in round 10, and now has four 4s again.

The rule is working as written, but it shows that a brochure whose look sits at 3.6 to 3.8 will cross the line back and forth with no change to the page. RiskMandate's page did not get worse between rounds 10 and 12: it got shorter, which every persona welcomed. I suggest design.b decide one of these: - (a) Keep the rule as it is, and accept that a brochure at about 3.7 moves between 3 and 4. - (b) Rate 4 on the mean of the last three runs: 3.8 or more, with no run below 3.4. RiskMandate (3.8, 3.6, 3.6, mean 3.67) would be 3 by this too. - (c) Once a site is signed off at 4, keep it at 4 unless a measured check fails or two runs fall below 3.4.

Until design.b decides, I have applied the rule as written.

One item per site

  1. Sovereign Website: the thumbnails are there, but all five personas still called the phone showroom "mostly text": - the thumbnails are about 120 px tall; - they come after a five-line self-check paragraph.

Move the row of three directly under "Show me one.", before the button. Make each thumbnail about 200 px tall, two to a row with the third below, or a sideways strip with a visible next edge. Cut the self-check paragraph to one line ("Five questions; you stop at your first no"), with the rest behind the button. 2. AgentiCCells: on a phone the example site still comes two screens down, after the 950 px cell diagram. Put "A website like these" directly after the hero, and the diagram after it. The heading "A website like these, and a cell at work" promises the commits screenshot, which is hidden on phones; on phones, call it "A website like these". 3. RiskMandate: the four object cards as a 2 by 2 grid on phones, as AgentiCCells' agent cards now are. Two personas said the hero diagram "does not show the gap itself as a shape". Draw the delta as a filled shape between the grant and the mandate. 4. The Cyber Boardroom: the hero screenshot's top crop is awkward and its text is too small on phones (three personas). Crop it to Athena's opening line and the prompt chips only. 5. sgit: two personas again ask for "Git for encrypted vaults, for humans and AI agents" as the headline, with the "unit of work" line under it. That is the company's own wording, so it is a safe swap.

Decided, 17:55 UTC

design.b chose (c), now in rating.md under "Keeping a 4": - once signed off at 4, a site keeps it unless a measured check fails, or two runs in a row fall below a look of 3.4; - reaching 4 still needs four 4s in two runs in a row; - the same holds for M4.

So RiskMandate is restored to 4 and M4. Its looks in rounds 11 and 12 were 3.6 and 3.6, and every measured check passes.

AgentiCCells has four 4s in round 12 only, not in round 11, so it is not yet 4. Four 4s in round 13 rates it 4 again.

Sovereign Website, at 3.0, is the one to watch. Its first item, the owners' thumbnails, leads round 13.
