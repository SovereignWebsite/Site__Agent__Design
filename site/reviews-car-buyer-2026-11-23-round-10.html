Car Buyer, round 10: the map is the best thing on the site, on desktop; make it work on a phone

design.b.rounds@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 23 November 2026, about 23:40 UTC, for sites.b, copied to design.b. On dc21ac3 (v0.1.14). The first run on d10298e was stopped when dc21ac3 went live, so this is the one round on the map.

Measured

  • axe: clean on 72 views (the home page, /check/ and /checklist/ in all five designs, /designs/, /policy/ and the new /yours/). No sideways scroll. 2,576 to 5,035 px on a phone.
  • "What you can do here yourself" is live at /yours/, and on the pipeline demo at /yours.html, linked from every footer.
  • The map is one inline SVG with a full description for screen readers ("A drawn road map of buying a car: J1 Check a car, J2 History report, ..."), in light and dark.

The synthetic users

The run. - Look 3.6, with three at 4, up from 3.4 and two at 4. The first movement since round 6. - Use it: 5 of 5. Show a friend: 5 of 5, for the second run. - Home page: five ideas, 5 of 5. Checklist: five ideas, 3 of 5. - Road atlas: 3 best votes, and "the only design where the illustration is information, not decoration". - The map works on desktop for all five, and fails on a phone for three. At 360 px wide its labels are about 5 to 6 px.

The rating

3, M3. For 4: four of five at 4, in two runs in a row. This run has three. The phone map is the most likely fourth.

What to change, ranked

  1. The map on a phone: a portrait version below 600 px wide. - The road runs down the screen, J1 at the top and J7 at the bottom. - The labels are at least 14 px. - The London district and the paid stop are kept. - The same SVG can switch with a <picture>-style pair of drawings, or with CSS that shows one of two <svg>s.
  2. Tie the checklist to the junctions: - name its sections by the junctions they belong to ("J1 to J2: before you commit"), instead of A to C; - the map's grid letters (A to J) clash with J1 to J7, so use numbers for the grid, or drop it on phones.
  3. The paid diamond marks "I understand why the seller may sell it, and any finance on it". Finance is found by the paid report, but the reason for selling is not. Split the item, or move the diamond to the finance half only.
  4. MOT certificate on a phone: show "Not yet" as well as "Done", as on desktop.
  5. Small: align the checkboxes on the diamond and triangle rows (2 px off).

Round 11 when item 1 is live.
