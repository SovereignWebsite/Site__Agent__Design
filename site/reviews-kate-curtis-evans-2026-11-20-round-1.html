Audit of the live page (390 and 1366 px, light and dark)

Good: no cookies, no storage, no request to any other host, no scripts, system fonts only, 54 KB first load at 390 px (the photo served at 640 px wide to phones), no sideways scroll, one h1, skip link, main, labelled sections, color-scheme meta, the photo credited under the image and in the footer, no em dashes. The first phone screen shows the eyebrow, her name, the one-line story, the why paragraph and both buttons.

To fix, worst first:

  1. Contrast, light scheme: 15 serious axe failures. --soft #8a867c on #f7f4ee is 3.3:1; it is used for the section kickers, the figure caption, the quote source and the photo credit. Use the muted colour (#5a5750, 6.6:1) or anything at 4.5:1 or more. Dark passes.
  2. Family bar links are 15 px tall (WCAG 2.5.8 wants 24 px) and are lightened with opacity. Use an explicit measured colour, .875rem text and min-height:24px; line-height:24px (as in the designs).
  3. Body text in muted. The hero's second paragraph and some body paragraphs are --muted; guidelines 4 keep body text in --fg.
  4. One design only. Rating stays at most 2 until five designs exist (rating.md level 3); below are five.
  5. Small: the title should be "Kate Curtis-Evans, Founder, Audio Genius" or similar ("Page, Site"); no favicon.svg.

Rating now: 1 (works, but fails WCAG AA contrast). After fixes 1 to 3: 2. With the five designs in site/designs/ and one chosen: 3.

Five designs, each an idea from Kate's world

Files: designs/index.html and 1.html to 5.html beside this file, built by the small Python files there (shell.py holds the shared family bar, footer and photo). They expect img/kate-at-desk.webp and img/kate-at-desk-640.webp as on the live site. Only the Matt Hinton desk photo is used, credited under it and in the footer; not the banner headshot, poster or logo.

# Design What it is a metaphor for Kate's own facts it draws
1 The Transcript The page is a legal transcript, the document she produces every day: a cover sheet, numbered turns, Q and A. Her story as answers; her quote as a verbatim turn; the beta figures as "Exhibit A"; her record as a schedule. The questions are labelled as the site's own.
2 The Edit Window Designed dark first, as a transcription editor: a waveform and multitrack timeline. Her four roles as tracks drawn to the year (2021 to 2026, "now" marked); the BA in a line below; figures as meter readouts. The waveform is decoration (aria-hidden), not data.
3 The Workflow Board Audio Genius is workflow management, so her own work is a board. Delivered (BA, Ubiqus, the 90-day beta, the September launch), In progress (her roles, foundations), On hold on purpose (legal glossary, template uploads), Unassigned (the technical co-founder, the regulated-data stack).
4 Foundations "The part nobody sees" comes first, so the page is drawn in section. Above ground: the product and figures, the two paused features; below: privacy and data protection; a dashed empty slot for the co-founder and the regulated-data work; bedrock: her six years.
5 The Brief Plain and printable, for people in a hurry. The ask, the product, the figures and her record; a print stylesheet that prints the link addresses.

Checked, served locally with the same audit as the live sites: axe-core 4.10.2 (WCAG 2.2 AA and best practice) finds no violations on any of the six pages in any of the four views; no sideways scroll at 390 px; no target under 24 px; no request to another host; 7 to 60 KB per page before the photo. On a phone each design shows its idea within the first screen or just after it (the cover sheet; the waveform; the board counts; the section drawing).

Recommendation

Lead with 3, The Workflow Board, or 1, The Transcript. Both turn the ask (a technical co-founder) into the shape of the page: on the Board it is the one unassigned job, in the Transcript it is the question every co-founder would ask. Kate (and Dinis) choose.
