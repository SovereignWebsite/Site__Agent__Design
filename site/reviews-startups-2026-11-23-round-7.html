The ratings. RiskMandate and Sovereign Website reached four of five at 4 this run; one more run like it rates them 4. AgentiCCells fell to two 4s this run. Under the two-run rule it keeps its 4, but one more run below four of five takes it back to 3. Its personas ask for the same thing in every run: a picture of the product or of a delivered site.

To fix, in order

  1. One date per capture, everywhere. The phone captions say 23 November and the desktop captions say 20 November; both may be right, since they are separate captures. But the footer credit still says "Logo and screenshots: sgit.ai, 20 November 2026" on a page whose phone shots are from the 23rd. Make the credit line name both dates, or drop the date there and leave it to the captions.
  2. AgentiCCells needs one real picture. Three personas in each of the last three runs asked for a photograph or screenshot of the product or of a delivered site. If the company has published one (the console, a live cell's page, a delivered site), show it with its source; if not, say so in the caption of the diagram.
  3. The source-number links are still small to tap on four sites. Set the whole row as one larger target, for example "Sources: 1, 2, 7, 14" as a single link to the list with the numbers inside it, or give each number padding:6px 8px.
  4. sgit: the hero vault screenshot is cut mid-sentence ("exits 0 if it is fixed and 1 if it is not" is clipped). End the crop on a whole paragraph. The /designs/ thumbnail for "Identity: sgit.ai" still shows the old home hero; regenerate it.
  5. The Cyber Boardroom: the hero screenshot repeats the logo above it. Crop to the page's content, below its header.
  6. The drawn lower-half bands (sgit, The Cyber Boardroom, RiskMandate), as planned. On RiskMandate, the personas also asked for one style for the two kinds of stat, with the "Measured" numbers at the larger size.
