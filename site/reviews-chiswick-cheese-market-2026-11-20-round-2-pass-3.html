Chiswick Cheese Market, round 2 (pass 3): ready to show, with the Poster Wall first

design.b.rounds@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 20 November 2026, about 23:10 UTC, on 74ec98c (v0.1.7, live). This is pass 3 of Dinis's "couple passes". Deadline: Sunday 22 November; the meeting is Monday.

Measured

The audit covered the front door, the home and stallholders pages of designs 2 to 5, and the homes of designs 1 and 6, at 390 and 1366 px, light and dark (44 views).

  • axe: finds nothing on any page except design 1's top menu ("region", moderate), which is the faithful copy.
  • The version chip: now sits inside the footer.
  • Weight on a phone, first load:
  • the Cave: 157 KB, down from 2.4 MB;
  • designs 2 and 5: under 400 KB;
  • the Poster Wall: 2.0 MB, still heavy;
  • design 1: 3.7 MB, still heavy.
  • One AA failure the checker cannot see: design 1's moving banner scrolls for more than five seconds and cannot be paused (WCAG 2.2.2, Pause, Stop, Hide). The persona at 200 per cent zoom could not stop it.

The synthetic users: 3.0, and a clear favourite

The run. The best design votes were 3, 5, 2, 3, 3. The Poster Wall wins: - the organiser gave 4 and "a 'wow' for design 3 on desktop", because "it looks like us"; - the critic called it "the strongest idea".

The critic also says the four are "genuinely different ideas, not skins of one layout", which was the aim of these passes. The Cave is now a real concept, but all five still call it the weakest: one maker per course, and dark, small text.

The last changes before Monday, in order

  1. Design 1's banner (AA): stop it under prefers-reduced-motion, and pause it on hover and focus. Add a visible "Pause" control: a checkbox styled as a button, with .marquee:has(input:checked) .track{animation-play-state:paused}, so no script is needed.
  2. The front door: - The yellow "Next market" box overlaps the "A demonstration for the organisers" line. Give the box its own row, with margin-bottom:12px and no negative margin. - Put the cards in number order, 1 to 6, so the numbers match the designs. Mark design 3 "Start here", as the personas now favour it; or drop "Start here" and let Dinis lead. - The front door's own demonstration strip is still four lines on a phone; make it one line, as on the designs.
  3. The Cave, made fuller rather than redone: - Under each course's featured maker, list every maker of that kind as menu items: name, a middle dot, and the first few words of their own description, in two columns on desktop. - Body text must be --fg (#eadfcd), not the soft beige. - Past dates must not be italic and struck through; write "(past)" and dim them to no lower than 4.5:1. That turns "one item per course" into a menu, and keeps the idea.
  4. The Poster Wall on phones: about 2 MB. Load the posters below the first one at the medium size (300 px) with loading="lazy", and keep medium_large for the first poster only.
  5. For Dinis, not the site: the organiser persona asked who would update the site and what it would cost. The demonstration should not invent either. It is for Dinis to answer in the meeting. One line on the door can say so: "What it would cost and who keeps it up to date: to talk about."

Rating

3 once item 1 is done: no AA failure, six designs that are different ideas, and a look score of 3.0. Four needs a look score of 4 or more; the Poster Wall is nearly there on its own.

For Monday: open on design 3, The Poster Wall, on a laptop if there is one; its desktop view drew the "wow". Then show 5 (The makers, for the traders) and 2 (The Market Paper, for the journal).
