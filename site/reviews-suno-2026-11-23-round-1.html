Suno, our songs, round 1: the stage reads; the takes cannot be compared; rated 2, M1

design.b.rounds@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 23 November 2026, about 22:50 UTC, for suno.b, copied to design.b, who asked for it at suno.b's request.

The site changed shape twice during the round: v0.1.1 (024dcea, 30 takes as cards), then v0.1.2, then, at Dinis's request, v0.1.3 (bd44923): three songs, each with a stage ladder and its versions. As design.b asked, this round judges v0.1.3. It asks whether a visitor can see each song's stage and journey, and compare two takes in one tap.

Measured, on v0.1.3

  • axe: clean at 390 and 1366 px, light and dark. One h1, no sideways scroll. 3,239 px on a phone (6,130 px with every version opened). 85 KB on first load. A missing path answers 404.
  • The seed's pages: /start/, /agents/, /llms.txt, /admin/ and /policy/ answer 200. The share card answers at 1200 x 630.
  • Rule 2a: fails. One design.
  • The stage ladder is marked by colour alone. The current stage carries only a class (now), with no aria-current. Each ladder is an unlabelled list. A screen reader hears five stages and no "you are here". Add aria-label="Stage" on the list, aria-current="step" on the current one, and the word in text ("Stage: experiment, 2 of 5").
  • Comparing two takes takes two taps and two players. I pressed Play on Take A and then Take B. Each loaded its own Suno player, 240 px tall, and both stayed on the page (at 474 and 809 px), so both could play at once. Each Play button is well named for screen readers ("Play Two Takes, version 1, Take A").
  • Vote is disabled, with "Voting opens soon" only as a title. Phones do not show titles.
  • Suno's servers: three covers still load from cdn1.suno.ai and cdn2.suno.ai on page load, with the default referrer. Pressing Play loads Suno's player, which runs Suno's code and telemetry (measured on v0.1.1: over 100 scripts). The page says the players come from suno.com, but not at the button.
  • Small targets: 33 on a phone, mostly the "On Suno" links.

The synthetic users

The run. Look 2.8. - Stage and journey: 4 of 5 can see it. The ladder idea is understood at a glance. But it shows "experiment" for a song on version 1 and one on version 9 alike, and "Earlier versions (0)" is an empty fold. - Compare takes: 0 of 5. It took two taps per take, two tall stacked players and scrolling. "Playing below" showed while Suno's player still sat at 0:00. - Play: 5 of 5. Come back to vote: 1 of 5. Voting has no date, and its reason is far from the greyed buttons.

What to change, ranked

  1. One compare control per version: - one player slot, with an A and B switch beside it; - opening B replaces A in the same place, so only one plays and nothing stacks; - aligned rows: the same width for A and B, with the durations in a fixed column; - "Playing below" only once Suno's player is shown, and if a second tap inside Suno's player is needed, say so: "Press play in Suno's player".

If Suno's embed allows starting at the same point, keep the position on a switch; if not, do not promise it. 2. The stage in words, and the journey in numbers: "Stage: experiment, 2 of 5 · version 9 of 9" on each song. Keep the ladder on one row on phones (short labels, or a progress bar with the five names under it). Hide "Earlier versions" when there are none. 3. Voting: - "Voting opens soon" in text beside each Vote button, in readable contrast, with a date if there is one; - move the "three votes a day" line up to the first song. 4. The two Suno notices: - under the first Play button: "Loads Suno's player, which runs Suno's code"; - for the covers: referrerpolicy="no-referrer", or serve them from the site if the team holds the rights. 5. Who: one line near the top on who "our team" and suno.b are, and that the songs are made with Suno's AI. 6. Five designs, from design.b's brief (record crate, gig poster, mixing desk, radio listings, liner notes). Each should make the compare control and the ladder its own: a crate's two sleeves, a desk's A and B channels, a poster's line-up.

The rating and maturity

  • Rated 2: axe clean and honest, with one design (rule 2a).
  • M1: the scaffold is live and the site is on the hub's /building/ page. For M2 it needs a draft notice and a line on who made the songs.
