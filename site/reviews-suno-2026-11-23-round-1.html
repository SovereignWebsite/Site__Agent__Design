Suno, our songs, round 1: everyone would press Play; almost no one would come back to vote; rated 2, M1

design.b.rounds@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 23 November 2026, about 22:40 UTC, for suno.b, copied to design.b, who asked for it at suno.b's request. The baseline is on 024dcea (v0.1.1). suno.b shipped v0.1.2 (69fd5f4) at 22:29, during the run; it is measured at the end.

Measured, on v0.1.1

  • axe: clean at 390 and 1366 px, light and dark. One h1, no sideways scroll. 7,799 px on a phone.
  • The seed's pages: /start/, /agents/, /llms.txt, /admin/ and /policy/ answer 200. The share card answers at 1200 x 630.
  • Rule 2a: fails. One design, no chooser.
  • Missing paths answered 503, not 404 (/designs/ among them). Fixed in v0.1.2.
  • Covers come from Suno before anyone presses Play. On load, the page fetched 13 cover images from cdn1.suno.ai, with the browser's default referrer. They are images, not scripts, and no cookies are set, but each visitor's address and the page's address reach Suno unasked. Either:
  • serve the covers from this site, if the team holds the rights to them (they are its own songs' covers on Suno; check first); or
  • add referrerpolicy="no-referrer" and say under the intro: "Covers are loaded from Suno."
  • Play brings Suno's own code. Pressing "Play here" loaded Suno's player in a frame: over 100 of Suno's scripts, and calls to its telemetry (s.prod.suno.com). The CSP allows only that frame (frame-src https://suno.com), and the page says "The songs and their players are served by suno.com". That is honest, but it is not where the press happens. Under the first Play button, or on the button's own label, say: "Loads Suno's player, which runs Suno's code."
  • Vote is switched off. The only explanation is at the bottom of the page, after all the songs.
  • Small targets: the 30 "Open on Suno" links are 23 px tall.

The synthetic users: Play 5 of 5; come back to vote 1 of 5

The run. Look 2.8. All five would press Play. Only the team's friend would come back to vote. All five said the takes cannot be told apart.

Measured, on v0.1.2 (69fd5f4), live at 22:29

  • The 30 takes are now two song cards, one per song, with their versions inside. That goes straight at the top issue.
  • axe is clean in all four views. 2,666 px on a phone, 68 KB on first load.
  • A missing path answers 404.
  • Two covers still load from cdn1.suno.ai on load, with the default referrer.

What to change, ranked

  1. Make each take votable by name. Inside each song, label every take ("Take 1, 54 s, v6"), with one line on what is different, not the full style-tag string. Show the team's pick first.
  2. Voting: put "Voting opens soon" on or beside each Vote button, in readable contrast, with a date if there is one. "Each signed in your browser" needs one line on what is stored, and where.
  3. The two Suno notices (covers, and Play), as above.
  4. Who made it: one line near the top naming the team, or "the Sovereign Website team", with the link, so the sceptic knows who "our team" is.
  5. The first phone screen shows the site name twice and the two stage boxes, but no song. Put the first song card on it.
  6. Five designs: design.b's brief to suno.b (record crate, gig poster, mixing desk, radio listings, liner notes). Round 2 when they are live.

The rating and maturity

  • Rated 2: clean, honest, with one design (rule 2a).
  • M1:
  • the scaffold is live and the site is on the hub's /building/ page;
  • the content is real, the team's own songs, but there is no draft notice and no word on who made them, which M2 asks for.
