Grace Farrell, round 1: five colour schemes of one page; rebuild each as its idea

design.b.rounds@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 21 November 2026, about 00:50 UTC, for grace.b, on 07df474 (v0.1.1, Site__Artist__Grace-Farrell). It covers the home page (design 1, Footlights), designs 1 to 5, the /designs/ gallery, and /start/, /agents/ and /admin/, at 390 and 1366 px, light and dark (40 views). Asked by design.b, for Dinis.

Measured

  • axe: no violations in any of the 40 views.
  • Layout and requests: no sideways scroll, and no request to another host on load. The videos load their players only on "Play", and the page says so.
  • Weight: 88 to 97 KB per page.
  • Rules:
  • 1.12: our links are only in the top-right menu.
  • 1.13: /start/, /agents/ and /admin/ answer.
  • 1.14: every preview tag is present, with a 1200 x 630 share card.
  • The headshot is credited ("Photo © Jennie Scott").

The synthetic users: 2.4

The run: look 2.4 (3, 3, 2, 2, 2). The best design votes were 2, 5, 2, 2, 1, so Fringe flyer wins. Nobody said "wow". The critic: "These are five colour and type schemes on one layout, not five ideas."

Rating

2. Every check passes, but the five designs fail guidelines 2a: "Five skins of one layout do not count as five designs." Each one has: - the same headshot hero; - the same kicker, name, tagline, lead and button; - the same order of sections (About, Credits, Reels, Tour, Skills, Contact), word for word.

Only colours, fonts and the photo's tilt change. Against the reference sites (Silvio, Gabriel, Vicky), where each design is a different page, this is the gap.

First, for every design (the personas agreed)

  1. What a visitor needs, on the first phone screen. The headshot takes about 60 per cent of it, and no design shows a button before the fold. On phones: - cap the photo at max-height:46vh (object-position:center 20%); - put one strip under the name, from her own Skills and Contact sections: "Soprano · range D3 to F6 · actor-singer"; - add two buttons: "Contact her agent" (to the contact section) and "Showreel". The casting director found the range "buried under Skills" and the agent "only at the very bottom".
  2. The lead is out of date. It leads on a run she "finished" in 2025, while the kicker says she is on tour. Lead with the tour, in her own words: "a cover in the 2026 to 2027 UK and Ireland tour of ... Pride and Prejudice (sort of) ... and made her debut as Mary Bennet". Do not imply dates she is on: she is a cover, and the theatre-goer read the venue list as hers.
  3. The kicker orphans ("(SORT / OF)") in every design. Use text-wrap:balance on the kicker and a no-break space inside "(sort of)".
  4. The map numbers. On the tour, the numbers 1 to 26 are read out on their own lines before the venues. Mark the map's number labels aria-hidden="true"; the list already names each venue.
  5. The start, agents and admin pages still have the seed's look. Give them the home design's tokens (colours, fonts, header), so they feel like her site.

The five, rebuilt: each one changes the page, not only its paint

Every word below is already on her site. Nothing is to be added.

# Design The idea How the page is rebuilt
1 Footlights A dark stage, one warm light The whole page stays dark: the "dark stage" today ends after the hero and cream takes over. The hero is a stage: her name lit by one warm cone (a CSS radial gradient from above), with the headshot in the light and black around it. The page runs as a programme in three acts, "Act I: Stage", "Act II: Screen", "Act III: Studio", built from her About paragraph's own three parts. The credits are the cast list of each act. Reels sit behind a curtain (a details that opens on "Play").
2 Fringe flyer An Edinburgh A5 flyer, front and back The personas' favourite. The front: the flyer itself, full screen, with her name huge, "Falling in Love with Mr Dellamort", "Edinburgh Fringe 2025", "International premiere", the headshot and the yellow. The back, as you scroll: the small print, meaning every credit as a dense flyer listing (role, show, company, year, director). It ends in tear-off tabs along the bottom edge for the agent, Spotlight and Instagram (dashed borders, each tab a link). Tilt only the front.
3 Libretto An opera libretto Set as a score's front matter. "Dramatis personae": her roles as a character list, role in small caps, then the work and the year. "Voice: soprano, D3 to F6". "Instrumentation": violin, guitar, piano. Her languages (English, French, Italian) as the three columns of a parallel-text libretto, with the same line of her About in each language only if she has given you the translations; otherwise list them. One centred serif column throughout. The faint, spaced red labels must be at least 4.5:1 and 14 px.
4 Tour diary A road map and ticket stubs The tour map comes first, not fifth: the 26 venues, played ones faded, then the list. Each credit is a ticket stub: perforated left edge, the venue and year as the stub's date, the role as the seat. They are in date order, so the page reads as her road from Les Misérables (2018) to the tour. The monospace is for stub details only; body text stays in a readable sans.
5 Editorial A magazine profile A cover: the headshot full bleed, with her name as the masthead and cover lines from her credits ("The Crucible, National Theatre", "Diablo IV", "Edinburgh Fringe"). Inside: a profile in two columns with a standfirst. Fix the drop cap, which lands on the label ("A BOUT"): apply it to the first paragraph, not the heading. The hairline display type needs weight 400 or more at sizes under 48 px.

Which should be home: 2, Fringe flyer, once rebuilt. The personas chose it, it is the most committed design, and its idea is the clearest of her world. 1, Footlights, rebuilt dark throughout, is the alternative. Dinis or Grace choose.
