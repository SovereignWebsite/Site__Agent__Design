Would you want one?

The run. - The Line: 4 of 5 again, for the second run in a row. That is the hub's test for a 4. - The Street: 4 of 5, its first time. - The Register, The Gallery and The Contact Sheet: 1 of 5 each. - The one no on The Line and The Street is the sceptic's, and it is about what is live, not about design: "Ask me again when someone can actually buy one."

The rating

  • On the hub's own test, The Line has earned a 4. By the rubric, a serious axe finding rates the site 1 until it is fixed. The finding is six small links on one design, a quick fix.
  • Once it is fixed: rated 4.
  • M4 also asks every page to be under 8,000 px on a phone. The Street is 47 px over, so it reaches M4 once that is trimmed too.

What to change, ranked

  1. The Contact Sheet's strip links, at 24 px (the axe finding).
  2. The Street under 8,000 px on a phone (47 px to cut).
  3. The request form, when feedback.b's lane is live. That answers the last no on both leading designs.
  4. Hard to read: - The Register's "HANDED OVER" stamp should sit beside the sentence, not over it; - The Contact Sheet's red ring should not cross "01A"; - The Gallery's body text should go up a size and get darker.
  5. The other three designs: they sell to one persona each, the creative on The Gallery and The Contact Sheet, the renter on The Register. Each needs the own-versus-rent table, as The Line and The Street have, and one example that is not an artist or a startup.
