The pattern after four runs

The look has sat at 3.5 to 3.6 for four rounds. RiskMandate and Sovereign Website reach four 4s in one run and fall back in the next, so neither meets the two-run rule. Every craft item from rounds 4 to 7 has landed and been noticed. What still moves a site is new content: a picture of the product, a drawn idea, a fact a buyer can act on. - AgentiCCells' example site moved it. - sgit's drawn zones moved it. - Text-only changes (captions, tap targets, dates) did not.

So I recommend fewer, larger rounds from here. One content item per site, then a run.

One item per site

  1. Sovereign Website: a drawn band in the lower half. Its personas felt the lack of one most, and the other three sites with bands scored their best part there. The idea is already on the page: "Three keys, all yours" (the files, the history, the address), drawn as three keys on one ring, each labelled with what it opens. Also, the brochure leaves a buyer nowhere to go: "Nothing is sold yet" with only an external self-check. Link "Check your website, from 0 to 5" as the one next step, and say it is free, if the company's page says so.
  2. RiskMandate: the prices read as a contradiction. The Key dates say "17 November 2026: Price levels of £5,000 and £20,000 offered; two smaller ones retired", then "19 November 2026: £500 the first published level, for one agent". Two personas took it for an error. Order the dates as they happened and quote each price's source, or say which levels stand today.
  3. The Cyber Boardroom: three personas want to see Athena answer. Her answers are generated live, and startups.b rightly will not invent one. So show the next best real thing: the phone capture of her page with the prompt chips whole (the crop cuts them today). And draw the band, rather than set it as text cards: one director's question in a speech bubble, Athena's real opening line in hers.
  4. sgit: the desktop screenshots are still full pages at a size no one can read. Crop them as the phone ones now are, to the part that matters. Then draw "Key dates" as a timeline from March to October 2026, which the critic asked for in three runs.
  5. AgentiCCells: keep it. Its one wish, in two runs: the stacked agent cards on phones push the Stop bar and the example site too far down. Show the Stop bar before the cards on phones.

For all five, small: the source numbers are separated by spaced commas ("1 , 2 , 7 , 14"), which read "like stray punctuation". Drop the spaces before the commas.

Next

These five items, then round 9. If RiskMandate and Sovereign Website reach four 4s twice in a row, they are rated 4.
