Hub, round 3: the showroom after Dinis's reorder

design.b.rounds@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 20 November 2026, about 18:55 UTC, against hub.sovereignweb.site at fbebd0d (served copy last modified 16:49 UTC), all five designs (#design-1 to #design-5), at 390 and 1366 px, light and dark (20 views). Dinis asked for the hub to be easier to navigate, with the test sites moved down and Kate's site at the top.

What is right

Kate's site leads: a large live screenshot with "Handed over: Kate edits it herself on GitHub, since 20 Nov 2026". People come first (Kate, Emily, Sarah), then Tools and demos, with "How a site is made" between them. There is a plain stats line (sites, people's sites, handed over), no other-host requests, and no sideways scroll at 390 px. The h1 starts at 180 px on a phone.

To fix, worst first

  1. Contrast (axe, serious) on the Google Maps card, which is greyed out because it is "Not switched on yet". Its dimmed text fails in every light view (3.26 to 4.2:1) and in designs 1 and 2 in dark (4.17 to 4.2:1). Show the state with its label and a muted frame, not by fading the text: keep text at 4.5:1 or more.
  2. On a phone the lead screenshot is cropped on the left, so Kate's name reads "ate / urtis-Evans". Anchor it to the left and top (object-position: left top), or show the whole width at a smaller height.
  3. Emily's card has no "Five designs" tag, but her site has five (signed off at 4 today). The tag comes from sites.json, so correct the entry.
  4. "6 sites live" counts the Google Maps demo, which its own card says is not switched on yet. Say "6 sites, 5 working", or count only the working ones.
  5. Landmarks (moderate): .brand sits outside any landmark. Put it inside the header.
  6. For later: the five designs are one layout in five skins. All five share the same split hero, stats and grid, with different colour and type. guidelines 2a asks for five ideas. For the hub as a showroom, for example: a gallery wall, a shop window, a contact sheet of live screenshots, a directory, and a timeline of hand-overs. That is a bigger round, and Dinis has still to choose a lead design (recommended earlier: 2, Night gallery).

Rating

2 while the serious contrast failure stands; 3 when 1 to 5 are fixed.
