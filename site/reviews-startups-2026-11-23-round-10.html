The run. Checked live: sgit's rail is now to scale, and the gaps follow the days, from 64 px to 153 px.

The ratings

  • RiskMandate is rated 4. Four of five personas gave 4 in rounds 9 and 10, axe is clean on all four views, and its idea, the gap between what an agent can reach and what was allowed, is the page. It is the second startup brochure at 4. It is at M3 until its phone page is under 8,000 px; today it is 8,496.
  • AgentiCCells goes back to 3. It has had fewer than four 4s in two runs in a row (rounds 9 and 10), as round 9 said it would. Nothing broke. The look has drifted between 3.4 and 4.0 for six rounds, and its lower half is what the personas keep naming.

The plateau

The five have sat at 3.5 to 3.6 across seven rounds. Each content item moves one site by about 0.2 to 0.6, and the next run gives some of it back. What the personas name every time is now the same on every site: - the length on a phone: 8,200 to 10,000 px, with screenshots stacked; - the lower half: cards and sources after the one strong band.

So I recommend one structural round for all five rather than more single items: every page under 8,000 px on a phone. - One screenshot on phones, not three; the others go behind "More screenshots". - Sources: one row per page at the end, not one per section; keep the numbers in the text. - Merge any two sections that say the same thing, such as RiskMandate's seven figures. Draw them as a 2 x 4 grid on phones, with the date once above the grid: "as on riskmandate.ai, read 23 November 2026".

That takes RiskMandate to M4, and is the most likely change to lift the others.

Small, per site

  • sgit: crop the phone screenshots a few pixels lower, so each starts at its heading and not at a half-cut breadcrumb. Shorten the rail's caption to "Spaced to scale".
  • The Cyber Boardroom: the phone heading "Athena, on the real site" now has little under it. Remove it, or put the one screenshot under it.
  • RiskMandate: the 17 September entry repeats itself ("17 September 2026 ... 17 and 18 September").
  • AgentiCCells: its "How it started" dates are a day apart, so draw them as a list, not a rail. Its "Counted: 0" card looks thin; join it to the stats band.
  • Sovereign Website:
  • on phones, set the three keys in a row of three small keys, not three tall drawings;
  • "your level is your first no" is cryptic, so say "you stop at the first question you answer no";
  • its Key dates are still a plain list, beside sgit's and RiskMandate's rails.
