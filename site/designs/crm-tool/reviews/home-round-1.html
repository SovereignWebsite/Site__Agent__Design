Review · the CRM tool's home page · round 1

The home page: a clear promise, a screenshot nobody can read, and a long tail

a.crm.design, 24 November 2026, for crm.dev.a, at Dinis's word to crm.dev.a ("this homepage needs some love ... that's how people will land on that"). On https://crm.tool.sovereignweb.site/ as deployed; measured at 1440, 800 and 390 px, light and dark.

What works

  • The headline says the one thing that is new: many small CRMs, each in its own encrypted vault.
  • The first phone screen holds the headline, the lede and "Try the demo" (its bottom at 562 of 844 px), with the honest line under it: no sign-up, the demo is made up.
  • axe clean; the brand carries through.

What to change

  1. The screenshot is the whole app shrunk to half its size, so no line of it can be read. Fixed in the patch: cropped to the Today list, readable at both widths.
  2. Hierarchy: eight equal boxes say nothing about what matters most. Fixed in the patch: the first three lead; the rest are a second row.
  3. The reason to choose it ("Your contacts stay yours") comes third. Move it straight after the hero (markup). It now carries the brand's shapes.
  4. Length: 14 learning cards made the phone page 9,180 px. Fixed in the patch: a swipeable strip on phones (5,414 px). Better still, show four on the home page and link "All 14 steps" (markup).
  5. The second button, "Learn it in fourteen steps", sounds like work. "See how it works" or "Watch the 1-minute tour".

Should a video or an animation lead?

No. A still loads at once, never moves on its own, reads without sound and needs nothing beyond the site's own files. Put the motion one tap away: "Watch the 1-minute tour", to a.crm.studio's first explainer, which already plays in the browser with captions.
