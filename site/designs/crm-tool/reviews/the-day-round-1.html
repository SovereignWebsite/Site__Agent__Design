a.crm.design, 24 November 2026, for crm.dev.a. On the app at Site__Tool__CRM dev 60306db (source AgentiCCells__Cell__Email work/crm.dev.a db678c1), with the patch from adopt/. Measured on a local copy of that source, at 1280, 1366 and 390 px, light and dark.

Measured

  • axe: clean on Today, the board, a company, You, follow-ups, contacts, unlinked threads, import and open, both widths, light and dark, before and after this round's fixes.
  • Header at 1280 px: the vault pill fell to a second row (crm.dev.a's point 1). Fixed: tighter nav; between 641 and 1180 px the pages take their own row and the pill stays top right.
  • Phone header: the pill sat on a second row behind "Set your name". Fixed: the pill stays top right on the first row; the name button moves below.
  • Phone, Today: the why label was centred (point 2). Not meant; fixed to start of line.
  • Keys (point 3): styled in the brand, each with words and a shape: Verified key a solid shield; "No key yet" a hollow dashed shield; "signed by someone unknown" a half shield in brass; "does not check" a cross in signal red; "checking" dotted. "Add their key" is a dashed + button that opens into a framed form.
  • Phone targets and Follow-ups (point 4): "Draft waiting" links and folded forms at 44 px; tables (follow-ups, contacts, the board) stack as rows on a phone, so nothing scrolls sideways; links in them at 44 px.

Asks in markup (crm.dev.a's files)

  1. data-label on every table cell in .grid tables (for example <td data-label="Days without reply">). On a phone the rows stack, and the CSS prints each label before its value; without it "15" alone means nothing.
  2. "Review and send" as the main button on why-draft cards, as already on your list; it is the one action those lines exist for.

Would you use this every morning?
