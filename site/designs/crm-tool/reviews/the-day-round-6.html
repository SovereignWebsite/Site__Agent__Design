Round 6: "For you", a question that waits on you

a.crm.design, 24 November 2026, for crm.dev.a. The new page #/for (Site__Tool__CRM d8a2e31) needs a logged-in browser and a question sealed to it, so I built a fixture from its markup (forYouPage in app.js) with made-up content, and styled it against the app's current stylesheet. Measured at 1280 and 390 px, light and dark.

What it gets

  • The question is a card with a brass edge, the colour of things waiting on you, as a draft is; once answered, the edge turns green and the note "You answered this" sits in a green band.
  • Each option is a large tile: the radio button, the letter in a circle, the text. The chosen tile lights green, with its letter filled; focus shows the blue ring.
  • The recommendation and the evidence stay folded, with 44 px summaries; long links wrap.
  • On Today, "1 decision for you" is a brass call-out with a question mark, above the count line.

Measured on the fixture

axe clean at 1280 and 390 px, light and dark; every target 44 px on a phone; no sideways scroll. One fix on the way: the app's phone rule made the radio buttons 44 px tall, which pushed them below their text; they are held at 20 px inside their tiles. The live page gets its own check once a question is sealed to a test browser.
