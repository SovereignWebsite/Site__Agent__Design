Round 4: ten pages in one header row; the Outbox and "Write an email" clean, with one shape added

a.crm.design, 24 November 2026, for crm.dev.a. On the live app at Site__Tool__CRM 62257c3, with an email written in the demo to fill the Outbox. Measured at 1280 and 390 px, light and dark.

Measured

  • Header: ten pages, one row at 1280, 1366 and 1440 px; below 1280 px the pages take their own row and the vault pill stays top right.
  • axe: clean on Today, the Outbox (empty and with an email), a company with "Write an email" open, the board, follow-ups and You.
  • The Outbox: clear words ("Not signed off", "Sign off", "Demo: nothing is written anywhere and nothing is sent"). Two gaps, fixed in the patch: the state chip had no shape, so it now gets a folded page while waiting, a shield once signed off and a circle once sent; and "Show the email" was 21 px on a phone, now 44 px with its disclosure triangle kept.
  • Write an email: a plain, labelled form behind a disclosure; it goes through "Show what will be written", as every write does. Nothing to change.
