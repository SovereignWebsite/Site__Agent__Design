Five designs for the SovereignWeb.site CRM

Five different ideas of the same tool, not five colour skins. Each one is a clickable prototype on made-up data: Today, the lead board, a company with its Activity and Log an action, follow-ups, contacts, import, open or start a vault, requests and You. Dinis chooses the home design after seeing all five; crm.dev.a builds it at crm.tool.sovereignweb.site/app/.

What every design keeps

  • The demo band, fixed at the top of every page, in words: "Synthetic demo".
  • The vault switcher top right on every page, always naming the open vault, with the QR hand-over one step from it.
  • Status by words and shape, never colour alone: Overdue 2 days Due today Draft waiting They replied yesterday No reply for 9 days
  • The one action (send the waiting draft, answer the reply, or log an action) on the first phone screen.
  • Static: CSP 'self' plus dev.send.sgraph.ai, system fonts only, 44 px targets on phones, light and dark.

The brand they share: the SovereignWeb.site CRM mark, colours and type. From design.b's brief and round 1.
