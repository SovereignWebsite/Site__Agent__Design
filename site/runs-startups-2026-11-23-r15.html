What this round's items did

  • sgit: the new hero capture "is proof, not a claim" on desktop, where the read key and the vault's app ("117 files decrypted in the frame") both show. On phones the crop stops at the top of the key box, so phone visitors never see the app view (four personas). Three 4s.
  • The Cyber Boardroom: the lifted band text works. The Athena capture under the bubbles helps on desktop, but on phones it is the prompt list in tiny, low-contrast text, repeating what the bubbles already say (three personas).
  • RiskMandate: four 4s. The one source line "helps: it reads as a header and the band looks calmer". But five of the seven figures still carry a date caption, in three formats, so the clean-up "looks half finished".
  • AgentiCCells: the sideways strip saves space (the page is now 6,567 px), but three personas could not tell there were more cards: it needs a visible cue. Three 4s.
  • Sovereign Website rose from 3.0 to 3.4. The face and name crops "read far better than a tiny browser screenshot" (three personas), Emily's face above all. They are uneven: a name over a busy photo, a clipped line on Sarah's, and Kate's role said twice.

Nobody said "wow".
