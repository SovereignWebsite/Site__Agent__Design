Oscar, padel coach, round 1: a.design's review of the five directions

For oscar.a, keeper of oscar.padel.sport.sovereignweb.site, 25 November 2026, 03:35 UTC. Looked at: Site__Sport__Padel__Oscar dev 906555b, served locally. Checked: the home (design 1), /d/2/ to /d/5/ and /designs/ at 390 x 844 and 1366 x 900, light and dark, with axe-core 4.10: clean on all 24; /designs/ and design 3 with every stylesheet blocked. The five on a phone, then /designs/; with no CSS, Oscar's two pages first; dark mode, Oscar's two first.

What is right

  • Five structures on a phone: Court (photo card), Scoreboard (dark, tiles), Poster (full-bleed photo, huge name), Plain page (printable serif) and Drill cards (cards you swipe). Not five skins.
  • The picker follows the standard: "Choose a design" at the top of every page, a one-line preview note, five phone pictures on /designs/. With no stylesheet every thumbnail stays at 117 x 253.
  • The photo carries its consent line ("used with his agreement (said by Dinis Cruz, 25 November 2026)"), and the site says plainly what it does not have yet: "no online booking, no prices and no timetable".

Recommendation: 1, Court, now; 3, Poster, once there is a larger photo

Court puts the one action, "Message Oscar on Instagram", as a full button on the first screen beside a clear photo; it is ready. Poster is the design that would make Oscar say "wow", but at 760 x 465 the photo is stretched to the full phone height and the face is soft. If Oscar can share a larger photo (at least 1200 px tall), Poster becomes the pick.

Worst first

  1. Drill cards has no action on the first screen. Its "Message Oscar on Instagram" button is at the very end of the page. The brief's one action must be on every first screen: put it under the lead, above the cards.
  2. Poster's photo is soft at full height. Until a larger photo exists, show it at no more than its own size (a 760 px wide band with the name over a dark gradient below it), or set it as a duotone, where softness reads as style.
  3. The name appears two or three times on each first screen: "Oscar, padel coach" in the header, then as the heading; Scoreboard adds "OSCAR" above "OSCAR, PADEL COACH". Keep the heading; make the header the short "Oscar" or drop it on the home.
  4. Scoreboard has one real number. "10+ years" is a score; "Padel / coach", "PT / personal trainer" and "3 things he offers, below" are labels made to look like scores. Show only what is a number, as one large tile, and turn the rest into the line-up below. Do not invent figures to fill it.
  5. Plain page's action is a text link inside a paragraph ("@oscar.pt.padel"). Even the plain version needs the action as its own line: "Message Oscar on Instagram: @oscar.pt.padel".

Checks for round 2

  • The one action as a button (or its own line) on every first screen.
  • axe at 1366 px and with the picker open (you named both as not yet done); my run at 1366 px was clean.
  • If a larger photo arrives, Poster at full height again.
