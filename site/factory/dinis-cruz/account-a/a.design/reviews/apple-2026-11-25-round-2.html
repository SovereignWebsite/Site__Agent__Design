Apple as a platform (unofficial), round 2: a.design's review

For apple.a, 25 November 2026, 01:20 UTC. Looked at: Site__Platform__Apple dev 9875df3, served locally. Checked: /design/ and the five homes at 390 x 844 and 1366 x 900, light and dark, with axe-core 4.10: clean on all 24; and the Apple Intelligence page in D1 and D2 on a phone. Homes and the picker on a phone; desktop, light; the Apple Intelligence page, D1 and D2. Round 1: review.

Round 1's list, checked

All seven acted on: a Menu button under 480 px (D3 a scrolling pill row instead); the address kicker gone; one sourced fact on the home's first screen ("In Apple's words, read 25 November 2026..."); D2's "On this page" list on content pages; D3 a mosaic on wide screens; D4 dark in both schemes; the picker shows real screenshots.

Are the five distinct on a phone now?

Three are: D3 (pill row and cards), D4 (dark, monospace) and D5 (poster type, ink notice band). D1 and D2 are still close on the home: the same order, D2 with boxes. They part on content pages, where D2's "On this page" box is the difference. That is enough for a manual, as long as D2's home also shows its manual idea (item 2).

What is left, worst first

  1. The sourced fact is a nine-line paragraph in every design. It is the right fact, but hard to scan. Set it as two short rows: "Works on: iPhone 15 Pro and newer; iPad and Mac with M1 or newer; Apple Vision Pro" and "Languages: 16", with the source and date under them; keep the developer quote as a third row.
  2. D2's home does not look like a manual on a phone. Give it the same "In this guide" box that its content pages have (the six sections, numbered), above the section cards, so the first screen says "manual".
  3. The Menu button takes a row of its own in D2 and D5, because "Apple as a platform (unofficial)" fills the line. On phones, keep the title and the button on one line (a smaller title, or "Apple as a platform" with "unofficial" carried by the notice just below it, which already says "Not Apple's site").

Recommendation

Unchanged: D2, Manual for the live site, D5, Poster as the showpiece. With items 1 to 3 it is ready for Dinis; no round 3 is needed for the choice.
