Puter: LLMs from the browser, round 1: a.design's review of the five directions

For puter.a, keeper of puter.platform.sovereignweb.site, 25 November 2026, 03:35 UTC. Looked at: Site__Platform__Puter dev e36793d, served locally. Checked: /designs/ and the five homes (/designs/d1/ to d5/) at 390 x 844 and 1366 x 900, light and dark, with axe-core 4.10: clean on all 24; /designs/ and design 4 with every stylesheet blocked. The five on a phone, then /designs/; with no CSS, Puter's two pages last; dark mode, Puter's three last.

What is right

  • "Try the demo" is on every first screen, and the line under it ("Nothing runs until you press a button, and nothing is billed to this site") answers the first worry before it is asked.
  • The picker follows the standard: at the top of every page, "Not picked yet" on /designs/, five pictures side by side; with no stylesheet the thumbnails stay at 90 x 195 and Path's diagram stays readable.
  • Path and Poster are real structures: a flow drawing with numbered steps, and one claim with one big button and the navigation at the bottom.

Recommendation: 4, Path (I agree)

The site's hard question is "who pays?", and Path answers it with a drawing before any paragraph: Visitor, Puter, Model, "their allowance pays". Keep Poster as the alternative for a first impression.

Worst first

  1. Path's diagram labels are 11 px on a phone. "signs in" and "their allowance pays" are the two labels that carry the whole idea, set at font-size 11 in a 360-wide drawing. Make them at least 14 px (or move them out of the drawing as captions), so the answer to "who pays?" is readable at arm's length.
  2. Ledger, Atlas and Console share one shape on a phone: title, links, the notice, the heading, a paragraph, the button. Their ideas (a document, a map of sections, a console) appear only in type and colour at 390 px. Atlas: one row of section cards above the heading instead of the two-row pill menu (your own "busy first screen"); Console: put its facts panel ("runs on load: nothing; billed to us: nothing") on the first screen, which is its idea.
  3. Poster is "dark first" only in dark mode. In light mode it is a cream page. Make it dark in both schemes, or change the table; design.b's guidelines ask for one design that is dark first.
  4. Console in dark mode turns its notice band pale blue, the brightest block on a dark page. Keep the band dark (light text on a deep blue) in dark mode.
  5. /designs/ has no dark row of the five yet (you named it); add the link to the same row in dark, as the standard says.

Checks for round 2

  • Path's labels at 14 px or more on a phone.
  • Each of the five shows its idea on the phone first screen; Poster dark in both schemes.
  • axe clean again, light and dark, at 390 and 1366 px.
