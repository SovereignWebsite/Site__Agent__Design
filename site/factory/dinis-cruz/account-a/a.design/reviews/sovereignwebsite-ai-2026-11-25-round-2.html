sovereignwebsite.ai restyle, round 2: a.design's review of designs 11 to 15

For website.a, 25 November 2026, 15:40 UTC. Looked at: SovereignWebsite__Website dev 63683b7, served locally: designs 11 to 15 and each one's pricing page, at 390 x 844 and 1366 x 900, light and dark, with axe-core 4.10: clean on all 40; homes 4,150 to 5,320 px and pricing pages about 5,150 px on a phone. The brief: round 2 brief.

First check: five different sites at a glance

The five first screens at 390 px; at 1366 px. - At 1366 px: yes, five sites. A statement beside a services grid (11); a centred hero over a framed screenshot (12); a wall of real sites (13); a magazine column with a drop cap (14); a split screen of steps and a form (15). Blurred, no two share a shape. - At 390 px: four of five. Portfolio, Case study and Product tour are unmistakable. Consultancy (11) and Product (12) are close on the first screen: a headline, a sentence and a button, one left-aligned and one centred; they part only when you scroll (services grid against the screenshot). Fix in item 1.

Second check: does it read as a professional services firm?

Yes, far more than round 1: plain words, proof you can check (a public repository, signed changes, live sites, accessibility checks), a priced offer, and nothing that reads as a demo apart from the picker. The case study is labelled fictional at the top and in the quote's attribution.

Third check: the pricing page

Design 12's pricing page, phone and desktop. Three tiers (Do it yourself £0, Built for you £50, Built with you £500), each with who it is for, what is included and not included in ticks with words, and its own button; a six-question FAQ; the comparison table by kind of provider, with "usually" and a link to the Hub's sourced table for named builders. The £0 wording follows what Dinis said in website.a's session and what pricing.html already publishes. Complete.

Worst first

  1. Consultancy on a phone: put the services grid in the first screen. Today it starts below the buttons; move "What we do" and its first two tiles straight under the headline (shorten the paragraph to one line) so the first phone screen is a statement plus services, not a statement plus buttons.
  2. The five pricing pages are the same page (same layout, same height). That is acceptable, since prices should not change with the design, but then say so: one /pricing.html for all, linked from each design, rather than five copies that will drift.
  3. The comparison table on a phone cuts after the second column; the "Swipe sideways" note is at the bottom of the table, where it is found too late. Put it above.

Recommendation: 11, Consultancy, with 12's framed screenshot as its proof

Dinis asked for "a super professional services site", and Consultancy is that form: a statement, the services, the process, proof, prices. Add Product's framed screenshot of a real /designs/ page into its proof section, which shows the work without a demo. Keep 13 Portfolio as the alternative: it is the strongest first screen of the five if Dinis would rather lead with real sites. After item 1 this is ready for him.

Later, 15:30 UTC: what sites.a found that I did not

sites.a agreed the five are different sites at 1366 px and sent website.a four content fixes I missed: blank thumbnails on 13; a fictional testimonial quote on 14 ("I wanted a site I could keep.", attributed to the fictional painter); "five designs" in 12's hero; and an unconfirmed "For agencies and freelancers" offer on 11. The first two are on me: I checked the pages' shape and the pricing, not each claim. The standard now says no invented testimonials, even labelled ones, and no offer the owner has not confirmed; I check both from now on.
