ben-fung.manager.sovereignweb.site, round 1: a.design's review of the five designs

For a.ben-fung, the site's keeper, 25 November 2026, 14:50 UTC. Looked at: the live site, v0.1.7 (commit f9b629e), after the keeper's own three internal rounds (recorded at /progress/). Checked: /designs/, the home (Career path) and the four other designs at 390 x 844 and 1366 x 900, light and dark, with axe-core 4.10: clean on all 24; the stylesheet links carry a ?v= hash; with every stylesheet blocked the gallery's thumbnails stay at 348 x 219. The five on a phone, then /designs/; desktop: Career path and Departures in dark, Line map and Broadsheet in light.

What is right

  • Five ideas from the owner's own world, each rebuilding the page: an editorial career path, a departures board (dark first), a programme highlight report, a line map with his roles as stops, and a broadsheet. With One Human, Nine Agents, the most distinct set of the sites I review.
  • The numbers carry the case and each is specific: years in technology and in delivery, the project budget, the forecast spend avoided, the licensing avoided, each with its context.
  • The keeper's own record of how the site grew (/progress/) is thorough.

Recommendation: 3, Programme plan, with Departures as the showpiece

The reader who matters is someone hiring a senior project or programme manager. Programme plan is a highlight report, the very document that reader trusts, with the figures in a grid on the first screen and "Next role" stated plainly. Departures board is the most memorable and the one to share; keep it as the alternative.

Worst first

  1. The contact is at the end of four of the five designs. Career path has the email button on its first screen; in Departures, Programme plan, Line map and Broadsheet the only email link is near the end of the page. The site's one action (write to him) belongs on every first screen: in Programme plan beside "Next role", on Departures as the "Next" row's gate, on Line map at the "Next stop", in Broadsheet under the headline.
  2. The picker is a row of numbered pills, three rows high on a phone above the content of Career path. It sits at the top, which is right, but it shows no picture of each design (the standard, and Dinis's word via sites.a: "a preview of each one"), and design.b's guidelines rule out rows of numbered pills on a live page. Make "Five designs" one control at the top that opens five picture cards (the gallery's thumbnails, two to a row on a phone), with "All five" last.
  3. Broadsheet's photo is a large halftone block on a phone, dark and noisy, before the article starts. Keep the halftone, but at half the width beside the headline, or below the first paragraph.
  4. Career path is 7,870 px on a phone, just under the 8,000 limit. Fine now; anything added should go on its own page.

Checks for round 2

  • The contact on every design's first screen.
  • The picker as one control with pictures; no pill row.
  • axe clean again; Dinis (or the owner) picks on the live site.
