# sovereignwebsite.ai sector pages: a.design's review

For website.a, 25 November 2026, 14:05 UTC. Looked at: SovereignWebsite__Website dev **e341693**, served locally: /for/artist.html and /for/profile.html, at 390 x 844 and 1366 x 900, light and dark, with axe-core 4.10: **clean on all 8**; 2,320 and 2,360 px on a phone, inside the 2,500 the direction set. [Artist and profile on a phone in full, then artist in dark](img/sovereignwebsite-ai-2026-11-25-sector-pages.jpg). The direction: [page](sovereignwebsite-ai-2026-11-25-direction.html).

## What is right
- It follows the template: the kind, a plain headline ("A website of your own, for your work"; "A profile of your own."), one sentence, the price line, one button, a drawn sample labelled "A fictional example: Ines Hart is not a real person", the same three things you get, three steps, the button again. A non-technical reader gets what, how much and what next in one short scroll.
- "What we need from you" is a good addition: it answers the question the email will raise.
- Each page has its own share card, so the link previews well in an email.

## Worst first
1. **The picker and the full header are wrong for these pages.** "Choose a design" at the top is for Dinis choosing the main site; a painter opening a link from an email will not know what it means. The sector pages are not part of the pick: leave the picker off /for/, and give them a quiet header (the mark and "Sovereign Website" only, no Menu), so the one button is the only thing to press.
2. **The button leaves the site without saying so.** "Yes, I would like one" goes to the Hub's start page, a different site. Say it on the button's line: "Yes, I would like one (opens the Hub, where you ask in a few lines)". When Dinis chooses the request route, point it at the request page with ?for=artist.
3. **The footer tells the reader "the hosted service under this name does not exist yet".** True, but to someone being invited by email it reads as "this is not real". Keep that line on the main site; on /for/ pages say only "Static files: no cookies, no tracking."
4. **The sample's pictures are empty boxes.** On the artist page, draw something in the four tiles (a few CSS waves or brush shapes, for "mostly the sea"), so the sample shows a painter's site, not a template. The profile page can stay as it is.

## Recommendation
With items 1 to 3, these are ready for Dinis to send. Make every further sector page from the same template, with its own drawn sample and the same three promises.
