sovereignwebsite.ai, design 6 (Studio): a.design's review

For website.a, 25 November 2026, 13:50 UTC. Looked at: https://sovereignwebsite.ai/designs/6.html live, and /designs/. Checked at 390 x 844 and 1366 x 900, light and dark, with axe-core 4.10: clean on all 8; no sideways scroll; 4,880 px on a phone. First screen on a phone, light and dark; the whole page on a phone; desktop. The direction: page (it serves; the 404 you saw was before the deploy finished).

What is right

  • It already reads as professional: ivory, ink and cobalt, the serif headline, plain price cards with no coupon border, hairlines, numbered steps. The difference from design 1 is clear at a glance.
  • The prices are stated plainly with who set them and when; the Hub band quotes the Hub's own words; examples are labelled by kind and "with their owners' agreement or ours".
  • Leaving the live home alone until Dinis picks is the right call.

Worst first

  1. The picker is not at the top. While the pick is open, "Choose a design" goes at the very top of every page with a preview of each design (the standard, from Dinis's word via sites.a). Design 6 has none, and its kicker ("Design 6 of six, a proposal: not yet the home page") is doing the picker's job. Put the picker strip at the top, with the one preview line in it, and give the kicker back to the brand ("Sovereign Website").
  2. The phone header still takes about 140 px before the headline: five links on two lines, then the button on its own line. Make it one 56 px line: name, "Menu" (a details), "Ask for yours".
  3. The North Studio strip shows one design, not five. The brief's point is five designs for one brief, and that is also what we sell. With no images, draw five small cards in CSS, one per North Studio design (each its own colours and type, the same headline), in a row that scrolls sideways on a phone, captioned as now. The grey gradient block reads as an empty image; drop it.
  4. Only one of the five directions is built. Studio is direction 1; The Brief, Five Designs, Ownership and The Hub are still to come (direction page, "Five directions to build"). Dinis should see all five side by side before picking.
  5. Not yet built: the sector page template, the comparison page and the request page. Studio's look is settled enough to build the sector template next, since that is what Dinis wants to email.

Recommendation

Keep Studio as direction 1, fix items 1 to 3, then build the other four directions as round 1 and send me the round.
