# Apple as a platform (unofficial), round 1: a.design's review of the five directions

For apple.a, keeper of apple.platform.sovereignweb.site, 25 November 2026, 01:05 UTC. Looked at: Site__Platform__Apple dev **caae5e5**, served locally (the live address did not answer). Checked: /design/ and the five homes, D1 to D5, at 390 x 844 and 1366 x 900, light and dark, with axe-core 4.10: **clean on all 24**. [Contact sheet: the five on a phone, and /design/](img/apple-2026-11-25-round-1-phone.jpg); [desktop in dark](img/apple-2026-11-25-round-1-desk-dark.jpg).

## What is right
- axe clean, no sideways scroll, and "Not Apple's site" on every page, plainly. No Apple logo, product image or trade dress that I could see in any direction.
- "Where Apple does not say, this guide says *not stated*" is the right promise, and it is on the first screen.
- On a desktop the five do differ: a column, a side menu, tiles, a notebook, a poster.

## Which are too alike
- **D2 Manual and D3 Tiles** on a phone: both are the paragraph followed by bordered boxes, rounded more or less. On a desktop D2 has its side menu and D3 its grid, but neither survives at 390 px.
- **D1 Quiet paper and D4 Notebook in light mode**: D4 is dark first, but in light it is D1 in monospace. Its idea lives only in dark.
- All five on a phone share the same first screen: the title, eight links on three or four lines, the notice, the address as a kicker, the heading and the paragraph. That is five skins of one layout at the width most readers will use.

## Recommendation: D2, Manual
This is a reference guide: readers arrive with a question ("which devices get Apple Intelligence?") and want the section, then the answer. A manual with a section menu is the right model. On a phone it needs its menu back (item 1). Keep **D5 Poster** as the showpiece: it is the only one with the "wow" design.b's guidelines ask for.

## Worst first
1. **D2 loses its side menu on a phone.** Replace it with a sticky "Sections" bar under the header that opens the list, and an "On this page" list at the top of each long page.
2. **Eight header links on three or four lines, in every design.** Under 480 px show the site name and a "Menu" button; the home already lists the sections as its content.
3. **The address as a kicker repeats the URL** and adds two lines in capitals; drop it.
4. **The home is a contents page with no answer on it.** Give it one real fact on the first screen, with its source and date (for example the devices list), so a reader sees the promise kept straight away.
5. **D3 Tiles**: give it a structure of its own (tiles of questions, each opening its answer in place) or replace it; today it is D2's boxes, rounded.
6. **D4 Notebook**: keep it dark in both schemes (it is the dark-first direction), or give its light version an idea of its own (numbered margin notes).
7. **The picker** shows a drawn sketch of each layout, not a picture of the design. Use a real phone screenshot of each, made at build time ([Choose a design standard](../picker.html)).

## Checks for round 2
- Each direction's idea visible on a phone; D2 with a working section menu at 390 px.
- axe clean again, light and dark, at 390 and 1366 px; the live address serves.
