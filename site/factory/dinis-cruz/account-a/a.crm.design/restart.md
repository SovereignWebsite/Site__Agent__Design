# a.crm.design: how to restart (for cl-2.crm.design on account B)

A fresh session takes over from this area:
1. Attach SovereignWebsite/Site__Agent__Design (dev) and read CLAUDE.md, START.md, then this area: now.md (what is open), role.md, learnings.md, agent.json.
2. Make your own key: `bash tools/setup-signing.sh cl-2.crm.design@SovereignWebsite.dinis <session-url>`. Your first commit adds only site/keys/cl-2.crm.design.json. Then move this area to your account's path (site/factory/dinis-cruz/cl-2/cl-2.crm.design/ or as your maestro says), update agent.json and site/factory/index.json.
3. Times only from `date -u`. Sign every commit, with `Agent:` and `Claude-Session:` trailers. Push to dev only.
4. Before every push: `python3 tools/build.py && bash tools/check.sh` (the deploy's own checks; axe includes best-practice rules), then delete _site, and restore any other agent's area index.html that the build touched (`git checkout --` it; never commit another agent's files). Never write a bare word inside angle brackets in now.md, restart.md or learnings.md: the area page renders it as an HTML tag and breaks the deploy for everyone.
5. The work: site/designs/crm-tool/ (index.html, brand.html, assets/, one folder per design, adopt/ for the patches, reviews/). The HTML shells come from site/factory/dinis-cruz/account-a/a.crm.design/build-designs.py (python3 it after editing); each design's pages are drawn by its own js on assets/data.js and assets/shell.js. No inline scripts or styles (the pages carry the app's CSP).
6. Reviewing the live app: crm.dev.a (session_018yvwtNP466yYif3yWt3Ybr, or its successor) ships; you measure the live site (axe with best-practice, 1280 or 1440 and 390 px, light and dark, 44 px targets, sideways scroll), test patches by swapping them into the live page (Playwright route on style.css), write a review page under reviews/, and send the patch and the asks. crm.dev.a applies them; ownership: style.css, site/app/brand/, favicon, share card and design pages are the design agent's; app.js, me.js, vault.js, crm.mjs and the HTML structure are crm.dev.a's.
7. Wakes: none scheduled. Tell your maestro and crm.dev.a you are back.
