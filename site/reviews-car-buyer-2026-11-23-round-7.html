Car Buyer, round 7: "show a friend" holds at 4 of 5; the content is done; the look is what is left

design.b.rounds@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 23 November 2026, about 17:50 UTC, for sites.b, copied to design.b. On 5610ba8 (v0.1.8), sites.b's answer to round 6.

Measured

  • axe: clean on 68 views. No sideways scroll. The checklist is 4,182 to 5,333 px on a phone.
  • The phone's first screen: the h1 now starts at 155 px (/check/, /checklist/) and 213 px (home), down from about 40% of the screen in round 6. The design chooser is at the foot of each page.
  • Tick colours: accent-color is set per design, five different colours (Forecourt green, Logbook plum, MOT green, Windscreen black, Road atlas blue).
  • Road atlas markers: set by kind, with the key on the checklist.

The synthetic users

The run. Look 3.2. - Use it: 5 of 5. Show a friend: 4 of 5, for the second run in a row. The low-vision reader moved to yes; the critic moved to no ("still marked as an unreviewed draft ... D4's empty £ tile, D3's table losing its headers on the phone"). - Home page: five ideas, 5 of 5, again. - Checklist: five skins, 3 of 5, again. Two personas call its devices ideas: the parent ("I can text 'do 2.3 at the viewing'") and the first-time buyer.

Where it stands

  • The words, the facts and the tools are done. Every persona would use it, and four of five would show a friend, two runs running.
  • What holds it at 3 is the look: one persona at 4, against the four that 4 needs. And "no pictures or illustrations anywhere".

So the next round should not add more checklist structure. It should add the one thing every design lacks.

What to change, ranked

  1. One drawn picture per design, of its own object, marked as an illustration. No photographs of real cars or people, and nothing that could be read as a real document. For example: - Logbook: a drawn V5C front, with the fields blank; - MOT certificate: a drawn certificate corner with a "PASS" stamp; - Windscreen card: a drawn windscreen with the card in the corner; - Road atlas: a drawn route map from "Before you commit" to "On the day you pay"; - Forecourt: a row of drawn cars under bunting.

Inline SVG, light and dark, under 20 KB each, with alt text, and aria-hidden where it is decoration only. 2. Windscreen card's home page: - it has no sentence saying what the site is, so add the one-line description under the title; - the "£ Fill in Costs" tile still reads as broken (the parent and the critic), so make it "Your first-year total: fill in Costs →", in the same weight as the other two. 3. MOT certificate on phones: - the TEST ITEM / WHERE / RESULT headers disappear, so label each stacked row instead; - on the checklist, "NOT CHECKED" under every unticked item makes it the longest page (5,333 px), so show the result only once an item is ticked, or on the same line. 4. Road atlas, dark mode: the orange diamond and red triangle are dim on navy (the low-vision reader). Lift both. 5. The draft notice: the critic's no. It goes when Dinis, the owner, has reviewed the site, and it is his to remove. I have noted this for design.b.

The rating

3, M3. "Show a friend" now has two runs in a row. For 4, it still needs four of five at 4 in two runs in a row.

Round 8 when the pictures are in.
