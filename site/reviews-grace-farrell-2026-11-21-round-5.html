Grace Farrell, round 5: steady at 3.2; one structural change left, then stop

design.b.rounds@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 21 November 2026, about 02:40 UTC, for grace.b, on 40154c8 (v0.1.12).

Measured

  • axe: no violations in 40 views.
  • Layout: no sideways scroll at 320, 390 or 1366 px.
  • "FARRELL": on one line.
  • Round 4 is in:
  • the agent line (Collective Agents, Call, Email) in every hero;
  • "(this week)" and the ticket link as real elements, dated by the visitor's own clock;
  • the list first and the map folded on phones;
  • the cover sentence once per page.
  • grace.b did not use "Tonight" in Footlights, since as a cover nothing says she is on tonight. That is right.

Synthetic users: 3.2 (4, 3, 3, 3, 3), level with round 4. The best design votes were 5, 5, 3, 4, 4. The casting director found "Call · Email" on the first screen of every design. The theatre-goer found "THIS WEEK Richmond" at the top of the Tour diary.

Rating

3. The look has held at 3.2 for two rounds, and the remaining complaints are of two kinds.

  1. Structural: the hero. The critic says the hero block is the same in all five: kicker, name, tagline, range line, two buttons, links, agent line. Only the styling changes. This is the one change left that can move the look. Give each design one composition that no other design uses: - Editorial: the face full bleed, with the name and the cover lines laid over it at the edges, like a magazine cover. The buttons go in a bar below the cover, not in the hero. On desktop, crop to the eyes and mouth, not the forehead. - Fringe flyer: a flyer at real proportion (A5, aspect-ratio:148/210), centred and tilted, with everything inside it. The page around it is the wall. Her name leads, then "On tour: Pride and Prejudice (sort of)". The Dellamort flyer moves into the credits, where it belongs, since the phone visitor read it as the page's subject. - Footlights: a proscenium. A dark stage, a curtain edge at both sides, and the name and role centre stage in the light. The portrait is a programme photo, small and set to one side. - Libretto: a title page, as now. It is already distinct. - Tour diary: this week's venue as a ticket, the first thing on screen.
  2. Rules and facts, which no design can fix: - "Choose a design" in the header puzzles visitors (three of five). The guidelines require a visual chooser on personal sites. Whether to keep it in her header is a decision for Dinis and Grace, not for grace.b. - "Grace is a cover", so nobody can say when she plays. The site is right to say so plainly.

Smaller

  • Fringe flyer:
  • make the cards upright on phones (transform:none below 600 px);
  • keep white text on magenta at 4.5:1 or more;
  • do not use slanted capitals for headings over 6 words.
  • Grey secondary text should be the body colour at 80 per cent or darker:
  • "(played)";
  • the skills lists;
  • the small capitals.
  • Design 4's "ABOUT ME" heads third-person text; use "About".
  • The showreel stills: the casting director could not be sure each still shows Grace. If a still shows another actor in the scene, say so in its caption ("Grace, right, with …"), but only from what Grace or her reel's own page says.

Recommendation

One more round, on the hero compositions above. If the look does not reach 4 after it, stop: the site is clean, honest and good, and the rest is the chooser decision.
