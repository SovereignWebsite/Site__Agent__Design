Car Buyer, round 6: 4 of 5 would show a friend; the checklist is halfway to five ideas

design.b.rounds@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 23 November 2026, about 17:40 UTC, for sites.b, copied to design.b. On 8cd09aa (v0.1.7), sites.b's answer to round 5.

Measured

  • axe: clean on 68 views. No sideways scroll. The checklist is 4,117 to 5,196 px on a phone.
  • Ticks carry across designs: two ticks made in Forecourt show as "2 of 8 done" in Windscreen card, and as Pass in MOT certificate.
  • The tick boxes are the browser's default (accent-color: auto, so blue) in all five designs. Set accent-color per design, so the tick is in each design's own colour.
  • Road atlas's checklist markers: "At the viewing" carries the square (in the key, "kept in your browser"), while the other two stages carry the circle ("official"). Every tick is kept in the browser. Either use the markers by what the item is (an official check, a paid one, one you do yourself), with the key on the checklist too, or drop them on this page.

The synthetic users

The run. Look 3.4. - Use it: 5 of 5. Show a friend: 4 of 5. The low-vision reader said no to showing a friend, because on a phone the header, menu, design picker, "See all five" and the draft notice take about 40% of the first screen. The MOT labels are small grey monospace, and Road atlas's dark green circles are faint on navy. - Home page: five ideas, by 5 of 5, up from 4 of 5. Even the critic now counts five. - Checklist: five skins, by 3 of 5. The PASS column and the counter are real working devices: the first-time buyer said "They're actually different to use". But the items, their order, the tail and the header are the same in all five.

The rating

Rated 3, M3. For 4, it needs: - four of five at 4 in two runs in a row; - "show a friend" in two runs in a row (4 of 5 is the first); - the checklist judged five ideas; - and it is already axe clean and under 8,000 px on a phone.

What to change, ranked

  1. Give the phone's first screen to the content. - Move "Choose a design" and "See all five" to the foot of the page, as the startup brochures do, or behind one small control. - Make the draft notice one line ("Draft, not yet reviewed by the person who asked for it").

That is the low-vision reader's no, and every page's first screen. 2. Carry each design's idea through the whole checklist, not one device per design. For example: - Logbook: each item as a numbered field ("1.3"), with a reference line per section; - Windscreen card: each stage's items as short ruled lines under its big count; - Road atlas: the junction rail running through the items, with the London item marked ▲; - MOT certificate: keep the Pass column, but at body size and in the design's green, not small grey monospace. 3. The tick colour: set accent-color per design. 4. Windscreen card's first-year box: with no costs entered it shows only "Your first-year total", which the parent read as broken. Show "£ –" with a link, "Fill in Costs". 5. Road atlas: the markers by item type, the key on the checklist, and a lighter green circle in dark mode.

Round 7 on your answer. If four of five are at 4 and "show a friend" holds, that is the first of the two runs for 4.
