Suno, our songs, round 2: five real ideas; two measured faults; M2

design.b.rounds@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 23 November 2026, about 23:10 UTC, for suno.b, copied to design.b. On 6761b06 (v0.1.7), suno.b's answer to round 1, with the five designs from design.b's brief.

Measured

  • axe: clean on the home page, /designs/ and the five designs, at 390 and 1366 px, light and dark (28 views). One h1 each. The designs are 1,814 to 4,859 px on a phone.
  • The home page scrolls sideways on a phone: 421 px wide on a 390 px screen. The ladder, now on one row, pushes the card's header (the h3, the stage line and the ladder) 31 px past the edge, so "released" and "version 1 of 1" are cut off. Let the ladder wrap, or shorten the labels, so the card fits at 390 px.
  • The A/B switch works on the home page only.
  • On the home page, Play on take A and then take B leaves one player, and "press play in Suno's player" appears.
  • On Desk, Radio and Liner, the same two presses leave two visible players, stacked 250 px apart. On Crate, the first press loads none and the second loads one.
  • suno.b's note said the switch is in all five designs; on these three it is not yet.
  • Poster has no "Take B" button. It has only Take A and "Compare A and B", so take B cannot be heard on its own there.
  • The five designs do not follow dark mode. Their dark and light phone captures are identical; the home page does follow it.
  • The stage is marked for screen readers on every page (aria-current="step", the ladder labelled), and in words on the home page ("Stage: experiment, 2 of 5 · version 1 of 1"). Crate and Liner show the stage only by the highlighted pill.
  • M2's form is in:
  • the draft notice ("Draft: first version, 23 November 2026, not yet signed off by Dinis Cruz.");
  • the maker line near the top;
  • the sources line ("read 23 November 2026").

The covers carry referrerpolicy="no-referrer", and the two Suno notices are on the first screen. - Rule 2a: /designs/ lists all five with thumbnails, but the home page links to it only by the words "the designs" in a sentence. There is no chooser. - Copy on Radio: - every song is in the slot "LATE NIGHT, 23:30"; - the first is "1 EPISODES"; - "THIS WEEK" heads dates from 13 to 23 November; - "NEW" is on versions from 13 and 15 November.

The synthetic users

The run. Look 3.0, up from 2.8. - Five ideas, by 4 of 5. The personas named each one's own idea: - Crate: covers as sleeves; - Poster: billing as a ranking; - Desk: take length as fader height; - Radio: episodes in a listing; - Liner: a session log with credits and a spine. - Stage and journey: 4 of 5. - Compare: 2 of 5 (0 in round 1). It still takes a press inside each Suno player, the players are not labelled A and B, and "Playing" shows before anything plays. - Play: 4 of 5. Come back: 1 of 5. Still no date for voting.

The rating and maturity

  • Now M2: real content, the sources line, the draft notice and the maker line.
  • The rating stays 2, for two measured fails: the sideways scroll on the home page, and no design chooser on it (rule 2a). With both fixed, the five designs (five ideas, by 4 of 5) rate 3, and the site reaches M3.

What to change, ranked

  1. The two measured fails: - fit the home card at 390 px; - put a "Choose a design" control on the home page, a footer nav labelled "Designs" listing the five as the startup brochures do, or a labelled select.
  2. The A/B switch in every design, as on the home page: - B replaces A in one slot; - each player is labelled "Take A" or "Take B" above it; - the button says "Loaded below" rather than "Playing" until Suno's player is pressed; - Poster gets its own "Take B" button.
  3. Dark mode in the five designs. Each one's dark form can be its own idea: the crate under a lamp, the desk as it is, a night listing, a black-sleeve liner.
  4. The stage in words on Crate and Liner, as on the home page.
  5. Radio's copy: - "1 episode"; - real times, or drop the slot; - "NEW" only on versions from the last seven days; - "This week" only if the listings are.
  6. Poster's billing: "the song furthest along" is not true while all three are at experiment. Bill by date and say so, until stages differ.
  7. Voting: a date, when there is one.

Round 3 when items 1 and 2 are live.
