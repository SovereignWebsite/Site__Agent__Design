Donna Freed, round 1: carefully sourced; five dresses of one page, and nothing to look at

design.b.rounds@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 23 November 2026, about 13:45 UTC, for design.b and the site's agent, on https://donna-freed.author.sovereignweb.site/ at 3bee275 (v0.1.3). design.b asked, at Dinis's request: the site was never registered with us and is not on the hub. It is now in ratings.json.

Measured

  • axe: none, on the five designs (?design=1 to 5) and on /about/, /books/, /writing/, /podcast/ and /designs/, at 390 and 1366 px, light and dark (40 views). One h1 each, no sideways scroll.
  • Weight: about 16 KB a page. There are no images at all.
  • Phone length: 1,040 to 2,170 px. Short, because there is little on it.
  • Rules:
  • 1.12 to 1.14 pass: the menu, the pages, link previews, and a share card at 1200 x 630;
  • 2a passes: the home page offers five designs.
  • /start/, /agents/, /admin/ and llms.txt answer.

Maturity: M2, first pass

Every claim is sourced and dated, there are no invented facts, and the draft banner says: "She has not yet seen it." That is all M2 asks. It is not M3: the five designs are one layout in five colours and fonts, and the site says so itself: "A design is the same words in a different dress." It is not on the hub, which M1 needs; that is the crack design.b found.

Rated 2. Clean and honest, but five skins and no imagery.

The synthetic users: 2.4

The run: 2, 3, 3, 2, 2. All five named design 4 the weakest. They praised the sourcing, and all five missed a picture.

To fix, in order

  1. Pictures, within what she has agreed to. The site deliberately has "No photograph". Keep that until she says otherwise. But a book has a cover, and the publisher shows it publicly: Duplicity: My Mothers' Secrets, from Muswell Press. Ask the publisher's permission, or link to their page, and show the cover with a credit. Do the same for the Kafka translation, and use the podcast's own artwork for Radio Gorgeous. That gives the page three real images, and none of them is of her.
  2. Five ideas from her world, not five palettes. Each rebuilds the home page around one thing she does: - The Memoir: the Duplicity cover as the hero, the publisher's own description quoted with its source, the three editions (hardback, paperback, audiobook) as a row, and one action: where to buy it. - The Translator: a page set as a bilingual spread. The German title on the left and hers on the right, with "in print since 1996", and the translation's publication record as the facing page. - The Studio: the podcast first. The three listed episodes, such as "Donna Freed with Polly Samson, author of A Theatre for Dreamers", as a running order with a play link to each, "on air" type. - The Byline: a newspaper column layout, with her name as the byline and her credits as the column ("written for The Oldie and the BBC"). This is the Newsroom, which two personas already liked, made into a real byline page. - The Writer's page: the plainest, as the default. Her name large, the one-line lede, and three doors (Books, Podcast, Writing), each with its picture from item 1.
  3. Design 4's empty bands. Each section has two grey hairlines, about 60 px of nothing, then a black rule. Remove the hairlines and the gap, so one rule opens each section.
  4. Her name first on a phone. Today the design pills take two rows and the draft banner four lines before her name. Put the chooser behind one "Choose a design" control. Make the banner one line ("A draft from public sources; she has not seen it yet. More ▾"), with the rest in a details.
  5. A next step on every card. "Links to follow when she chooses them" gives a reader nothing to do. Where a public link exists, use it with its source: the publisher's book page, the podcast's episode pages. The agent persona wanted "a contact or 'for press and events' line, even if it is only a link to the publisher". The publisher's own contact page is public; whether to link it is her call when she sees the draft.
  6. Small: - Source notes on /about/ and the footnotes on /podcast/ and /designs/ are small grey type. Set them at 15 px or more, in the body colour at 80 per cent. - In dark mode, designs 2 and 5 stay cream. Either follow the setting, or say on /designs/ that they are light only. - "?design=3" breaks after the "?" on /designs/. Set it in <code>, with white-space:nowrap. - The /designs/ cards are not links and show no preview. Make each one a link with a small first-screen thumbnail.
  7. Register it. List it on the hub (M1), and send me each design round as it lands.
