The run.

Checked live: every source list item carries its value, and no /designs/ page says "the top of every page" any more. Each source row sits on the content gutter: 16 px on a phone and 107 px on desktop. On Sovereign Website the rows sit under each owner's own text column, which is right.

AgentiCCells is rated 4. All five personas gave 4, axe is clean and the rules pass. It is the first startup brochure at 4. RiskMandate and Sovereign Website have four of five at 4. In both, the low-vision reader's 3 comes from dark-mode text and the phone screenshots. They reach 4 when items 1 and 2 are done.

To fix, in order

  1. Dark-mode card text. The darkening reached the light theme only. In dark mode: - sgit's "How it works" steps, The Cyber Boardroom's cards and Sovereign Website's step cards are still a soft grey; - so are RiskMandate's captions in the dark band.

Use the dark theme's body colour at 90 per cent there too, in the same rule as the light one: css @media(prefers-color-scheme:dark){.card p,.step p,.band figcaption{color:color-mix(in srgb,var(--fg) 90%,transparent)}} 2. The real-size phone crops (round 4, item 4; not done yet). On sgit and The Cyber Boardroom, three personas again called the screenshots unreadable on a phone. 3. "Key dates": the source row comes after the "Next step" box on all five, so it reads as the button's. Put the row inside the Key dates section, before the Next step box. 4. Visible gaps in the numbering. The value fix keeps the numbers true, but the list now shows its gap: - AgentiCCells jumps from 17 to 19; - Sovereign Website jumps from 18 to 20.

Two personas read the gap as a source removed. Renumber the sources without gaps, in the text and the list together. 5. RiskMandate, dark mode: the light sections turn near-black like the dark band, so the section edges disappear. Give the alternate sections a slightly lifted dark surface (for example #16181b against the band's #0b0c0e), with a 1 px rule between them. 6. Sovereign Website: - "Three keys, all yours" is followed by "Quoted from the four plain promises". Three personas noticed the mismatch. Either say which promise is not drawn, or say "from the plain promises". - "Nothing is sold yet" leaves a newcomer no next step; say in one line what a visitor can do today (for example, "Check your website, from 0 to 5"). 7. The lower halves (sgit, The Cyber Boardroom, RiskMandate): round 2's drawn bands are still the way past 3.5. One each, below the fold: - sgit: the three zones (your machine, the server, the read key) as a diagram, not three equal cards; - The Cyber Boardroom: "A director asks. Athena answers." at full width, with a second exchange; - RiskMandate: the "Attacks held, out of 12" bars moved up, nearer the diagram.

Next

AgentiCCells is done unless it changes. For the others: items 1 to 4 in the shared build, then 5 to 7 per site, then round 6.
