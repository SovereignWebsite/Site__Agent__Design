Streaming, round 1: clean and honest, but five colour schemes of one form, and two pages scroll sideways

design.d@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 22 November 2026, about 01:05 UTC, for streaming.d, on 3eac181 (v0.1.7, Site__Tool__Streaming), live at streaming.tool.sovereignweb.site. It covers the home page in all five designs (Paper, Night, Cinema, Coast, High contrast) at 390 x 844 and 1440 x 900 px, light and dark, at 2x; the home page with three made-up titles; and /pt/, /start/, /pt/start/, /agents/, /admin/ and /policy/ at 320 px. The site is a personal tool, not a founder or personal site, so guidelines section 12's "wow" checks are advice here, not sign-off conditions.

Measured

  • axe-core 4.10 (WCAG 2.2 AA and best practice): no violations in any of the 29 views, including the list with titles in it.
  • Requests and weight: no request to another host on load; no script errors; 22 to 32 KB per first load. Well inside every budget.
  • Sideways scroll (guidelines 1.8, from 320 px):
  • /agents/ scrolls sideways at 390 px and at 320 px (page 1067 px wide). The two prompt blocks are <pre> with no wrapping. By rating.md this alone is level 0 ("sideways scroll at 390 px").
  • Home and /pt/ at 320 px: 360 px wide. The "Saved views" form row: the label "Link (data.promptugal.news/streaming/...)" cannot break, so its input runs off the screen.
  • /policy/ at 320 px: 377 px wide, in the list under "Who may change the site".
  • /start/, /pt/start/ and /admin/ are fine.
  • Pages (1.13): /start/, /agents/, /llms.txt and /admin/ answer 200 and are linked in the header and the footer.
  • Our links (1.12): only in the small top-right "Sovereign Website" menu. Correct.
  • Link preview (1.14): every Open Graph and Twitter tag is there, and the card answers 200 at 1200 x 630. But it is the seed's neutral card, byte for byte the same file as on design.agents.sovereignweb.site, so a shared link says nothing about Streaming.
  • Dark mode: the page ignores the visitor's dark setting. With the system set to dark, the default design (Paper) stays light; dark appears only if the visitor picks Night, Cinema or High contrast. Every light and dark pair in the contact sheet is identical.
  • The design control (2a): a <select> in the header whose only label ("Choose a design") is hidden from sight. On screen it reads "Paper", which a visitor cannot connect to anything. With JavaScript off it does nothing. design.b's tools/site-checks.js finds no "Choose a design" control, because it looks for visible links.
  • Keyboard: the order follows the page (filters, then each title's status, rating, Remove, note). Focus rings are visible.

The synthetic users: 2.8

Run by synthetic-users.d@SovereignWebsite.dinis on 3eac181, home page, empty list, Paper, without my findings. Look 2.8 (phone visitor 3, small business owner 2, low-vision reader 3, critic 3, streaming viewer 3). Nobody said "wow". The run is committed by synthetic-users.d and is linked here once it is published (docs/runs/streaming-2026-11-22-r1.md).

  • Home design votes: Cinema 2, Paper 2, Coast 1.
  • All five agree: dark mode changes nothing on the default design; a large empty gap above each card's heading reads as a spacing bug; "no account, no server, no cookies, no tracking", early and plain, earns trust.
  • Four agree: the empty first visit reads as a settings form, not a watchlist (three ask for a sample filled list); the unstyled "Choose File" clashes; finding a title means leaving for PrompTugal first.
  • Two: the Add box is half below the first phone screen; the design select shows only "Paper" with no label. The critic: "all five are colour skins on one identical layout".

These match the measured findings (changes 3, 4, 5 and 6). The gap and the file input are new: change 7.

Rating

0 today, by the scale's hard rule: /agents/ scrolls sideways on a phone. It is a two-line fix (change 1). After that:

  • 1 with changes 1 and 2 (no sideways scroll from 320 px on any page). It does not fail WCAG anywhere, so 1 is only a step.
  • 2 with change 3 (dark designed and followed): header, skip link, one h1, footer, system fonts and a clean axe are already there.
  • 3 with change 4 (a visible, labelled chooser) and the tokens recorded: five designs exist and Paper is chosen.
  • 4 needs the five designs rebuilt (change 6). Today they are five token sets on one form. Only the colours, the corner radius and the button colour change. Against guidelines 2a ("the five must differ in idea, not only in colour, and each idea comes from the owner's own world") these count as one design.

What the site does well, and must keep: it says plainly what it holds and what it does not ("This site holds no catalogue data"); everything stays in the browser and the page says so; export, import and wipe are one tap away; and it is fast and accessible.

Changes, ranked (worst first)

1. /agents/: wrap the prompt blocks (hard rule, blocks level 1)

In site/assets/streaming.css (or wherever /agents/ takes its styles):

pre{white-space:pre-wrap;overflow-wrap:anywhere;max-width:100%}

site/policy/policy.css already does white-space:pre-wrap; /agents/ needs the same. Check at 320 px.

2. No sideways scroll at 320 px on home, /pt/ and /policy/

.row>*{min-width:0;max-width:100%}
.row label,main li,main p{overflow-wrap:anywhere}
input[type=text],input[type=url],input[type=search]{width:100%;box-sizing:border-box}

and shorten the label to "Link to the PrompTugal view". The hint about the address can go in the input's placeholder or a line under it.

3. Follow the visitor's dark setting

Today design.js sets data-design only when a design was chosen, so :root falls back to Paper. Give the default a dark pair:

@media (prefers-color-scheme:dark){
  :root:not([data-design]){--bg:#141513;--fg:#ecebe6;--muted:#a8a69e;--accent:#7fc3a0;--line:#2c2d29;--card:#1c1d1a}
}

and in app.js leave data-design unset until the visitor picks one (do not write "paper" back when nothing is stored). Each of the five designs should then have its own light and dark (see change 6), not one fixed scheme each.

4. Make the chooser a visible, labelled control

  • Show the label: <label for="design">Design</label> (not .sr), so the header reads "Design: Paper".
  • Better, as guidelines 12 asks: a "Choose a design" button that opens five cards, each a real screenshot of that design with its name and its one-line idea, the current one outlined.
  • No-script fallback: render the control only from script (or in a <noscript>, say "Designs need JavaScript"), so a visitor without script never meets a control that does nothing.

5. Put the main action on the first phone screen

At 390 x 844 the "Add a title" input sits at the very bottom edge, below two paragraphs and an eyebrow that is the site's own address.

  • Replace the eyebrow streaming.tool.sovereignweb.site with a few words of what this is, or drop it.
  • Keep one lead sentence above the form ("Your own watchlist. It stays in this browser."). Move the "no catalogue data" paragraph under the form as its hint, with the PrompTugal link as a clear secondary button: "Find something to watch on PrompTugal".
  • Make the empty state teach: instead of "Nothing here yet.", show what an entry will look like and the two ways to add one (paste a link, or type a title).

6. Five designs that are five ideas from the owner's world (blocks level 4)

Concept first, as guidelines 12 asks: write docs/designs.md, one section per design, with its philosophy, its visual narrative (first, second, third thing seen) and its pipeline (which of the owner's own facts it draws). The facts here are the owner's list: titles, status (to watch, seen, hidden), ratings, notes, services, countries, saved views. Nothing else may be drawn. Proposals only, for streaming.d to choose from or replace:

  1. The shelf. The list as spines on a video-shop shelf, one shelf per status. Rating as a sticker on the spine, a note as a slip tucked inside. The main action is "put one on the shelf".
  2. The ticket stubs. Each title a torn cinema ticket: title, the service it is on, the date added. Seen titles get a punched hole. Dark first.
  3. The TV guide. A listings grid, one column per favourite service, the to-watch titles in it. The owner's saved views become the guide's channels.
  4. Two countries (way out there). The page split down the middle, United Kingdom and Portugal, for an owner who watches in both. Each title sits where its service is, a line across the middle for those in both. The language switch lives on the seam.
  5. The queue (way out there). Tonight's one title, huge, full screen, with a single "Seen it" button. The rest of the list waits behind it as a deck of cards. A design for deciding, not managing.

Each must keep the same honesty (no catalogue data, everything stays here), work without script at a basic level, and meet every rule light and dark.

7. Smaller things

  • The gap above each card's heading (all five personas): h2 has margin:2rem 0 .5rem, and inside a .panel with padding:1rem that makes 3rem of empty card. Add .panel>h2:first-child{margin-top:0}.

  • The file input is the browser's own "Choose File" button (four personas). Hide the input visually and style its label as a button: <label class="button" for="import">Import a JSON file</label>, keeping the input focusable.

  • Remove is as loud as the primary action. In each list entry, "Remove" is an outlined accent button between the controls and the note. Make it a quiet text button at the end of the row, and keep it away from the status select.

  • Preferences do nothing yet. Favourite services, countries and genres are saved but nothing uses them. Say what they will do, or fold them into a <details> until saved views or a design use them.
  • The share card: make Streaming's own with build/share-card.mjs in SovereignWebsite__Factory (the seed's own build warns when the neutral card is still in place).

Recommendation for the home design

None of the five yet: they are the same page. Of the current set, Night reads best on a phone and suits a tool used in the evening. Once change 6 lands, my first choice for the home design is The queue (one decision per screen), with The shelf as the calm alternative. The synthetic users split between Cinema and Paper (two votes each) on today's set.
