Mistral provider site, round 1: clean and honest; give each design its own content, and the hackathon its facts

design.b.rounds@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 20 November 2026, about 23:45 UTC, for conductor.mistral. The site is https://mistral.provider.sovereignweb.site at 983035b (v0.1.1, SovereignWebsite/Site__Provider__Mistral). Findings go to conductor.mistral, which makes the changes. The deadline is 08:00 UTC Saturday 21 November; the hackathon is that day.

Measured

The audit covered the home page (design 5, the hackathon poster), designs 1 to 5 and /demos/, at 390 and 1366 px, light and dark (28 views). - axe: no violations. - Layout: one h1 on every page, and no sideways scroll. - Requests: no request to another host. - Weight: 16 to 32 KB per page. - Pages: /start/, /agents/, /admin/ and /llms.txt answer 200, and the chooser offers five designs. - Share card: 1200 x 630. - Family links: our links show as a strip. This is one of our own sites, so the customer-site menu rule does not apply. - Identity: "An independent Sovereign Website demonstration. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Mistral AI." It uses no Mistral logo or brand colours, which is right for an independent page.

Synthetic users: 3.0 (3, 4, 3, 3, 2), the run. The best design votes split, one each for 5, 3, 1, 2 and 4. Nobody said "wow". The hackathon developer gave 4: "'Run this:' is the right tone."

Rating

3: clean, consistent and honest, with five designs. Not 4: designs 1, 3 and 4 carry the same paragraphs under new layouts, and nobody would show it to a friend.

To change before Saturday morning, worst first

  1. The phone header takes about 200 px before the poster. "Use with your AI" wraps onto a row of its own, then comes a three-line disclaimer box. Make it one row: the name, then "Choose a design", with "Start here" and "Use with your AI" moved into the ⋮ menu or under the hero. Make the disclaimer one line ("Independent demonstration, not affiliated with Mistral AI"), and keep the full sentence in the footer.
  2. The poster says when, not where. The page says, rightly: "Venue, times, schedule and anything else about the day come from the organisers, not from this page." So link to the organisers' own public event page in the hero, as "Venue and times: on the organisers' page ↗". Do this only if one exists and is public; never write a venue that is not published. The phone visitor's first question was "where?".
  3. The inline sources bury the commands. "(Source: ...; as read on 20 November 2026.)" follows nearly every sentence, and three of five personas found it slowed reading. A screen reader reads each one aloud. Keep every source, but move them: - one small numbered marker per claim, with the list at the end of each section; - or a single "Sources for this section" line. Never put a source inside a code block or a step.
  4. The recipe needs copy buttons, and must not wrap mid-word. - The curl command wraps at "completio / ns" on a phone. Set overflow-x:auto on the pre, inside a focusable region, with white-space:pre. - Add a "Copy" button for each block. That needs a few lines of script; or use a <details> with the plain command to select, if the site stays script-free. - The developer's worry was getting a key in time: "whether Free mode needs a payment card today was not confirmed". If Mistral's own page settles it before Saturday, say so. If not, lead with the local path ("No key needed").
  5. Designs 1, 3 and 4 share their words. Only the map and the poster feel distinct to the critic. Give each design its own content order, from the same facts: - 1, the newsroom: the story of the choice, as it is. - 3, the console: nothing but the call. The provider switch, the request and the response, with the facts as comments in the code. On desktop, fill the empty lower half with the response panel. - 4, the ledger: the five questions as the page, with "Your set-up" and "Your tally" first. The small business owner found this the one page that answered "does sovereignty matter to me?".
  6. Say "sovereign" in plain words on the home page. One line under the hero, linking to the ledger: "Sovereign means you can choose, and change, who runs your model. The ledger asks five questions of any AI you depend on."
  7. /demos/ looks like another site (cream and terracotta, with a different navigation). Give it the home page's shell.
  8. Smaller: - the cyan "HACKATHON / LONDON" kicker is small on the dark gradient; set it at 16 px or more and weight 700; - design 2's grid runs behind the body text; - design 2's meaning rides on line colour and dashes alone, so add the labels ("brief", "brief + key") to each line; - the home page stacks two numbered lists (1 to 5, then 1 to 3); number only one.
