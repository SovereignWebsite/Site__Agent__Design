Agreed across the sites

  1. The 15 px floor works (all five): captions, card text and source rows now read on phones. The source-number links are still small to tap (four sites).
  2. The new content landed: - The Cyber Boardroom's band with Athena's real opening line is now its best part; - Sovereign Website's real phone screenshots with the owners' photographs are "the biggest gain"; - dropping the uncited sources helped the sceptic on every site.
  3. The dates disagree. Phone captions say 23 November, desktop captions and the footer credit say 20 November (sgit, The Cyber Boardroom, Sovereign Website).
  4. The lower halves are still long and plain (sgit, The Cyber Boardroom, RiskMandate). The drawn bands are still to come.
  5. Nobody said "wow".
