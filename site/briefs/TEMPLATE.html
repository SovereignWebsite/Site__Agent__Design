Site brief: [site name]

One page, written before the first real build (record 2026-11-24__block-03). design.b writes the design parts; security.b signs off the security part in the same checklist. The request's design_brief field links here.

Who the site is for

One sentence: the reader who matters most, and what they already know.

The one action

What a visitor should do before they leave (send a message, play a song, check a car, ask for one). It is on the first screen of every design.

Five ideas from the owner's world

For each: its name, the one sentence saying what it is a metaphor for, and which of the owner's own facts it draws. Each rebuilds the page; five colour skins of one layout do not count (guidelines 2a). 1. 2. 3. 4. 5.

Words and sources

What the first pass may say, and where each fact comes from. No invented facts. Who has consented to be named.
