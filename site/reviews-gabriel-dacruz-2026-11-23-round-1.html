Gabriel DaCruz, round 1: six strong first screens, one shared body, and no way to commission him

design.b.rounds@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 23 November 2026, about 15:35 UTC. Recorded for design.b to route once Dinis names the keeper. It is the first round on https://gabriel-dacruz.agenticcells.site/ at ddf6875 (v0.1.26); the repository is AgentiCCells__Site__Gabriel-DaCruz.

Measured

Checked: the six designs, the chooser /d/, and /works/, at 390 and 1366 px, light and dark (32 views). The six designs are: - Premiere, the home page; - The Studio; - The Sleeve; - The Cue Sheet; - The Score; - The Signal Chain.

  • axe, serious:
  • "scrollable-region-focusable" on all six designs. The credits box (.credits) scrolls but cannot be reached by keyboard. Give it tabindex="0", role="region" and an aria-label ("Credits"), or let it grow instead of scrolling.

  • Small targets:

    • the superscript source links (for example sup > a[href$="#s10"]) on Premiere, The Sleeve, The Cue Sheet and /works/;
    • the design links in the chooser line on The Score and The Signal Chain.

    Give each padding:4px 6px and display:inline-block, with a 24 px minimum. - axe, moderate: "heading-order" on Premiere, and "region" on The Studio. - One h1 each, no sideways scroll, nothing from another host. The videos say "nothing loads from YouTube before that" press, which is right. - Dark mode: the site does not change with the setting. Premiere, The Studio, The Sleeve and The Signal Chain are dark by design, and The Score and The Cue Sheet are light. That is fine as their ideas, but mark it in the chooser. - Phone length:

The synthetic users: 3.2

The run: 3, 3, 2, 4, 4. Its capture saved /d/ over the home page, so Premiere was judged only from its thumbnail. I have since checked Premiere's first phone screen myself: - his portrait out of the dark; - "COMPOSER · MULTI-INSTRUMENTALIST · MUSIC PRODUCER"; - the name large; - "THE BAY · WAR FLOWERS · THE GAUNTLET"; - one plain sentence; - "Watch: Back To The Future".

It is one of the strongest first screens of the people's sites.

All five personas chose the same best line: "He can take a song, write it out for a full orchestra, and then stand in front of that orchestra."

Rated 1 today, for the two serious axe findings only. Fix those and it is 3: six real first-screen ideas and a chooser. Like Silvio's and Vicky's sites, what holds it from 4 is the shared body below the fold and the length.

Maturity: M2 (M3 once fixed). It is carefully sourced, and it marks what is unconfirmed.

To fix, in order

  1. The credits box and the small targets, above. Two CSS rules.
  2. A way to commission him. The director persona's one wish: "a 'For directors' block near the top, with the three productions, a reel of cues and a way to get in touch". Today the only route is Instagram, "about lessons". The contact route is his call (ask through Dinis). Until then, put the three productions and the cue reel together near the top of each design.
  3. "TO CONFIRM", once per page. It appears on every design ("his exact role on each is TO CONFIRM") and in /works/ ("Initials match the film; his role: to confirm"), and four personas read it as unfinished. Keep it honest, but say it once, in one note under the screen work ("His credits on these films are being confirmed with him"), and drop the repeated tags.
  4. Each design its own body. Below the fold, all six share the same drawings, decay chart, poster cards, reel chart, SoundCloud cards and video grid. A few already have their own ideas below the fold, and they show the way: - The Cue Sheet's "1M1 / 1M2 / 2M1" contents and "EXT. THE ALGARVE" scenes; - The Sleeve's Side A and Side B; - The Signal Chain's rack; - The Score's movements and plates.

Carry each through the whole page, and leave out what does not fit it. 5. Music on the first screen of every design. The phone visitor wanted to "put a play button for one track or video on the first screen". Premiere does this; The Studio's first button is a video essay, and The Score has none. 6. Phone layouts: - the "Same facts, six designs" line wraps to two or three lines; put it behind the "Designs" button, which is already there; - the poster strip shows one and a half posters with no sign that it scrolls; add a visible edge or arrows; - the /works/ table is cut at "SPOT"; let it scroll inside a focusable region, or stack it as cards on phones. 7. Blank tiles. Video tiles and posters were black or blank in the full-page captures; give each tile its real thumbnail as the poster. 8. Under 8,000 px on a phone, which items 3 and 4 mostly do.
