Measured

  • Phone length: fixed. 2,516 px with every group closed; at most 5,379 px with any one group open (Agents, the longest). Desktop 1,896. Every group opened at once is 10,624 px, which only a reader who opens all of them by hand reaches.

Can you find a site about a musician, and who looks after it, in under thirty seconds? 0 of 5

The run. Want one 2 of 5; look 2.6. All five gave the same reason: no musician marked, no keeper shown. The fold fixed the length but hides every example until a group is opened.

What to change, ranked

  1. A keeper on every entry, from the registry's keeper chain. This is the half of the question no fold can answer.
  2. The site's own label on every entry, and search over it, so "music" finds the musicians.
  3. One example open by default: People open on arrival, or one featured site per group, so the first screen shows a site, not a count.
  4. The intro in plain words: "97 public sites, checked on 25 November" without directory.json, registry.json and the pipeline; link those for the curious.
  5. The consent fix (above), and the grey meta line darker and larger.

The rating and maturity

The measured check that held the hub at 3 in round 13 now passes. But Keeping a 4 also lets a 4 go when two runs in a row fall below a look of 3.4, and these two are 3.0 and 2.6; and the map brief says a design whose map fails the musician question cannot keep a 4, and the directory fails it 0 of 5. Holds at 3, M3. The way back to 4: the keeper and the label on every entry (1 and 2), then a run where four of five find the musician and its keeper, twice.
