Hub, round 9: the case works (The Line 1/5 to 3/5); "I want one" goes nowhere

design.b.rounds@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 23 November 2026, about 13:40 UTC, for design.b and hub.b. On https://hub.sovereignweb.site/ at 4f23d10 (v0.1.35), with three designs caught at 5c6bb6a (v0.1.36) as it deployed. design.b asked: run "would you want one?" with The Line as home and the case on its first screen, against The Street's 3/5 from round 8, "since this decides which design is home".

Measured

  • axe: none, on all five, at 390 and 1366 px, light and dark. One h1 each, no sideways scroll.
  • Weight on a phone: 0.24 to 0.30 MB.
  • "I want one" leads to /start/#get-one: "There is no request form yet. Read how it works and the prices on sovereignwebsite.ai, and see the sites made so far."
  • /features/ and /more/ answer 404 (with a GitHub 503 on the first try). I will run the "can it do Spotify?" check when /features/ is tagged.

Would you want one?

The run, every answer verbatim.

Design Round 8 Round 9
The Line (home) 1/5 3/5
The Register 1/5 2/5
The Gallery 1/5 1/5
Contact Sheet 1/5 1/5
The Street 3/5 3/5

Which is home. The case first screen moved The Line from the least wanted design to joint first, level with The Street. On the yes count alone, this run does not separate the two.

Two things tip it to The Line: - it carries the brief's case and "I want one" on the first screen, and The Street does not yet; - the low-vision reader called its button "the easiest button on any of these pages to find".

So keep The Line as home, and judge again in round 10 with the case ported into The Street (brief, step 6). If The Street then passes The Line, swap them.

Rated 3. No design has four yeses.

The decisive finding: "I want one" satisfied 0 of 25

Every persona who pressed it reached "There is no request form yet" and a link to another site for prices. The two top reasons for "no" are the same thing seen twice:

Reason Mentions in 15 noes
No way to actually ask for one 11
No price on the page 9
Unproven, only one site handed over 5
The design says "not for someone like me" 4
Doesn't say who I deal with or who looks after it 3
Hard to read 2

The verbatim answers are in the run. hub.b's list, in that order:

  1. A way to ask, and the price, on the hub itself. These two decide the next round more than any design change. Both are Dinis's decisions (brief, "I want one"; strategy.b for the price). - The page cannot honestly add a form or a price until he decides. - Until then, put the price from sovereignwebsite.ai on the hub, quoted with its link and the date checked, in place of "read the prices on sovereignwebsite.ai". The persona renting from a website builder said: "My builder at least shows a monthly price."
  2. Proof. "1 handed over" out of 33 is read as one customer, in every one of the sceptic's noes. This is a fact, not a design fault. - Lead with the eleven people's sites (brief, step 3, and /more/ for the rest). The 33 includes demos and "made-up companies", and that dilutes the proof. - Say what happens after hand-over in one line: who looks after the site, and what it costs then. The page does not say, and three personas asked.
  3. "Not for someone like me." The Register reads as a land registry, The Gallery as an art gallery, Contact Sheet as "for photographers". A tradesperson does not see himself in any of them. Port the case first screen into all four (brief, step 6). In each, show one non-artist's site near the top, if one exists among the eleven and its owner agrees.
  4. The Gallery and Contact Sheet stay dark in light mode (rounds 8 and 9). Give each a light variant.
  5. "Want one?" is grey on near-black in The Gallery, and sits a screen and a half down on The Register, The Gallery and The Street. When the case is ported, it moves up with it.
