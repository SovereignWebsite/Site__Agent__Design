Measured

  • axe: no violations in 40 views.
  • Layout: no sideways scroll at 320, 390 or 1366 px.
  • Our links: in the "⋮" menu at the top right (1.12).
  • Round 3's fixes are in:
  • the stills' titles sit in a bar under each still;
  • Libretto is left-aligned, with its years back;
  • the first-screen strip is new;
  • the tour is newest first.
  • The tour's "this week": Richmond (16 to 21 November) carries it today. Two notes on how it is done: 1. new Date().toISOString().slice(0,10) is UTC, not the visitor's clock as the comment says. Near midnight it is a day out for visitors east of London. Build today from getFullYear(), getMonth() and getDate(). 2. The label is a CSS ::after, which not every screen reader announces. Add a real <span class="this-week">(this week)</span> from the script instead.

Rating

3. Four needs a look score of 4 or more.

What holds the look (three or more personas)

  1. Her agent is at the very foot of every page, after the 26-venue tour. Add the agency's name and "Call" and "Email" links to the hero, next to "Contact her agent". The casting director's one change: "put the agent's phone and email in the hero".
  2. All five open on the same hero parts in the same order: the eyebrow, the name, the subtitle, the range line, two buttons, then Spotlight and Instagram. Change the order and the parts per idea: - Editorial: the cover with cover lines. Put the name in the masthead, above the face, not across her mouth on desktop. - Fringe flyer: the title of the show first, her name second, as a flyer bills it. - Footlights: "Tonight", then the role, then her name in the light. - Libretto: the title page, "Dramatis personae" first. - Tour diary: the current venue and date first, then the map.
  3. The tour map is clutter on a phone (three). On phones below 600 px, show the list and hide the map behind "Show the map" (a details). The dashed route lines read as tangled; draw only the dots, and the route on desktop.
  4. "Grace is a cover…" appears three times on every page. Keep it once, in the tour section. Put the ticket link beside the "(this week)" venue as "Tickets: on the tour's page ↗", not inside a paragraph.
  5. Design 4's phone headshot is a thumbnail. Show it at full width on phones, under the venue line.

Smaller

  • Design 1 files the National Theatre Studio My Fair Lady workshop under "Act Three: Studio", beside Diablo IV. A workshop of a stage musical belongs in "Act One: Stage".
  • Design 2 says "WATCH", then "WATCH HER". Keep one.
  • Design 3's fleurons and "In which our soprano is introduced": keep them if Grace likes them. The critic found them pastiche.
