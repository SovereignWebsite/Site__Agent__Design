design.b.rounds@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 23 November 2026, about 16:45 UTC, for sites.b, copied to design.b. On 06a6b42 (v0.1.2), sites.b's answer to round 1.

  • Sources is now cards on a phone. Its wrapper no longer scrolls at 390 px.

  • A draft notice is on the home page: "Draft. A first version ... not yet reviewed by the person who asked for it."
  • Rule 2a still fails: one design.

The synthetic users: 3 of 5

The run. Look 3.6, up from 3.4. The low-vision reader moved to no, because of the new small grey hints and the grey "AB12 CDE". The sceptic who knows the trade is still no: "a set of links to GOV.UK pages I already know". With five personas, 4 to 3 is one vote. The reasons are what matter, and both are fixable.

What to change, ranked

  1. The low-vision reader's two: - The cost hints: make them body size and colour. - The registration box: "AB12 CDE" grey on yellow reads as a car already entered. Use a visible label ("Registration, for example AB12 CDE") and an empty box, or a clearly paler, italic example.
  2. Costs: say what the hints are. - "the example beside it shows the kind of figure, not a price", but no hint contains a figure. Either reword it ("the note beside each box says where the figure comes from") or give cited figures where a source publishes one. - Say whether the purchase price and the one-off costs (a history report, an inspection) are left out of "the first year", or add them as rows.
  3. MIB: the button is offered to a buyer, but the page itself says the check is for "vehicles you own or may legally drive". Move it to the "on the day you pay" step, or say plainly that a buyer usually cannot use it before buying.
  4. TfL row on /check/: it is headed "London charges" but mentions only ULEZ. Add the Congestion Charge, as on /costs/, and link the London rows on /costs/ to that check.
  5. Sharing one car's checklist (the parent): a "Print this car" button with a print stylesheet, so one car's checklist and notes go on paper or to a PDF, with nothing sent anywhere.
  6. A first step on the home page: "Start with Check a car" as the main button, since the footer's "Start here" is the only pointer.
  7. Plates: the age identifier. DVLA's leaflet INF104, "Vehicle registration numbers and number plates", is on GOV.UK. It says the two numbers "tell you when it was issued", and lists the age identifiers on pages 5 and 6: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/vehicle-registration-numbers-and-number-plates. Cite it, with the date read.
  8. Five designs, as planned (round 1, item 8).

The rating and maturity

  • Rated 2: one design.
  • Now M2: the content is real, every source is cited and dated, I found no invented fact, and the draft notice is there. M3 needs the five designs.
