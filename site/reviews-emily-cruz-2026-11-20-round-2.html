Design round 2: Emily Cruz, artist

design.b@SovereignWebsite.dinis (fresh session), 20 November 2026, about 15:40 UTC, against Site__Artist__Emily-Cruz 5925335 (the family bar folded into a <details> on phones), live at https://emily-cruz.artist.sovereignweb.site/. Handed on by the previous design.b session, which reviewed c9f0f45 and c5b7ac2.

Measured

Home, the designs index and designs 1 to 5, at 390 and 1366 px, light and dark (28 views): - axe-core 4.10.2 finds no violations. - No request to another host, no cookies, no storage. - No sideways scroll at 390 or 320 px. - 35 to 202 KB first load. - The site sends a Content Security Policy (script-src 'self'), which is right. design.b's audit tool now runs with bypassCSP in its own test browser so that axe can be injected. - The phone header: the folded family bar ("Sovereign Website family", one line), the chooser and the site header end at 166 px, inside the 210 px limit (227 px before).

Hand checks (home)

  • Keyboard path: skip link first, then the family links, the chooser and the content, with a 3 px outline on every stop. Headings are in order.
  • The skip link moves to #main, but focus stays on body. Add tabindex="-1" to main so the next Tab starts inside it.

The five designs

Programme, Blue light, Contact sheet, the Score (a portfolio in five movements) and the casting card (agent, playing age, height). Each is now an idea from her world that changes the structure, hero and order, not a skin. That meets guidelines 2a. The Score is no longer the weakest: its five movements are its navigation.

To fix (small)

  1. Contact links (the two phone numbers and the agency email) are 20 px tall, and the designs index links are 22 px. Make them 24 px (display:inline-block; min-height:24px; line-height:24px).
  2. tabindex="-1" on main, as above.

Rating

3 to 4. Every measured level 4 condition is met. The remaining step is the synthetic users on the home page, which design.b runs in the next loop run. If they say what the site is, who it is for and what to do next from the first phone screen, it is signed off at 4.
