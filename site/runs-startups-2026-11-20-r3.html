Synthetic users: the five startup brochures, round 3

Sites: sgit (6c1fcae), The Cyber Boardroom (344e189), RiskMandate (3b27943), AgentiCCells (115fd9f), Sovereign Website (49f41b9), all v0.1.8. Run: 20 November 2026, about 23:05 UTC, by design.b.rounds. The review.

Site Look Scores The best part, in the personas' words
sgit 3.2 3, 3, 3, 3, 4 The dark "Plaintext here. Ciphertext there." band is "the best moment"; the developer liked the four-line quickstart.
The Cyber Boardroom 3.0 3, 3, 3, 3, 3 "Meet Athena" is clear; the director found "What questions should I ask my CISO?" "exactly my question".
RiskMandate 3.4 4, 3, 3, 3, 4 The headline and green button "do their job quickly"; the attacks bar is "the best graphic".
AgentiCCells 3.4 4, 3, 3, 3, 4 "One cell. One job. Your stop button." and the dark stat band are "close to wow".
Sovereign Website 2.8 3, 3, 2, 3, 3 The live screenshots of named owners' sites are "the strongest trust signal".

Method: Synthetic users. One agent per site played all five personas in turn, with the chooser captured with its thumbnails loaded.

A fault in this run's set-up. The five agents ran at once and two of them first wrote their crops into one shared folder. Each noticed another site's images there, discarded those crops and redid its own in a private folder; their verdicts are about their own site only. The next run gives each agent its own folder from the start.

These are simulations: they inform a rating, and never replace the measured checks or a real person.

Agreed across the five sites

  1. The source numbers in the body text clutter reading (all five sites).
  2. "Choose a design" and "⋮" in the header read as tools, not content (four sites).
  3. The calls to action are clear (all five).
  4. "Nothing is sold yet" and "first proposal" are honest but leave a buyer nothing to do (RiskMandate, AgentiCCells, Sovereign Website).
  5. Shrunk or cropped screenshots still read as broken on phones (sgit, The Cyber Boardroom).
