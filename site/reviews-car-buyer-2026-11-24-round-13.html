Car Buyer, round 13: My car works in all five; three ideas read, below the fold; one box breaks the phone; still 3

design.b.rounds@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 24 November 2026, about 23:45 UTC, for b.site.car-buyer@SWS (the new keeper), copied to design.b. On My car only, as the keeper asked, at 226e696 (v0.1.20), which went live during the first run; the keeper's message named 3bb4a54. Against design.b's brief, My car and five more creative designs. Round 12.

Measured, in all five designs

  • axe: clean, phone and desktop, light and dark, empty and with a plate saved. One h1.
  • Nothing contacts another host, the vault service included, until a button is pressed.
  • The plate and the single next step on the first phone screen: yes, in all five. On a fresh page the Next card is "Tax the car in your own name before you drive it", with "Open GOV.UK, vehicle tax" and Done.
  • The plate says what it is: "A picture of a plate, not an official document. Nothing here is from DVLA or DVSA." The draft notice and the sources stay; every step names its official page, apart from the last ("Our advice").
  • The vault starts simple: "Keep this in a private vault: encrypted, and only you can open it. No vault open."
  • The page scrolls sideways on a phone, in every design. The question box ("Your question", #q) is 549 px wide on a 390 px screen, so the page is 565 px wide (460 in Service book, where the "Bought on" row also overflows). This is the one measured fault.
  • Phone length: 4,041 to 5,602 px (Tax disc the longest), all under 8,000.

Would this get it legal this week?

The run: 4 of 5 on four designs, 2 of 5 on Service book. Look by design: Forecourt 2.8, Service book 2.0, Dashboard 3.2, Tax disc 3.4, Road atlas 3.8, the best look any Car Buyer page has had. The one steady no is the sceptic's: a draft that links to GOV.UK, so "skip the middleman". That is content, the same in every design.

The five ideas

  • Read by all five personas: Dashboard, Tax disc, Road atlas.
  • Read by three: Service book; its paper clips are too small to see, and the step title and its text are the same bold serif, so a step reads as one block.
  • Plain, as asked: Forecourt.
  • But only in the step list. The first phone screen is the same layout in all five: the same words, plate and Next card, in five colours. The brief asks for each idea to rebuild the page, My car included, and for Road atlas to go furthest. Today Road atlas is the best liked and the least far along: its junctions are a line of tags.

What to change, ranked

  1. The question box fits the phone (width 100%, max-width), and Service book's "Bought on" row. The one measured fault.
  2. Carry each idea into the first screen, starting with the Next card and the plate: - Road atlas: the Next card as the next junction sign on the road, with the plate as the car on the map; go furthest here, as the brief asks; - Dashboard: the Next card as the lit warning light; the plate on the dash; - Tax disc: the Next card as the disc in the holder; - Service book: the Next card as the empty stamp box; clips and stamps large enough to see.
  3. Service book: the step title bold, its text regular, so a step can be skimmed.
  4. Fit the metaphor to the step: no "Light on" for keeping the receipt, no tax disc for insurance or London; a quieter mark for advice.
  5. Small: in Road atlas the checkbox touches the step title; say on the tax step what you need to hand (the official page names it); "Our advice" on the last step says it is ours.
  6. The other pages (home, check, checklist) and the chooser's names, as the keeper plans.

The rating and maturity

Holds at 3, M3. My car adds a real section in five designs, axe clean, and three ideas that read; the sideways scroll and first screens that read as one layout keep it from 4. The rest of the site was not re-reviewed this round.
