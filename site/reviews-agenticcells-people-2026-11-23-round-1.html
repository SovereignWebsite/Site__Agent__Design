Four AgentiCCells people's sites, round 1: Marc Noah, Indi Gupte, José Bota, Mondweep Chakravorty

design.b.rounds@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 23 November 2026, about 16:05 UTC. These are recorded for design.b to route once Dinis names the keepers. It is the first round on each. Indi, José and Mondweep had never had one.

Marc Noah: rated 3; the cleanest of the people's sites

Six designs, each an idea from his world: - Editorial; - The Coast, with a drawn map of the Algarve; - Azulejo, each song a tile; - Tour Poster; - The Songbook; - Side A, Side B, a cassette inlay.

axe finds nothing, and the pages are light. The personas' best part is "Editorial's photo hero and live-film button".

  1. The counts disagree. The Tour Poster says "Has played them in 25 places since 2023", but its own list starts at "22 OCT 2022 LOULÉ Cine Teatro Louletano", and Editorial says "40 concerts ... since October 2022". Use one count, from one source, on every design. The poster's marquee also repeats "RIBEIRA GRANDE".
  2. Booking in one card, on every design. Formats, crew and one film per format, with the label's email (Editorial already has this). The Coast gives only "Book through his Epopeia artist page".
  3. Empty frames: - Azulejo's album cover; - the Songbook's "Also" polaroid; - the cassette's "Liner notes"; - and black video boxes.

Give each its image, or remove the frame. 4. Footnotes off the hero. The numerals ("sings. 1 2 3") break the first lines. Keep the sources, below the fold. 5. Each design its own body. Azulejo, Tour Poster, The Songbook and Side A, Side B share one body, word for word. The poster's date grid and the cassette's two sides show the way.

Indi Gupte: rated 1 for two axe findings, 3 once fixed

Five designs: - The Briefing; - Lingua, with trilingual headings; - Front Page, a newspaper; - Agent Console; - The Card, a single card.

  1. The "Designs" button has no name on phones (axe "link-name", serious, on four designs). On a phone its text is hidden and only an icon with aria-hidden is left. Add aria-label="Choose a design" to .designs-btn.
  2. Front Page's wide menu is #ede7da on #fbfaf6, 1.17:1 (axe, serious). Give the links the body colour.
  3. His own part. Three personas found the work told through his agents ("27 AI agents", "277 commits by his agents"), and could not see what he did himself. Lead each design with what he did, in his words if he has given them.
  4. The footnotes are "honest but too heavy". Fewer on the first screen.

José Bota: rated 2; one design, no chooser

The home page's hero is clear and confident: a promise, his photo, three facts, and Email and LinkedIn on the first screen. axe finds nothing serious.

  1. Five designs (rule 2a). There is one design, and no chooser. Rule 2a holds a site at 2 until there are five, each an idea from his world. For an automation expert, these come from his own words: - a workflow drawn as a pipeline; - a before and after of one process; - a run log; - a plain CV page; - the current home.
  2. Below the fold the page fades (four personas): grey low-contrast text and pale cards. Check them against 4.5:1. Give the lower half the hero's confidence.
  3. The Web Summit photo is a black box on a phone. Check it loads at phone size.
  4. Dark mode: the home page has none, though the photos page has. Add it, or say the design is light only.

Mondweep Chakravorty: rated 1 for two axe findings, 3 once fixed

Five designs: - home, a timeline; - Blueprint, a drawing sheet; - Broadsheet; - Run Log; - Vault, a policy ring.

  1. Small source links on the home page (#ref-3, #ref-4, axe "target-size", serious). Give them 24 px targets.
  2. Blueprint's schedule table scrolls but cannot be reached by keyboard (axe, serious). Give the wrapper tabindex="0", role="region" and an aria-label.
  3. About him, not only the run. Four personas found it is "about one proof of concept more than about the person", with terms not explained ("SGit", "Ruflo", "mandate", "Agent Behaviour Policy"). Put one line on who he is and what he does before the run. Explain each term the first time it appears, in eight words or fewer.
  4. His photo on every design. Blueprint and Run Log, which lack it, "feel colder".
  5. Doubled superscripts ("12 7") and stray "3 4" lines on phones: merge them into one note each.

For all four

  • The shared body below the fold. As on Vicky's, Silvio's and Gabriel's sites, the designs are real ideas on the first screen and one body below it. That, and the phone length, is what stands between them and 4.
  • The older seed. These are AgentiCCells Factory sites: /start/ and /admin/ are not there.
  • Contact:
  • Marc's routes all go through his label;
  • Indi's only through LinkedIn ("No email or phone: please use LinkedIn");
  • José's and Mondweep's are on the page.

Each is the owner's choice. Note it, do not change it.

On the maturity levels. None of the four carries the draft banner that M2 asks for; these sites predate it. Marc, Indi and Mondweep cite a source for every claim, so I have placed them by their sources, at M2, and Marc at M3 for his rating. José's page cites few sources, so it stays at M1 until it does.
