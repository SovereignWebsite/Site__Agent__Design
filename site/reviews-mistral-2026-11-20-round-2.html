Mistral provider site, round 2: round 1 answered; small footnotes, a clipped console, two old headers

design.b.rounds@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 21 November 2026, about 00:00 UTC, for conductor.mistral, on 6cbfa89 (v0.1.9). The audit covered the home page, designs 1 to 5, /demos/, and the new /team/, /evals/ and /evals/run/, at 390 and 1366 px, light and dark (40 views). /swarm/ is not yet live.

Round 1, answered

  • The header is one row on phones, and the disclaimer is one line ("Independent demonstration, not affiliated with Mistral AI.").
  • One plain line under the hero says what sovereign means and links to the ledger.
  • Sources are now numbered footnotes, out of the sentences.
  • The recipe has a Copy button and leads with the no-key local path.
  • The console is just the call, with the facts as comments in the code.
  • The ledger now opens with "Does sovereignty matter to you? Five questions" and "Your set-up".
  • The venue is not invented; it has gone to Dinis as a question.
  • No request goes to another host, nothing scrolls sideways, and pages are 18 to 37 KB.

To fix

  1. Target size (serious), designs 1 and 2. The new footnote markers are 7 x 17 px links, below WCAG 2.2's 24 px minimum (axe: a[href^="#fn"], #fn-9 > a). Give each marker display:inline-block;min-width:24px;min-height:24px;text-align:center, or put 24 px between neighbouring markers. Keep them small to look at.
  2. The console's code is clipped, not scrollable. On a 390 px phone the request block is 1,476 px wide in a 358 px box, with overflow-x: visible. The long lines are cut off, for example "https://api.eu.mistral.ai/v1/chat/completion". Use pre{overflow-x:auto} with tabindex="0", role="region" and aria-label="The request", so it scrolls and keyboard users can reach it. Or wrap the comment lines (white-space:pre-wrap on the comments only).
  3. /team/ and /evals/ keep the old two-row phone header and the long disclaimer (as you noted). Give them the shared shell.
  4. /evals/: - "Read this first" is a good honest box. - The suite results need a one-line plain summary at the top before the tables, for example "On these small tests, every model passed the About job; the JSON job split them". - Write that line only from the run's own figures.

Rating

3 once item 1 is done; it is held at 2 until then, by the serious target-size failure. The synthetic users run again in round 3, once /swarm/ is live and items 1 to 3 are in, before 08:00 UTC.
