Design pass: Site__pipeline-demo

design.b@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 20 November 2026, against dev 57b9d89 (first made on 7fa98f0, re-applied after the groups and private-site text). Waiting on Dinis's go before it goes to sites.b. Not committed anywhere.

What changes

Words are unchanged. site/ here is the whole proposed site; changes.diff is the diff against 57b9d89; screens/ has before and after sheets (phone light, phone dark, desktop light, desktop dark).

  1. The family look. design 1's tokens (warm paper, terracotta, Georgia headlines), as on sovereignwebsite.ai, in place of the old green. All values measured: muted/bg 7.1:1, accent/bg 5.7:1 (light), 9.0 and 8.7 (dark).
  2. A site header. A "Pipeline demo" brand linking home, and the three pages as the site's nav with the current page underlined. The eyebrow stays above the h1.
  3. The request is shown as the ticket. The JSON on "Asking for a site" sits in design 1's dashed ticket and wraps, so nothing is cut off at any width; it is keyboard focusable and labelled.
  4. Steps as numbered circles joined by a line (the .ai component). The timeline and the fields table become stacked rows on phones, with the time or field name in the accent.
  5. Body text in --fg, the lead paragraph in muted at a larger size, sections divided by a rule above each h2.
  6. Family bar with explicit colours (8.96:1 light, 9.99:1 dark), no opacity, .875rem text, 24 px tall links. This is the fix to propose for seed/shared/family-bar.css too.
  7. color-scheme meta, a terracotta favicon.svg, titles of the form "Page, Pipeline demo", the footer as a real landmark outside main.

Checked

Served locally and run through the same audit as the live sites: axe-core 4.10.2 (WCAG 2.2 AA plus best practice) finds no violations on any of the three pages, in any of the four views (it found 8 contrast failures and 1 unreachable scroll region before). No horizontal overflow at 390 px; no requests to other hosts; no cookies or storage. On a phone the h1 now starts about 190 px down, and the first sentence is on the first screen.

Known and left

  • A long code token (requests/<date>__<label>/site.json) breaks mid-word on a 390 px phone. Acceptable; a <wbr> after each / would make it break cleanly if sites.b prefers.
  • Whether the demos use .ai's terracotta or a sibling accent is Dinis's choice (review point 1). This pass uses terracotta; only the token block changes if he picks otherwise.

Sign-off

Once sites.b has made these changes and they are live, design.b re-runs the audit on the live address and records the verdict in design/reviews/pipeline-demo/.
