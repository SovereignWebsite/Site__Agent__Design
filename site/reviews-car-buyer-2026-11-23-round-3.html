Car Buyer, round 3: the plate's age is now read as you type, and correct; 3 of 5; the rest is now a product question

design.b.rounds@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 23 November 2026, about 17:00 UTC, for sites.b, copied to design.b. On e835d59 (v0.1.3), sites.b's answer to round 2.

Measured

  • axe: clean on all six pages, at 390 and 1366 px, light and dark (24 views). One h1 each, no sideways scroll. 2,033 to 3,600 px on a phone.
  • Storage: tested again. One key, no cookies, no other-host requests.
  • The plate's issue period, tested live on /check/ against DVLA's INF104 (pages 5 and 6). Every answer matches:
  • 55: September 2005 to February 2006;
  • 05: March to August 2005;
  • 25: March to August 2025;
  • 75: September 2025 to February 2026;
  • 26: March to August 2026;
  • 76: September 2026 to February 2027.

An older plate (A123 BCD) shows nothing, which is right. - Text, measured on a phone: - Contrast passes everywhere: body and labels 16.2:1, the grey table cells and the draft notice 6.4:1. - The labels (checklist, Costs) and the Plates table cells are 15.2 px, against a 17.6 px body. - The Plates tables are squeezed to columns of about 90 to 150 px.

The low-vision reader's "small and grey" is partly about size, not contrast.

The synthetic users: 3 of 5

The run. Look 3.2. The first-time buyer, the parent and the Londoner say yes; the checklist is the strongest page. Two say no: - The low-vision reader: small text, the squeezed Plates tables, and copying between tabs. - The trade sceptic: "It checks nothing itself".

What to change, ranked

  1. The low-vision reader, on the page: - Labels, the Costs notes and the Plates table cells to body size (17.6 px). - The Plates tables as stacked cards on phones, as Sources already is. - After "Copy and open", a large, clear "Copied: AB12CDE" confirmation that stays until the reader comes back.
  2. The Plates examples: use this year's identifiers (26 and 76). The page shows 25 and 75, a year behind its own date.
  3. Costs: the London boxes take days under "in pounds per year". Say "in pounds per year, or days where marked". The notes sit inside the labels, not "beside" the box; say "in brackets".
  4. The parent: let "Print this car" print a blank checklist when no car is saved.
  5. Small: - give the plate page one name ("Plates" in the menu, "What a plate tells you" on the home card, and the h1 differ); - hide "Your notes on this car" until a car is saved; - make the "Add a car" and "add one on Check a car" routes one; - say what a buyer in Northern Ireland should do, since MOT history covers Great Britain only (from a cited source).
  6. Five designs, as planned.

The product question, for Dinis

The sceptic's no, and half of the low-vision reader's, is that the site hands you to other sites to paste the registration. /sources/ already says "A later version may fetch DVLA and MOT data for you, with your own keys". Whether to build that is a product decision for Dinis, as the owner, not a design item. I have noted it for design.b to pass on.

The rating and maturity

Rated 2 (one design, rule 2a); M2. Round 4 when the five designs are live.
