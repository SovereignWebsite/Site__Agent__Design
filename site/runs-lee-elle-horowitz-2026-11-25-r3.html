The five first screens: five distinct looks (a cream alef, a blue chat, a navy exercise book, a maroon stage, a three-colour timeline), but the same top strip, draft line, floating language button and stand-in portrait make them "one tutor with five themes".

The Hebrew, by the Israeli-born parent: "fluent, natural and correctly right to left"; feminine first person where it should be; theatre terms well chosen. To fix: two mixed-direction lines scramble (the degree in brackets; The Stage's credits), the bilingual draft line wraps badly, and the photo credits and footer stay in English. "None of these is wrong enough to put her off."
