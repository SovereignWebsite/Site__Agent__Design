Car Buyer, the Insurance page: careful and sourced, but it never says what to do first; 1 of 5

Measured

  • axe clean in all five designs, 390 and 1366 px, light and dark. One h1, no sideways scroll, nothing loaded from another host.
  • Phone length as loaded: 4,183 to 4,821 px across the five designs, under 8,000. Every section opened by hand: about 12,800 px; not counted, as on the hub's directory.
  • Ten folded sections, two open on arrival ("The short answer" and "Can an agent find insurance for you?"); "What a buyer should know" is folded, fifth.
  • 14 sources: 13 answer 200; developer.aviva.co.uk gave no response from here, which may be this machine's network, so unverified, not broken.
  • Dates disagree: the page says "Read on 26 November 2026"; the site-wide footer, on this page too, "Sources read on 23 November 2026".
  • A heading that says the opposite of what follows: under "What an agent can honestly do today:" the first item is MoneySuperMarket's terms prohibiting "any robot, bot, spider, scraper". That is what an agent cannot do.
  • The design chooser (guidelines 2a, 25 November): Car Buyer's chooser is a labelled select, on this page and the home page; the rule asks for one "Choose a design" button opening a panel of real screenshots. A site-wide gap, not this page's.

Would this page help you get the car insured this week? 1 of 5

The run; look 3.2. Only the comparison-site user said yes. Every persona trusted it ("We name no best deal", the read dates); four could not find what to do.

What to change, ranked

  1. A "Do this first" block at the top, open: insure it before you drive it away (GOV.UK, [5]); get quotes; choose the cover and the use; check the record on the MIB's database ([8]). Each step with its source, as the rest of the page has.
  2. Reorder and refold: "What a buyer should know" open and second; the agents and APIs sections folded, last.
  3. Fix the agents heading: "What an agent can do today" should list what it can do; put the MoneySuperMarket terms under "What an agent cannot do".
  4. The cases people have, each from a source or marked as not covered: young and first-time drivers, adding a car to a policy (multi-car), named drivers, the no-claims discount, cover beyond third party.
  5. One date: the footer's 23 November made per page, or the page's own read date used.
  6. Readable: darker body text and the "Our reading" notes; source markers large enough to tap; fewer folds.
  7. Site-wide: the design chooser to the 25 November rule.

The rating and maturity

Car Buyer holds at 3, M3. The page is clean on every measure; its persona result (1 of 5) is the page's, and the fixes are content and order, not design.
