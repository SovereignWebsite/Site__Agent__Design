What holds the look (three or more personas)

  1. The video stills: - the titles are printed over the images (the home page's pink "At the microphone" on a near-white still); - two captions are the same, word for word; - one still appears twice. Put the title and "Play on …" in a bar under each still, not on it. Give each still its own caption, and show each still only once.
  2. Libretto is the weakest for four of five. It has small grey serif details, small capitals and justified text with gaps. Use left-aligned text (no text-align:justify), detail text at the body colour and 15 px or more, and small capitals for role names only. Give the credits their years again: they dropped them.
  3. The header chrome ("Choose a design", "Sovereign Website") reads as not hers (four). It is required on her site (guidelines 12), so make it quieter. Make "Choose a design" a small outline button, and put our links behind the "⋮", as on Kate's site.

Smaller

  • "Actor-singer and soprano" is said twice on the first screen; keep the strip and drop the line above it.
  • The theatre-goer noticed that today falls within Richmond's dates. Mark the venue whose dates include today as "this week" at build time, from the tour's own dates. Never mark it "with Grace", since she is a cover.
  • Design 4 lists credits oldest first, and the casting director wanted newest first. Keep the journey order, but put the newest credit first, at the top of the stubs.
