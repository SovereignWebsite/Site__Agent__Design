Synthetic users (home page)

Five simulated visitors, given only the screenshots and the visible text: the phone visitor in a hurry, a small business owner, a low-vision reader, a design critic and the site's own audience (an engineer with legal tech experience who might be a technical co-founder). All five said, unprompted, what the site is, who it is for, and that the next step is "Message Kate on LinkedIn", found on the first screen. Look: 4, 4, 4, 4, 4; synthetic-user score 4.0.

What they agreed on (four or five of five):

  1. The family bar puzzles visitors. "Hub, Vault, AgentiCCells, RiskMandate" reads as someone else's menu and takes three lines on a phone. The bar is the family layer and stays, but it needs a label. Proposal for Dinis and the Factory seed: a short visible lead-in such as "Part of the Sovereign Website family:" and, on phones, a one-line bar with the rest behind a <details> "More". Not for kate-curtis-evans.b to change alone.
  2. The footer link to the five designs reads as a draft. Move it to a quieter line ("This page in five other designs") or keep it only on the designs index until a design is chosen.
  3. The overlapping dates confuse. The facts are right (Kate holds roles at the same time). Group them: "Founder, Audio Genius (Nov 2025 to now)", then "Alongside it, since 2021:" the specialist, eScribers and Ubiqus roles. Nothing added, only arranged.
  4. The text in the boxes looks faint (it passes 4.5:1, but reads pale). Set the "Also open to" and partnership box text in --fg, captions only in --muted.

One persona each: a colour photo (not available: only the cleared black and white desk photo is used); a product screenshot (needs material Kate supplies and Dinis clears); equity or terms (not in the material; never added).

Rating

2 now (clean in all four views). 3 as soon as Kate and Dinis choose one of the five designs. 4 after that design is live with points 2 to 4 above, and design.b's hand checks (keyboard path, 200 per cent zoom, reading order) pass. Rounds so far: 2.
