The synthetic run. Nothing scrolls sideways and nothing is requested from another host. The share cards are 1200 x 630, and every chooser offers five designs, with all thumbnails loading.

What round 1 changed, and it worked

  • Five designs from each company's own world, not one template set. Of the 25 designs, 20 are now distinct ideas:
  • sgit: a terminal, a sealed vault that opens;
  • The Cyber Boardroom: a board pack, a chat with Athena;
  • RiskMandate: the gap drawn, a policy form;
  • AgentiCCells: a cell with a working Stop, a Web Summit badge;
  • Sovereign Website: a hand-over note, a showroom of real sites, a maturity dial. The Newsroom is the same on all five, which follows Dinis's rule of one newsroom design.
  • The real identity, credited and linked: logos, colours, wordmarks and dated screenshots of each real site, with Dinis's permission stated.
  • A one-row phone header, with the headline at or under about 130 px.
  • The company's own first step as the main button, and the version chip inside the footer.

Why the look is still 3, in the personas' words

What they agreed on, in three or more of five on most sites:

  1. The source number on the headline. A display-size "1" after "string", "readiness" and "Show me one." looks like a typo. Keep every marker (design.b's call) except on h1 and h2. Move a headline's source to the standfirst under it, or to the screenshot caption.
  2. No plain line saying what it is. "A vault is a unit of work" (sgit) and "Show me one." (Sovereign Website) do not say what the company does in ten seconds. Under each h1, add one line from the company's own words, for example: - sgit: "Git for encrypted vaults, for humans and AI agents" (sgit.ai's own headline); - Sovereign Website: "Your website. Built by agents, owned by you."
  3. Screenshots shrunk to illegible on phones. On a 390 px phone, show a crop at real size of the part that matters, such as the hero headline and button of the real site (a 780 x 900 crop at 2x), not the whole page at a quarter of its size. Keep the full screenshot behind the link.
  4. After the first screen it is card after card. Each home page needs one full-bleed band below the fold that changes the rhythm. Use a fact the site already has, drawn big: - sgit: a three-part diagram, "your machine (plaintext) → the server (ciphertext only) → a reader with the read key", from its own wording. Also a four-line terminal snippet (pip install sgit-ai, clone, commit, push) from the quickstart. - The Cyber Boardroom: a question-and-answer strip: a director's question going to Athena, and Athena's real opening line coming back ("I'm Athena, your personal AI cyber security advisor ..."). Also, near the top, the real site's own "Important Note" on not entering confidential data, quoted. The director persona's first worry was data handling. - RiskMandate: the four counted figures as a full-bleed dark band (191, 2,262, 2,980, 1), and one real policy excerpt from the sixteen examples, quoted and linked. Give the founders their own small block. - AgentiCCells: the Web Summit to live-site numbers as a band (around 250 agents, 24 minutes, 87 seconds). The "$11.62" sits on a page priced in pounds, and the page does not say what it counts. Say so in the company's own words, or leave it out. - Sovereign Website: keep the showroom; it was the most trusted part on every persona. Under it, one band: "The files, the history, the address: yours", drawn as three keys.
  5. The header chrome confuses visitors ("Choose a design", and a two-line "Sovereign Website"). Both are required. Do as Kate's site does: put our links behind a small "⋮" button labelled "Made with Sovereign Website" for screen readers, and keep "Choose a design". This also stops the wordmark wrapping. Sovereign Website's "Sovereign Agentic Website" runs to three lines on phones: use "Sovereign Website" there.

Smaller, per site

  • sgit: the headings fall back to Times on some systems. Name the stack: "Iowan Old Style", Charter, Georgia, serif. A "What it offers" card sits alone on a row on desktop. The Sources note promises diagrams that are not on the home page (item 4 fixes this).
  • The Cyber Boardroom: the hero button is red and the end button teal; pick one. Dark mode on phones barely changes the body. "Key dates" is a plain list; draw it as the timeline it is.
  • RiskMandate: on desktop, the delta box in the hero diagram reads as separate from the grant; draw it inside the grant's edge, as the gap. The diagram labels are about 9 px; make them at least 12 px.
  • AgentiCCells: the card titles are underlined like links but are not links. The price bars say "500" without "£". "The / conductor" breaks badly on phones.
  • Sovereign Website: "Sources" is set in sans among serif headings. The 4 + 2 grid in "The family" and the orphan card "The first change" leave gaps.

The two patterns across the designs

  • The Board Pack (The Cyber Boardroom), The Policy (RiskMandate) and The Hand-over (Sovereign Website) share one layout: a document with a cover block and an agenda. Each should look like its own document:
  • a board paper on A4 with the "For noting" stamp;
  • a policy form with fields and signature lines;
  • a luggage-tag hand-over with three keys.
  • On each site, the two identity designs share one layout (a hero beside a screenshot). Make the second one lead with something other than the home page. For example: sgit's four vaults as a grid of cards that open, The Cyber Boardroom's Athena page as a chat, RiskMandate's sixteen examples as a library shelf.
