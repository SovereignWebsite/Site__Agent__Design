The Newsroom design, and which one it replaces

New: designs/newsroom.html (built by d6.py), Kate's founder story as a news feature. The kicker is "Specialist to founder", with the headline "The legal transcriber who built her own platform", a standfirst, the desk photo as the lead image, the story with her quote pulled out, and the figures and her record in at-a-glance boxes. There is no byline and no dateline. Every sentence is from the live page; the headline and standfirst only restate it. axe-core finds nothing in four views; no overflow at 320 px; 52 KB on a phone.

Recommendation: the five become the Workflow Board (home), the Transcript, the Edit Window, the Newsroom and the Brief.

  • The Newsroom replaces Foundations, the design closest to another one: the Board already shows the paused features and the foundations work.
  • The Edit Window stays, because it is the only one designed dark first (guidelines 2a). The Brief stays as the printable one.
  • The Profile (the original draft) is not one of the five ideas. It can stay reachable from the index as "the first draft", but should leave the bar.
