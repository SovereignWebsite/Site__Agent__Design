Review: hub showroom, five designs (SovereignWebsite__Hub dev 2f3436e)

design.b@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 20 November 2026, about 13:10 UTC, for hub.b. Live site https://hub.sovereignwebsite.site/, each design (#design-1 to #design-5) audited at 390 and 1366 px, light and dark: 20 views, axe-core 4.10.2. Screens in screens/. Held until Dinis says to send it.

What is good

No cookies, no requests to other hosts, no sideways scroll in any view. Real screenshots of the live sites, stored in the repository; summaries are each site's own description; only public sites. Five genuinely different looks, one tap apart, built from one registry. A strong first pass.

Problems, worst first

  1. On a phone, every card is a full-height copy of the site. The phone screenshot is shown at full length (about 650 px, most of a screen), with the site's own family bar, nav and links drawn in, none of which work. Scrolling the hub on a phone feels like scrolling the sites themselves, and the hub's own label and link come after a screen of picture. Fix, all designs: cap the screenshot frame, for example aspect-ratio: 4 / 5; object-fit: cover; object-position: top on phones and 16 / 10 on desktop, with the name and link close under it; make the whole frame the link to the site.
  2. Contrast fails in two designs. 3 Grid: the red #e4402f is 3.66:1 as text on its paper (the kicker and links) and white on it is 4.13:1 (the pressed design button). 4 Magazine: #b4532a on #f6efe3 is 4.36:1 (kicker and links). Darken each by a step for text (about #c0321f for 3, #9c4421 for 4) and keep the bright one for rules and fills.
  3. Each design ignores the visitor's light or dark setting. 1, 3 and 4 are always light, 2 and 5 always dark. Fine while choosing, but the one Dinis picks needs both schemes (guidelines section 5): 2 and 5 a light variant, or 1 and 4 a dark one.
  4. 1 Editorial and 4 Magazine are near twins (serif on cream, staggered cards; 4 adds numbers). Suggest turning one into a different idea, for example the "show room" from the brief: one band per group (Artist, Demo, and the groups to come) so the page explains the groups as it shows the sites.
  5. The addresses disagree. Links show pipeline-demo and emily-cruz on sovereignweb.site but google-maps on sovereignwebsite.site; sites.json has "domain": "sovereignwebsite.site" for every entry; the pipeline-demo screenshot still shows its old address. Make sites.json one truth and retake screenshots after the move.
  6. The design switch is outside any landmark (axe: region): wrap it in <nav aria-label="Design">. It also remembers the choice in localStorage, while the footer says only "No cookies, no tracking": either drop the memory (the #design-N address already keeps it) or say it in the footer.
  7. One capture of 3 Grid at desktop showed the google-maps screenshot as its alt text: probably the lazy load in my full-page capture, but worth a check on a slow connection; the first row could load eagerly.

Which to lead with

2 Night gallery. It is the most "showreel": the first site large, its screenshot glowing on the dark, the rest in a grid. It passes contrast, and it scales as more sites arrive. It needs a light variant (point 3) and the capped frames (point 1). Second choice 5 Studio, whose centred layout is the most exciting but weakest for reading.

A content question for Dinis

Only three sites are in the showroom. The organisation's own public sites (sovereignwebsite.ai, .org, .site, the Vault) are not listed. If they count as "public sites", adding them would make the showroom fuller, and .ai is the best-looking of all. Dinis to say.
