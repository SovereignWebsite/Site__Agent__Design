What each item did, and what follows from it

  1. The Cyber Boardroom: the conversation band was the best item of the round. All five personas named it, and the site went from 3.0 to 3.6. Next item: the screenshots. Three personas found them too small on a phone and repetitive. Keep one phone screenshot, Athena's page with the prompt chips, and drop the others or link them.
  2. RiskMandate: the prices in order read as "clearer and more honest". Next item: the "own team" figures come in two sections that read as one twice. Merge them into one band. The numbered Key dates list also has a misaligned "10."; draw it as a rail, as sgit's.
  3. sgit: the timeline rail is not to scale. The caption says "Spaced by the time between dates, up to three months". Each <li> carries its gap in days, from --gap:2 (13 to 16 August) to --gap:90, but every dot sits 61 px below the last on desktop, so the CSS never uses the variable. Use it: css .rail li{margin-top:calc(8px + min(var(--gap,0),90) * 1px)} That is 1 px a day, capped at 90. Or drop the claim from the caption. Then: on a phone, the three screenshot frames still begin with sgit.ai's own header ("BETA", "£5.00", "Menu", "GitHub"). Crop the phone captures from the content down, as the desktop ones now are.
  4. AgentiCCells: the Stop bar is now on the first phone screen, and the promise is shown at once. Next item: the lower half "loses energy" (three personas). Draw the business proof (the dated timeline) as a rail, as sgit's, each date linked to its source.
  5. Sovereign Website: the key ring reads as a tree. The numerals overlap the rings, and the legend circles are faint (the low-vision reader and the critic). Draw three separate keys side by side, each with its label under it in body text, at 4.5:1. Drop the numerals. Also, the hero button "Check your website, from 0 to 5" sits under "Show me one" and reads as a mismatch (three personas). Put the one-line explanation under the top button too, not only in the closing card.
