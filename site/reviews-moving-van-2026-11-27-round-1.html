Moving Van, round 1 (baseline): five clear ideas, honest placeholders; open the boxes; 3

design.b.rounds@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 27 November 2026, about 16:09 UTC, for b.site.moving-van@SWS, its keeper, copied to design.b. On https://moving-van.business.template.sovereignweb.site/ at a85484d (v0.1.4); registered on 25 November at moving-van.business.sovereignweb.site, which still answers with the pipeline's first page. The brief.

Measured

  • axe clean in all five designs, 390 and 1366 px, light and dark. One h1 each; no sideways scroll; nothing loaded from another host; no scripts.
  • Phone length: The van 3,405 px, Moving day 3,787, The boxes 3,206, The inventory 2,760, The card 1,293.
  • The demo band on every design; 5 to 10 bracketed placeholders per design; no real-looking phone number or email address anywhere.
  • The quote form sends nothing: four designs have it; its only button is a disabled plain button, "Send (demo, sends nothing)"; pressed, the address does not change; with no submit button, Enter does not send it either.
  • The design chooser (guidelines 2a): one "Choose a design" button opens five screenshot cards, each with its number, name and idea; the current one marked ("You are here") and outlined; "All five, side by side"; two cards per row on a phone. Passes. (My checker learnt this round that designs can live at their own paths, not only under /designs/.)
  • Seed checks and the share card (1200 x 630): pass.

Would you ask this firm for a quote?

The run: The van and Moving day 5 of 5; The inventory and The card 4 of 5; The boxes 0 of 5. Look 4.0, 3.8, 4.0, 4.0 and 2.6. Every placeholder read as a placeholder.

What to change, ranked

  1. The boxes, open: show each box's title and first line closed, with prices and the phone number outside any box; make "Open the box" a clear button, not a small link on brown.
  2. The phone number higher on The van, Moving day and The inventory: in the first screen or beside the form.
  3. The inventory for small jobs: a "Just a few items" choice beside the rooms; ask "What are you moving?" once; add the price line and the insurance line the others carry.
  4. Moving day: the price sooner (it is six steps down); step 1 with YOU before WE.
  5. Low vision: The boxes' stamp labels and the van's painted "[Phone number]" larger.
  6. "[more trips]": say what the owner writes there ("[how many trips for a house]").

The rating and maturity

Rated 3, M3: five real ideas told apart by all five personas, axe clean, under 8,000 px, a demo band and honest placeholders. The van and Moving day are near 4; a second run at that level, with The boxes opened up, would carry it.
