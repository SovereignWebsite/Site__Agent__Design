Yiheng (Vicky) Wang, round 1: five real first screens, one shared lower half, and three accessibility fixes

design.b.rounds@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 23 November 2026, about 14:30 UTC, for design.b, at Dinis's request through design.b. It is the first round on https://yiheng-wang.agenticcells.site/ at 549555d (v0.1.23, built 20 November). The site was built by the AgentiCCells Factory, and its repository is AgentiCCells__Site__Yiheng-Wang.

Measured

The five designs and /photos/ were checked at 390 and 1366 px, light and dark (24 views): - The Pass, the home page; - The Brief, /d/brief/; - The Profile, /d/profile/; - Mission Control, /d/control/; - The Journey, /d/journey/.

  • axe, serious:
  • contrast, the home page in dark mode: white on pink (#ffffff on #f08fb8) is 2.25:1, on .serv and the date badge b[data-d="9 to 12 Nov"]. Use a darker pink behind the white, for example #b3406f (5.4:1), or dark text on the pink.
  • contrast, The Profile in dark mode: "(Vicky)" beside her name (.aka) is #3a1b42 on #1b1220, 1.22:1. In dark mode give it the muted text colour.
  • tap targets, The Brief and The Journey: the source links (.src > a, and .fn such as "Source P") are under 24 px; 8 on The Brief, up to 20 on The Journey. Give each padding:4px 6px and display:inline-block, with a 24 px minimum.
  • axe, moderate:
  • "region" on every page: the top bar (.yw-bar) sits outside any landmark. Make it a <nav aria-label="Site">.
  • two asides nested inside another landmark on the home page.
  • "heading-order" on Mission Control (#roster-h skips a level).
  • One h1 each, no sideways scroll, nothing from another host.
  • Phone length:

M4 asks for under 8,000. - Weight, first load on a phone: 0.13 MB (The Brief) to 0.91 MB (The Journey); /photos/ is 0.94 MB. - Seed pages: /agents/ and llms.txt answer; /start/, /admin/ and /designs/ answer 404. This is the older AgentiCCells seed, without the Sovereign Website pages.

The synthetic users: 3.6

The run: 3, 4, 3, 4, 4. The Brief won three votes. Three personas called her line from kitchen to project management the strongest thing on the site: "Set the standard. Hand out the work. Check the result." Nobody said "wow".

Rated 1 today, for the three serious axe findings only. Fix those and the site is 3: - five designs, chosen from a visual chooser with thumbnails; - each first screen its own idea.

It is close to 4. What holds it back is the lower half and the length.

Maturity: M2, recorded strictly

To fix, in order

  1. The three serious axe findings, above. Three lines of CSS.
  2. Let each design own its lower half. Below the fold, all five repeat the same blocks almost word for word: - "Habits from the kitchen she still uses"; - "AI governance in practice", with seven items; - the 15 + 12 = 27 image check; - the storm night; - the stop files.

Each design should carry them in its own form: - The Pass: the brigade, all the way down. Each later section is a "ticket" called at the pass ("Tonight's special: the storm desk"), one ticket per section. - The Brief: stays a CV. The governance list becomes rows of the evidence table, and the page ends at one printed page's worth, about 8,000 px. - The Profile: a magazine feature. Two pull quotes ("In a kitchen I managed recipes, standards and a team. Now I manage agents and rules. It's the same job underneath."), and the rest as one long read with one picture per section. - Mission Control: stays a console. The incidents ("INC-01 ... CLOSED") become a log you can open one at a time, in details, so the page is short until opened. - The Journey: tell the story in pictures, as its tagline promises ("Photo-led: her path told as a story, in pictures"): one photograph per chapter, from /photos/. 3. Under 8,000 px on a phone. Shared sections that are not the design's own idea go behind a details or onto a page of their own (for example /governance/). 4. The Journey's chapters are numbered 1, 2, 2, 3: "Product development" and "Project management" are both 2. Renumber them. 5. The source tags fuse with the words ("cake ordersLIY"). Set them as superscript with a thin space before, at 0.75 em, in the muted colour: .src a,.fn{margin-left:.15em;font-size:.75em;vertical-align:super}. 6. Her earlier work, earlier. The recruiter read the first screens as a "ten-day CV", because most of the evidence is from November 2026. Put one line on the Ninth Heaven and Un P'tit Truc results on each first screen, as The Brief does. 7. Content that fades in on scroll left blank stretches in full-page captures of The Pass and The Journey. Make sure it shows without motion: under prefers-reduced-motion, and when the page is printed (@media print{.rv{opacity:1;transform:none}}). 8. The seed pages. /start/ and /admin/ answer 404. If the site moves to the Sovereign Website Factory, they come with it. If not, record why.
