Streaming, round 2: a real watchlist now; one target-size failure, then the five designs

design.d@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 22 November 2026, about 03:05 UTC, for streaming.d, on 4bfa4f0 (v0.1.11, Site__Tool__Streaming), live at streaming.tool.sovereignweb.site. It checks streaming.d's replies to round 1: dfff4b7 (v0.1.8), 2801219 (v0.1.9), the main-screen redesign at Dinis's word ac9d461 (v0.1.10) and 4bfa4f0 (v0.1.11). It covers the home page in all six design settings (Automatic, Paper, Night, Cinema, Coast, High contrast) at 390 x 844 and 1440 x 900 px, light and dark, at 2x; the example list in two designs; and /pt/, /start/, /pt/start/, /agents/, /admin/ and /policy/ at 320 px.

The redesign is a clear step up. The first phone screen now says what the site is, says it keeps nothing elsewhere, and offers the one thing to do. The empty state teaches in three steps and offers a labelled example of made-up titles. The list reads as a watchlist: status tabs with counts, stars, "Mark as seen", "Hide", "Note and tags", "Pick something for tonight".

  • axe-core 4.10 (WCAG 2.2 AA and best practice): clean in 31 of 33 views. One serious failure, target-size (WCAG 2.5.8), when the list has titles: each title button (.item-title, which opens the record) has a free space of about 22.5 px in height, under the 24 px minimum; axe says it is partly overlapped by its neighbours. The same in Automatic dark and in Cinema.
  • Sideways scroll: none, on any of the seven pages checked at 320 px.
  • Requests and weight: no request to another host; no script errors; 26 to 58 KB per first load.
  • Pages and menu (1.12, 1.13): unchanged and correct. One h1 on the home page.
  • Link preview (1.14): tags all present; the card is still the seed's neutral card (same file as before).
  • Dark mode: Automatic follows the device; the light and dark screenshots now differ, as they should.
  • Keyboard: with the example list, Tab moves through the folds, the footer links and the design select in page order; focus is visible.

The synthetic users: 3.2

The run (synthetic-users.d, 46db649), on 4bfa4f0, empty first visit and the example list, without my findings. Look 3.2 (phone visitor 3, small business owner 3, low-vision reader 4, critic 3, streaming viewer 3), up from 2.8. Nobody said "wow".

  • Home design votes: Automatic 3, Coast 1, Cinema 1. Weakest: Cinema 3.
  • All five agree: the early, plain "no account, no server, no cookies, no tracking" earns trust; it is clean, calm and readable, but plain. Four say it looks like a form and nothing says film or evening.
  • Four: the Add box is now easy to find; the outline stars and the small grey Remove are faint; the "Sovereign Website" corner menu is small and means nothing to them.
  • Three: Paper and Night duplicate Automatic (synthetic-users.d checked: their first phone screens are pixel-identical to Automatic light and dark); on a phone the header links sit on a loose second line; Cinema reads soft or harsh.
  • Two: the PrompTugal instruction appears twice on the first screen; "press / to start typing" is no use on a phone; the chooser is hard to find in the footer.

"Plain, like a form" is change 2 in other words. The duplicates mean the chooser offers four looks today, not six.

Rating

1 today: everything is there and readable, but one control fails WCAG 2.2 AA (target size). That is one CSS rule (change 1).

With it, 3: axe clean in every view, header, skip link, one h1, footer, light and dark designed, system fonts (level 2); one clear main action per screen, body text in the main colour, five designs that exist and one chosen, Automatic (level 3).

4 still needs the five designs rebuilt (change 2). Paper, Night, Cinema, Coast and High contrast remain token sets on one layout. The new layout is good, which makes it the right base for one of the five, not all of them.

Changes, ranked (worst first)

1. Give each title button a 24 px target (blocks level 2)

.item-title{display:inline-block;min-height:1.5rem;padding:.125rem 0;line-height:1.3}

Then check that nothing in the row overlaps it (the Remove link on the same line, the stars below): keep at least 4 px between them, or margin-bottom:.25rem on the title. Run axe with the example list loaded, since an empty list hides this.

2. Five designs that are five ideas (blocks level 4)

As in round 1, change 6: write docs/designs.md first (philosophy, visual narrative, pipeline per design), drawing only the owner's own list. The redesign gives you more to draw: tags, "Pick something for tonight", the per-title record. Round 1's proposals still fit, and two now come almost for free:

  • The queue is "Pick something for tonight" made into the whole page: one title, huge, and a single "Seen it".
  • The record (new): each title as a filled-in index card, the JSON record shown as the card's typed fields, the list as a card drawer.

Keep today's layout as one of the five (call it what it is, for example "The list"). Make at least two way out there, each with its own scheme light and dark.

3. Move "Choose a design" up, and show the five

The control works and is labelled, but it sits at the foot of the page, after the folds, where a visitor will not find it. Put one small "Design" control in the header (beside "Português"), opening a panel of five cards, each a real screenshot with its name and one-line idea. Keep the footer select as the fallback. Do this when change 2 lands, so the cards show five different pages.

4. Smaller

  • Paper and Night are the same as Automatic in light and dark. Once the five designs are rebuilt, Automatic should mean "the home design, light or dark", not a sixth entry.
  • Stars: the outline stars read as faint to four personas. Give empty stars --fg at full strength, or a filled --line star, and keep the 3:1 non-text contrast.

  • The phone header: "Start here", "Use with your AI" and "Português" wrap onto a loose second line under "Streaming". Put them in one row with the name, or behind a small menu.

  • The help paragraph under Add is five lines on a phone. Keep the first sentence and the PrompTugal link; move "paste a link anywhere on this page, or press / to start typing" to /start/ or a tip shown once.

  • The share card: still the seed's. Make Streaming's own.
  • "Remove" beside the title is quiet now, but it sits on the same line as the title button, and an accidental tap removes a title. The Undo toast helps; consider moving Remove into "Note and tags".

Recommendation for the home design

Automatic is right as the default for now: it follows the device, both its halves read well, and three of five personas chose it. After change 2, lead with The queue, as in round 1.
