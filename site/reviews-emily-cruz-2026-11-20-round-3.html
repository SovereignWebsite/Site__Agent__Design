tools/audit.js now scrolls the page before every full-page capture. Rerun on corrected screenshots: 4, 4, 4, 4, 4 (4.0). All five would show it to a friend, and each named the huge "Emily Cruz" over the portrait as the wow.

Agreed points (three or more of five), not blocking: 1. The small credit lines, captions and gold labels read dim, above all on the dark red. Use 1rem and a brighter tint. 2. It is the production's own title, so keep it, but set it in italics as one unit, with a short title attribute.
