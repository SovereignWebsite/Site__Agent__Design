New, measured

  • Follow-ups scrolls sideways on a phone: its table is 656 px on a 390 px screen, so the whole page moves. (The board's table scrolls inside its own box, which crm.dev.a has listed as not done.)
  • Today hides most drafts. Each card shows one reason, so a company that is overdue and has a draft shows only "Overdue". The board shows "Draft waiting →" for seven companies; Today offers it on two.
  • A company page on a phone opens scrolled down to the log form, so its stage, owner and next step are out of view; the form's button says "Show what will be written", where a reader expects "Save".
  • Repeated words on the cards: "Overdue overdue since 2026-11-21", "Draft waiting a draft is waiting".
  • Requests has its own cut-down menu, with no way back to Today and no Menu button on a phone.

Would you use this every morning? 1 of 5; look 3.4

The run. The look rose from 3.0 to 3.4; the yes count fell from 2 to 1, because Today now answers the question and the personas judged it as their whole morning. The founder said yes ("Today is what I want at 8 in the morning"). The others want Today to be complete and theirs.

What to change, ranked

  1. Today is the whole list: a card shows every reason that applies (overdue, and a draft waiting), with "Review and send" on the card whenever a draft waits.
  2. A count at the top and an owner switch on Today: "6 to do today: 2 overdue, 2 drafts" and "Mine / Everyone".
  3. The person, not only the company, on each card: the name and where we met.
  4. No sideways page scroll on Follow-ups; on a phone, rows as cards (the same work as the board).
  5. Write, not only log: a "Write the follow-up" that opens the email draft, beside "Log the follow-up".
  6. 44 px targets on a phone, and the grey meta line darker and larger.
  7. Small ones: the company page opens at its top; "Save" on the form; say each status once; Requests shows the vault and the full menu.
  8. Export to CSV (the sceptic's "a way back out").

The rating and maturity

Rated 2: clean, with one design (rule 2a holds it at 2 at most until five designs are live). M2. The next step up is the five designs; Today is The Day's seed.
