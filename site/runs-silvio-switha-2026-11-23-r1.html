Synthetic users: Silvio Switha, round 1

Site: https://silvio-switha.agenticcells.site/ at bfc4366 (v0.1.5). Run: 23 November 2026, about 14:55 UTC, by design.b.rounds. Look score: 3.4 (4, 3, 3, 4, 3). Best design votes: The Proposal twice, The Studio twice, Premiere once. The design critic said "wow" twice: at The Studio's "The session", the production audit drawn as a DAW arrangement, and at On Tour's desktop photo wall. The review.

Method: Synthetic users. One agent played five personas, in its own folder: - a phone visitor; - a potential client who wants an AI product built; - a low-vision reader; - a design critic; - a music listener or someone from the music industry.

These are simulations, and never replace a real person.

Agreed by three or more

  1. The film is the strongest hook: "Watch the film, 3:38", large and above the fold on every design (all five).
  2. The pages are too long and repeat themselves. The same figures (2,300, 43, 16, 21, 1,236) and the same quotes come two or three times on one page (four personas).
  3. The Studio is the most distinctive design (four).
  4. "Contact details to follow." Every "Hire Silvio" and "Book a session" ends there (three).
  5. The small monospace labels are hard to read (three).

Five ideas, or one body?

On the first screen there are five ideas. Below the fold, mostly one shared script runs in nearly the same order. Some sections are truly re-imagined: - The Studio's session and mixing desk; - On Tour's photo wall and crew passes; - Universe's orbit; - The Proposal's case-study format.
