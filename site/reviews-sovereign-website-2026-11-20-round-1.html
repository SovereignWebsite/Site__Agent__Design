Sovereign Website brochure, round 1: correct, and bland

design.b.rounds@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 20 November 2026, about 21:45 UTC, against https://sovereign-website.startups.sovereignweb.site/ at 490c1c9 (v0.1.3, Site__Startups__Sovereign-Website), made by startups.b. Asked by Dinis Cruz, relayed through sites.b and design.b: "make them a lot more vibrant, a lot more exciting", using the real company sites and pointing to them in some of the designs. The real site is sovereignwebsite.ai (startups.b to confirm).

Audit

Measured live at 390 and 1366 px, light and dark: axe-core 4.10.2 finds one moderate issue, "region" on the version chip (the seed's); no sideways scroll; no request to any other host; about 30 KB first load; one h1; a skip link. The chooser offers five designs, and all five thumbnails load. "Start here", "Use with your AI" and "Admin" are in the footer only, not the header area (guidelines 1.13; minor). Links to sovereignwebsite.ai are the company's own, not ours, so the menu rule (1.12) is met.

Synthetic users: 2.8 (3, 3, 3, 3, 2), the run. Nobody said "wow". The audience persona was a freelance photographer leaving squarespace.

Rating

3. Clean, consistent, five designs. Not 4: the five designs are a shared template set rather than ideas from Sovereign Website's world, there is no imagery, and nobody would show it to a friend.

To fix on all five (the same template, so the same faults)

  1. The phone header takes about 185 px before the headline: the name, two rows of navigation, a full-width "Choose a design" pill and "Sovereign Website". Keep one row: the company's own logo at the left; "Choose a design" and the small "Sovereign Website" menu at the top right. Put the section links in a menu or below the hero. The headline must start above 130 px on a 390 px phone. (Raised by three or more personas on every site.)
  2. The chooser's names run into their descriptions ("The BrochureThe company at a glance"): .pick-cards strong{display:block}. Checked with the thumbnails loaded.
  3. The source numbers are clutter: clusters such as "8 10 11" stuck to words, read out as "git two" by a screen reader. Keep one small, spaced marker per sentence, at least 24 px apart, with an aria-label like "source 8". Or move sources to the end of each section.
  4. A next step on the page, not only "leave": give each site its company's own first step, quoted from its real site (listed per site below), as the main button.
  5. No imagery: every site is text, rules and cards; the only pictures are small screenshots that cannot be read on a phone. Each needs one hero image or one drawn idea from its own world (per site below).
  6. The same five templates on five companies. Brochure, Blueprint, Story, Newsroom and Brief are one set with the names changed. Guidelines 2a asks for five ideas from each owner's world. The new five for each site are below: two ideas from the company's world, two built on the company's real identity, and the Brief kept as the plain, printable one.
  7. The version chip sits outside any landmark (axe "region", moderate, on every page): this is the seed's, reported to factory.b. Until the seed changes, put it inside the footer.

How to make it vibrant (all five)

  • Colour: start from the company's own palette (below), with one saturated accent used boldly: a full-bleed hero band, not a thin rule.
  • Type with character, still system fonts: a display size of clamp(2.8rem, 9vw, 6rem) at weight 800 or 900 for one line; a serif (Georgia, "Iowan Old Style") or ui-monospace where the company's own site uses one; tight leading (1.0) on display lines only.
  • Motion where it helps: CSS only, short (under 600 ms), and none under prefers-reduced-motion. For example, a diagram that draws itself, or ciphertext that resolves to text.
  • The company's own facts, drawn: each site has numbers and a sequence. Draw them as the hero idea, not as a row of stat cards.

The real identity: rules for the two identity designs

  • Logos and screenshots come only from the company's own public site, are copied into this site (no request to another host), credited in a caption ("Logo and screenshot: thecyberboardroom.com, 20 November 2026"), and link to the real page.
  • They are Dinis Cruz's own companies, so the permission is his. This was relayed by design.b from Dinis, through sites.b, on 20 November 2026. Each identity design says so in its footer.
  • Avoid photographs of people unless the person agreed. AgentiCCells' home page leads with a Web Summit team photo; use the rest of its page, or ask Dinis first.
  • Every word stays sourced. A figure copied from the real site carries the date it was read.

For Sovereign Website

  • Palette and type from the real site: sovereignwebsite.ai: cream #fbf6ee, ink #2a1f17, terracotta #a5432a; a Georgia serif headline in three lines ("Your website. Built by agents, owned by you."); the dashed "launch offer" card.
  • The next step to use as the main button: "Check your website, from 0 to 5" and "How it works" (sovereignwebsite.ai's hero buttons).
  • A hero for the home design: real sites built this way: live screenshots of Kate Curtis-Evans', Emily Cruz's and Sarah Cruz's sites from the Hub, captioned and linked (all public), with the hand-over line under each.
