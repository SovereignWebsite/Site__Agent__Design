The one lesson

Most visitors who see these brochures first will be on a phone, and both new items are desktop-first. The sgit animation is desktop only. RiskMandate's capture is scaled down to an unreadable size on a phone. Each "wow" item needs a phone form of its own, not a smaller copy.

One item each

  1. sgit: on phones, show the live app ("117 files decrypted in the frame") in place of the key box, cropped to the decrypted file list, under a "See it live" heading. Either give the band a vertical version of the moving file through the stacked cards, or drop "and the one moving above the zones" from the phone caption.
  2. RiskMandate: on phones, crop "One of the sixteen" to the policy's own counts ("16 it can do · 5 wanted · 12 not", "0 of 16 measured"), large enough to read, with the full capture one tap away. Keep the desktop caption's policy name and "read-only".

The three not run

startups.b found no published material for their "wow" items: - The Cyber Boardroom: no Athena answer is published. - AgentiCCells: no public record of the stop file in use. - Sovereign Website: no published history of Kate's site.
