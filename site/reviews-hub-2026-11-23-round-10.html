Hub, round 10: The Line at 4 of 5 for the first time; the features search misses its own example

design.b.rounds@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 23 November 2026, about 13:30 UTC, for design.b and hub.b. On https://hub.sovereignweb.site/ at 4a60c45 (v0.1.42). Dinis has decided the "I want one" route and the price, but neither is live in this version. "I want one" still ends at "There is no request form yet".

Measured

  • axe: none, on all five designs, at 390 and 1366 px, light and dark. One h1 each, no sideways scroll.
  • Weight on a phone: 0.17 MB (The Line) to 0.33 MB (The Street).
  • Light mode: The Gallery and Contact Sheet now follow it; light and dark captures now differ.

The Line is home. It reached four of five for the first time. What moved it was the own-versus-rent table: the café owner said yes because of it, and no to The Street because The Street lacks it. One more run at four of five rates The Line 4 under the two-run rule. The only no left on The Line is the sceptic's, and it is about facts, not design: "'Nothing is sold yet', 'There is no request form yet', and only '1 handed over to its owner so far'. I'd wait till it's real."

Rated 3, until the second run.

To do, in order

  1. Put the route and the price live. Dinis has decided both. They are the sceptic's no on every design, and 8 of 25 answers mention the price. The Line's "Ask us for the price" with no way to ask is the gap.
  2. The features search: match words, not the whole phrase. "contact form" is the box's own example and finds nothing, though "A message box with replies" is listed with "contact" and "form". Split the query into words and show a feature when every word is in its list: js var ws=v.split(/\s+/).filter(Boolean); var s=!ws.length||ws.every(function(w){return f.dataset.words.indexOf(w)>-1;}); With that, "contact form" finds the message box, and "book an appointment" still honestly finds nothing.
  3. Say "not yet" in plain words for the common asks. Nine of my sixteen searches found nothing: - booking; - book an appointment; - contact form; - blog; - reviews; - opening hours; - gallery; - newsletter; - video.

Some of these exist on the family's sites in fact: a gallery (The Gallery itself, Sarah's work), video (Silvio's film), opening hours (any page can carry them). Add an entry for each that is true, with an example site. For the ones that are not built (booking, reviews, a newsletter), add an honest "Not yet" entry, so the visitor reads an answer rather than "Nothing here matches". The electrician and the café owner both asked for something basic and got "nothing", which "feels worse than her Squarespace". 4. "price" leads only to "A shop, NOT STARTED: not built, and no price set". Add "price" and "cost" to the words of the entry that will carry the price, once it is live. 5. The Street lacks The Line's table. If The Street stays, give it the table too. The café owner's no was only that. 6. "Nobody like me" (4 noes): no tradesperson and no real business. This needs a real site, not a design change; until then, do not lead any design with the made-up businesses.
